❓ Help
What is Mosque Builder?

An interactive game where you build a mosque step by step. Each element teaches you about mosque architecture and its Islamic significance.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once loaded, everything runs in your browser with no internet needed.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Works on any device with a modern browser — phone, tablet, laptop, desktop. Tap on the canvas to place elements.

How do I change the theme?

Click any theme button (🖥 🌌 🔥 etc.) in the controls bar.

How do I change the language?

Click EN / FR / ع. Arabic enables right-to-left layout automatically.

What are the difficulty levels?

Easy has 6 main elements, Medium has 9, and Hard has all 12 architectural elements.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser. No data is sent anywhere.

🕌 Purpose

Learn mosque architecture by building one element at a time. Each piece teaches its Islamic significance.

🎨 Themes

8 built-in themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Arctic, Sand.

🌐 Languages

Trilingual: English, Francais, العربية. Arabic auto-enables RTL layout.

🔊 Sound

Web Audio API — no files needed. Correct = rising tone, wrong = buzz, complete = melody. Toggle with the sound button.

🏗 Elements

12 mosque elements: Foundation, Walls, Dome, Minaret, Mihrab, Minbar, Courtyard, Fountain, Entrance Door, Windows, Crescent, Carpet Area.

🔒 Privacy

Local-first. No tracking, no analytics, no external calls. All data stays in your browser.
