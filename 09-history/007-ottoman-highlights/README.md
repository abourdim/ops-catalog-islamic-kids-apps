# Ottoman Empire Quiz

Interactive Ottoman Empire highlights quiz for Muslim kids.

## Overview

- **Title:** Ottoman Empire
- **Subtitle:** Explore 600 years of Islamic rule
- **Type:** Multiple-choice quiz (4 options per question)
- **Questions:** 31 total across 3 difficulty levels
- **HS_KEY:** `wdiy-hs-ottoman`

## Difficulty Levels

| Level  | Questions | Coverage |
|--------|-----------|----------|
| Easy   | 10        | Major events: founding, Constantinople, Suleiman, Hagia Sophia, Topkapi, Mecca & Medina, end of empire |
| Medium | 15        | Adds Janissaries, Selimiye Mosque, calligraphy, miniature art, Hejaz Railway, Kanun, Tulip Period, Lepanto, Tanzimat, Blue Mosque |
| Hard   | 25+       | Full bank including devshirme, Siege of Vienna, millet system, Ottoman medicine, Ibrahim Pasha, Piri Reis, timar system, Treaty of Karlowitz, Evliya Celebi, Hurrem Sultan, Enderun School |

## Features

- Single HTML file, zero dependencies, fully offline
- 8 CSS themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Arctic, Sand
- Trilingual UI: English, French, Arabic (RTL)
- Splash screen with Bismillah
- Sidebar help panel (FAQ, How-To, Wiki tabs)
- Sound effects via Web Audio API (toggle on/off)
- High score persistence via localStorage
- Keyboard shortcuts: 1-4 for answers, Enter to start, Escape to close help
- Timer and progress bar
- After correct answers: displays date/era, key figure, and historical significance
- Preferences (theme, language, sound) persist across sessions

## Topics Covered

Osman I (1299), Conquest of Constantinople (1453), Istanbul, Hagia Sophia, Suleiman the Magnificent, Mimar Sinan (Suleymaniye & Selimiye mosques), Ottoman calligraphy, miniature art, Topkapi Palace, protection of Mecca and Medina, Hejaz Railway, Kanun legal system, Janissaries, Tulip Period, Ottoman medicine, Ottoman navy (Battle of Lepanto), Siege of Vienna, Tanzimat reforms, end of Empire (1922), devshirme system, millet system, Piri Reis, Evliya Celebi, Hurrem Sultan, Enderun School, Treaty of Karlowitz, timar system, Blue Mosque, Ibrahim Pasha, legacy across Balkans/Middle East/North Africa.
