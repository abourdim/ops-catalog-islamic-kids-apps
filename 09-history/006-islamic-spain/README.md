# Al-Andalus — The Golden Age of Islamic Spain

Interactive quiz game for Muslim kids about the history of Al-Andalus (Islamic Spain, 711-1492 CE).

## Features

- **25+ questions** spanning the full history of Muslim Spain
- **3 difficulty levels**: Easy (10 major events), Medium (15), Hard (25+)
- **Educational info boxes** after correct answers showing date, key figure, and lasting impact
- **8 CSS themes**: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Arctic, Sand
- **Trilingual UI**: English, French, Arabic (with RTL support)
- **Sound effects** via Web Audio API (no external files)
- **High score** persistence via localStorage
- **Keyboard shortcuts**: 1-4 for answers, Enter to start, Escape to close help
- **Sidebar help** with FAQ, How-To, and Wiki tabs
- **Single HTML file**, zero dependencies, fully offline

## Topics Covered

- Tariq ibn Ziyad's crossing (711 CE) and the naming of Gibraltar
- Abd al-Rahman I (the Emigrant) and the founding of the Emirate of Cordoba
- Abd al-Rahman III and the Caliphate of Cordoba
- The Great Mosque of Cordoba (Mezquita)
- Libraries of Cordoba (400,000+ books)
- Madinat al-Zahra palace city
- The Alhambra Palace in Granada
- The Translation Movement (Arabic to Latin)
- Ibn Rushd (Averroes) — philosophy and scholarship
- Maimonides studying under Muslim scholars
- Ziryab — fashion, music, and food culture
- Irrigation innovations and paper mills
- Abbas ibn Firnas and early flight
- Convivencia — coexistence of faiths
- Almoravid and Almohad dynasties
- Battle of Tours (732 CE) and Battle of Las Navas de Tolosa (1212 CE)
- Fall of Toledo (1085), Cordoba (1236), and Granada (1492)
- Boabdil's departure and the Last Sigh of the Moor
- Arabic loan words in Spanish language
- Islamic geometric art and architecture
