❓ Help
What is Al-Andalus?

An interactive quiz about Islamic Spain (Al-Andalus) covering its 800-year history from 711 to 1492 CE. Learn about key figures, events, architecture, science, and cultural achievements.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once loaded, everything runs in your browser with no internet needed.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Works on any device with a modern browser — phone, tablet, laptop, desktop.

How do I change the theme?

Click any theme button (🖥 🌌 🔥 etc.) in the controls bar.

How do I change the language?

Click EN / FR / ع. Arabic enables right-to-left layout automatically.

What do the difficulty levels mean?

Easy = 10 major events. Medium = 15 questions covering more detail. Hard = 25+ questions spanning the full history of Al-Andalus.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser. No data is sent anywhere.

🎯 Purpose

Learn the history of Al-Andalus (Islamic Spain) through an interactive quiz. Covers 800 years of Muslim civilization in the Iberian Peninsula.

📜 Topics

Tariq ibn Ziyad, Gibraltar, Cordoba, the Great Mosque, Abd al-Rahman I & III, Alhambra Palace, libraries, translation movement, Averroes, Ziryab, irrigation, convivencia, and the fall of Granada.

🎨 Themes

8 built-in themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Arctic, Sand.

🌐 Languages

Trilingual: English, Francais, العربية. Arabic auto-enables RTL layout.

🔊 Sound

Web Audio API — no files needed. Correct = rising tone, wrong = buzz, win = melody. Toggle with 🔊 button.

🔒 Privacy

Local-first. No tracking, no analytics, no external calls. All data stays in your browser.
