# Ahl as-Suffah — Interactive Educational App

The People of the Bench — the homeless scholars who lived in the Prophet's mosque, dedicating their lives entirely to learning and worship.

## Features

- Single HTML file, zero dependencies, fully offline
- 8 CSS themes, trilingual EN/FR/AR with RTL, Bismillah splash, help sidebar
- Web Audio API sounds, localStorage progress, keyboard navigation

## Chapters

| # | Chapter | Key Fact |
|---|---------|----------|
| 1 | Who Were They? | The People of the Suffah numbered between 70 and 400 at different times, all living in the mosque. |
| 2 | The Prophet's Care | The Prophet personally cared for the Suffah residents, often sending them food before eating himself. |
| 3 | A University Before Universities | Ahl as-Suffah was essentially the first Islamic university, producing scholars who taught across the Muslim world. |
| 4 | Legacy | The Suffah residents proved that material poverty is no barrier to becoming the greatest scholars. |
