# Fatimah az-Zahra — Interactive Educational App

The life of Fatimah az-Zahra — the beloved daughter of the Prophet, leader of the women of Paradise, and mother of Hasan and Husayn.

## Concept

Fatimah az-Zahra (فاطمة الزهراء) — explore 6 interactive chapters with trilingual content and key facts.

## Features

- Single HTML file, zero dependencies, fully offline
- 8 CSS themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Arctic, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Sand
- Trilingual: English, French, Arabic (with RTL support)
- Splash screen with Bismillah
- Sidebar help panel with FAQ, How-To, and Wiki tabs
- Web Audio API sound effects
- Keyboard navigation (Arrow keys)
- Reading progress via localStorage

## Chapters

| # | Chapter | Key Fact |
|---|---------|----------|
| 1 | Birth and Childhood | Aisha said: 'I never saw anyone more similar to the Prophet in speech, manner, and bearing than Fatimah.' |
| 2 | Defending Her Father | Fatimah was nicknamed 'Umm Abiha' (mother of her father) for her protective devotion to the Prophet. |
| 3 | Marriage to Ali | Ali's entire dowry to Fatimah was his iron shield — they began married life with almost nothing. |
| 4 | Mother of Hasan and Husayn | The Prophet said Hasan and Husayn were the leaders of the youth of Paradise. |
| 5 | Lady of Paradise | The Prophet declared Fatimah the leader of the women of Paradise. |
| 6 | Legacy | All living descendants of the Prophet trace their lineage through Fatimah. |
