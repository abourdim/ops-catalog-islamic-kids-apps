# Abu Dharr al-Ghifari — Interactive Educational App

The biography of Abu Dharr al-Ghifari — the fearless truth-speaker, ascetic companion who championed justice for the poor and spoke truth to power.

## Concept

Abu Dharr al-Ghifari (أبو ذر الغفاري) — explore 6 interactive chapters with trilingual content and key facts.

## Features

- Single HTML file, zero dependencies, fully offline
- 8 CSS themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Arctic, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Sand
- Trilingual: English, French, Arabic (with RTL support)
- Splash screen with Bismillah
- Sidebar help panel with FAQ, How-To, and Wiki tabs
- Web Audio API sound effects
- Keyboard navigation (Arrow keys)
- Reading progress via localStorage

## Chapters

| # | Chapter | Key Fact |
|---|---------|----------|
| 1 | The Seeker | Abu Dharr rejected idolatry independently, even before hearing about Islam, showing his innate search for truth. |
| 2 | Bold Declaration | Abu Dharr publicly declared his Islam at the Ka'bah knowing he would be beaten — showing extraordinary courage. |
| 3 | Converting His Tribe | Abu Dharr converted his entire tribe of former highway robbers to Islam. |
| 4 | Champion of the Poor | The Prophet said: 'There is no one under the sky or on earth more truthful than Abu Dharr.' |
| 5 | The Ascetic Life | Abu Dharr lived in extreme simplicity, refusing to keep any wealth beyond his immediate daily needs. |
| 6 | Legacy | The Prophet compared Abu Dharr's asceticism to that of Prophet Jesus (peace be upon him). |
