# Companion Professions — Interactive Educational App

What the companions did for a living — they were merchants, farmers, blacksmiths, tailors, and more. Islam honored all honest work.

## Features

- Single HTML file, zero dependencies, fully offline
- 8 CSS themes, trilingual EN/FR/AR with RTL, Bismillah splash, help sidebar
- Web Audio API sounds, localStorage progress, keyboard navigation

## Chapters

| # | Chapter | Key Fact |
|---|---------|----------|
| 1 | Merchants & Traders | The Prophet said the honest merchant will be with the prophets on the Day of Judgment. |
| 2 | Farmers & Shepherds | The Prophet said every prophet was a shepherd, honoring pastoral and agricultural work. |
| 3 | Craftsmen & Artisans | The Prophet said: 'No food is better than that which a person earns by the work of their own hands.' |
| 4 | Scholars & Teachers | After the Prophet, companions like Aisha, Abu Hurairah, and Ibn Abbas became full-time scholars and teachers. |
