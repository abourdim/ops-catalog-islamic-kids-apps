# Zubair ibn al-Awwam — Interactive Educational App

The biography of Zubair ibn al-Awwam — the Disciple of the Prophet, first to draw a sword for Islam, and one of the ten promised Paradise.

## Features

- Single HTML file, zero dependencies, fully offline
- 8 CSS themes, trilingual EN/FR/AR with RTL, Bismillah splash, help sidebar
- Web Audio API sounds, localStorage progress, keyboard navigation

## Chapters

| # | Chapter | Key Fact |
|---|---------|----------|
| 1 | Early Life | Zubair accepted Islam at just 15 years old and endured torture without wavering. |
| 2 | First Sword for Islam | Zubair was the first Muslim to draw a sword in defense of Islam and earned the title 'Hawari' (Disciple). |
| 3 | Warrior in Every Battle | Zubair's body was covered in so many battle scars that his chest looked like eyes on a hide. |
| 4 | Legacy | Zubair's son paid off 2.2 million dirhams in debts his father had guaranteed for others. |
