# Musab ibn Umayr — Interactive Educational App

The life of Musab ibn Umayr — from the most pampered youth of Mecca to the first ambassador of Islam, whose sacrifice and preaching prepared Medina for the Prophet's migration.

## Concept

Musab ibn Umayr (مصعب بن عمير) — explore 6 interactive chapters with trilingual content and key facts.

## Features

- Single HTML file, zero dependencies, fully offline
- 8 CSS themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Arctic, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Sand
- Trilingual: English, French, Arabic (with RTL support)
- Splash screen with Bismillah
- Sidebar help panel with FAQ, How-To, and Wiki tabs
- Web Audio API sound effects
- Keyboard navigation (Arrow keys)
- Reading progress via localStorage

## Chapters

| # | Chapter | Key Fact |
|---|---------|----------|
| 1 | The Most Handsome Youth | Musab was so well-dressed that people in Mecca would recognize him by the scent of his perfume from a distance. |
| 2 | Secret Conversion | Musab went from being the best-dressed youth in Mecca to wearing rough patches after accepting Islam. |
| 3 | First Ambassador to Medina | At barely twenty years old, Musab was chosen as Islam's first ambassador — preparing Medina for the Prophet's migration. |
| 4 | Master Teacher | Musab converted the two most influential leaders of Medina, causing entire tribes to embrace Islam. |
| 5 | Martyrdom at Uhud | Musab held the banner at Uhud even after both arms were severed, dying as the ultimate symbol of sacrifice. |
| 6 | The Shroud That Was Too Short | Musab's shroud was so short it could not cover both his head and feet — a stark contrast to his former luxury. |
