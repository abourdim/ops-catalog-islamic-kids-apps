# Great Women Companions — Interactive Educational App

Profiles of great women companions — Sumayyah the first martyr, Nusaybah the warrior, Umm Salamah the advisor, and Asma bint Abu Bakr.

## Features

- Single HTML file, zero dependencies, fully offline
- 8 CSS themes, trilingual EN/FR/AR with RTL, Bismillah splash, help sidebar
- Web Audio API sounds, localStorage progress, keyboard navigation

## Chapters

| # | Chapter | Key Fact |
|---|---------|----------|
| 1 | Sumayyah bint Khayyat | Sumayyah was the very first martyr in Islam — an elderly enslaved woman who chose death over apostasy. |
| 2 | Nusaybah bint Ka'b | Nusaybah fought with a sword at Uhud and the Prophet saw her defending him everywhere he turned. |
| 3 | Umm Salamah | Umm Salamah's advice at Hudaybiyyah resolved a crisis when the companions hesitated to follow orders. |
| 4 | Asma bint Abu Bakr | Asma tore her belt to carry food during the Hijrah, earning the title 'She of the Two Belts.' |
