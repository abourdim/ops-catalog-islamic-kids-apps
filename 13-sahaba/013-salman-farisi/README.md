# Salman al-Farisi — Interactive Educational App

The incredible journey of Salman al-Farisi — from Zoroastrian nobleman in Persia through Christianity to Islam. A seeker of truth across continents.

## Concept

Salman al-Farisi (سلمان الفارسي) — explore 7 interactive chapters with trilingual content and key facts.

## Features

- Single HTML file, zero dependencies, fully offline
- 8 CSS themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Arctic, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Sand
- Trilingual: English, French, Arabic (with RTL support)
- Splash screen with Bismillah
- Sidebar help panel with FAQ, How-To, and Wiki tabs
- Web Audio API sound effects
- Keyboard navigation (Arrow keys)
- Reading progress via localStorage

## Chapters

| # | Chapter | Key Fact |
|---|---------|----------|
| 1 | A Persian Nobleman | Salman's journey from Persia to Islam spanned decades and thousands of miles across multiple countries. |
| 2 | Journey Through Christianity | Each dying Christian monk directed Salman to the next, creating a chain that eventually led him to seek the final Prophet. |
| 3 | Enslaved on the Way | Salman was enslaved and brought to the very city where the Prophet would later migrate — a divine arrangement. |
| 4 | Meeting the Prophet | Salman verified three signs of prophethood that a Christian monk had described to him decades earlier. |
| 5 | Freedom Through Planting | The Prophet personally planted all 300 date palm saplings and every single one survived — a miracle. |
| 6 | The Trench Strategy | The trench strategy from Persian warfare was completely unknown to the Arabs and saved Medina from invasion. |
| 7 | Legacy | Despite being governor of a former imperial capital, Salman wove baskets and earned his own bread by hand. |
