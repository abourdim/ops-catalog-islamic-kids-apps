# Bilal ibn Rabah — Interactive Educational App

The life of Bilal ibn Rabah — from enslaved and tortured to the first muezzin (caller to prayer) of Islam. A story of faith, endurance, and liberation.

## Concept

Bilal ibn Rabah (بلال بن رباح) — explore 7 interactive chapters with trilingual content and key facts.

## Features

- Single HTML file, zero dependencies, fully offline
- 8 CSS themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Arctic, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Sand
- Trilingual: English, French, Arabic (with RTL support)
- Splash screen with Bismillah
- Sidebar help panel with FAQ, How-To, and Wiki tabs
- Web Audio API sound effects
- Keyboard navigation (Arrow keys)
- Reading progress via localStorage

## Chapters

| # | Chapter | Key Fact |
|---|---------|----------|
| 1 | Enslaved in Mecca | Bilal accepted Islam at a time when it was most dangerous, being one of the most vulnerable people in Meccann society. |
| 2 | Torture and 'Ahad, Ahad' | Under torture with a boulder on his chest in the scorching desert, Bilal would only say 'Ahad, Ahad' — affirming the Oneness of Allah. |
| 3 | Liberation by Abu Bakr | Abu Bakr paid an enormous price to free Bilal, and when criticized for overpaying, he said: 'I would have paid even more.' |
| 4 | The First Muezzin | Bilal was the first human being to call the adhan — a practice now performed by millions daily across the world. |
| 5 | Companion in Every Battle | On the day of Mecca's conquest, Bilal — a former slave — gave the adhan from atop the Ka'bah itself. |
| 6 | After the Prophet | When Bilal gave the adhan one final time in Medina years later, the entire city wept from the memory. |
| 7 | Legacy of Equality | The Prophet told Bilal he heard his footsteps ahead of him in Paradise — the highest honor imaginable. |
