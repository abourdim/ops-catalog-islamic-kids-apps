# Amr ibn al-As — Interactive Educational App

The biography of Amr ibn al-As — the brilliant diplomat, military strategist, and conqueror of Egypt who brought Islam to North Africa.

## Concept

Amr ibn al-As (عمرو بن العاص) — explore 6 interactive chapters with trilingual content and key facts.

## Features

- Single HTML file, zero dependencies, fully offline
- 8 CSS themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Arctic, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Sand
- Trilingual: English, French, Arabic (with RTL support)
- Splash screen with Bismillah
- Sidebar help panel with FAQ, How-To, and Wiki tabs
- Web Audio API sound effects
- Keyboard navigation (Arrow keys)
- Reading progress via localStorage

## Chapters

| # | Chapter | Key Fact |
|---|---------|----------|
| 1 | The Shrewd Diplomat | The Quraysh considered Amr their best diplomat and negotiator, sending him on the most critical missions. |
| 2 | Conversion to Islam | Amr converted alongside Khalid ibn al-Walid — two of the Quraysh's best warriors joining Islam together. |
| 3 | Commander at Dhat al-Salasil | The Prophet appointed Amr as commander over more senior companions, showing Islam's merit-based leadership. |
| 4 | The Conquest of Egypt | Amr conquered Egypt with just 4,000 initial soldiers against the Byzantine Empire. |
| 5 | Founding Fustat | The Mosque of Amr ibn al-As in Cairo is the first mosque ever built in Africa and still stands today. |
| 6 | Legacy | The Prophet described Amr as one of the righteous people of Quraysh. |
