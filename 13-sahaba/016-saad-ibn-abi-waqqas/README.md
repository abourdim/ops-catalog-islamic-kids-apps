# Saad ibn Abi Waqqas — Interactive Educational App

The biography of Saad ibn Abi Waqqas — the first to shoot an arrow for Islam, hero of Qadisiyyah, and one of the ten promised Paradise.

## Concept

Saad ibn Abi Waqqas (سعد بن أبي وقاص) — explore 5 interactive chapters with trilingual content and key facts.

## Features

- Single HTML file, zero dependencies, fully offline
- 8 CSS themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Arctic, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Sand
- Trilingual: English, French, Arabic (with RTL support)
- Splash screen with Bismillah
- Sidebar help panel with FAQ, How-To, and Wiki tabs
- Web Audio API sound effects
- Keyboard navigation (Arrow keys)
- Reading progress via localStorage

## Chapters

| # | Chapter | Key Fact |
|---|---------|----------|
| 1 | Early Conversion | Quran 31:15 was revealed specifically about Saad's situation with his mother's hunger strike. |
| 2 | First Arrow for Islam | The Prophet's supplication for Saad meant his arrows never missed and his prayers were always answered. |
| 3 | Hero of Qadisiyyah | Saad directed the Battle of Qadisiyyah from a stretcher while severely ill, defeating the Persian Empire. |
| 4 | Builder of Kufa | Saad founded the city of Kufa, which became one of the most important centers of Islamic scholarship. |
| 5 | Legacy | A mosque in Guangzhou, China is attributed to Saad ibn Abi Waqqas, suggesting Islam reached China very early. |
