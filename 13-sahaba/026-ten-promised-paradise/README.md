# The Ten Promised Paradise — Interactive Educational App

The Ashara Mubashara — interactive cards exploring all ten companions whom the Prophet explicitly promised Paradise.

## Features

- Single HTML file, zero dependencies, fully offline
- 8 CSS themes, trilingual EN/FR/AR with RTL, Bismillah splash, help sidebar
- Web Audio API sounds, localStorage progress, keyboard navigation

## Chapters

| # | Chapter | Key Fact |
|---|---------|----------|
| 1 | Abu Bakr as-Siddiq | Abu Bakr was the first adult man to accept Islam and the closest companion of the Prophet. |
| 2 | Umar ibn al-Khattab | Umar was called al-Faruq and his caliphate saw the largest expansion of the Islamic state. |
| 3 | Uthman ibn Affan | Uthman married two daughters of the Prophet and standardized the Quran for all time. |
| 4 | Ali ibn Abi Talib | Ali was called the Gate of Knowledge and was the first youth to accept Islam. |
| 5 | Talha ibn Ubaydillah | Talha took 70+ wounds at Uhud shielding the Prophet and was called 'the living martyr.' |
| 6 | Zubair ibn al-Awwam | Zubair was the first to draw a sword for Islam and was titled the Prophet's Disciple. |
| 7 | Abdur Rahman ibn Awf | Abdur Rahman donated entire caravans and built his empire from nothing in Medina. |
| 8 | Abu Ubayda ibn al-Jarrah | Abu Ubayda was called the Trustee of the Ummah and lived with extreme humility despite his rank. |
| 9 | Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas | Sa'd was the first to shoot an arrow for Islam and his prayers were always answered. |
| 10 | Sa'eed ibn Zayd | In Sa'eed's home, Umar heard the Quran that transformed him from enemy to champion of Islam. |
