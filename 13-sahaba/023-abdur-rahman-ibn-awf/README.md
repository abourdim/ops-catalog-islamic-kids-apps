# Abdur Rahman ibn Awf — Interactive Educational App

The biography of Abdur Rahman ibn Awf — the richest companion who donated entire caravans, master merchant, and one of the ten promised Paradise.

## Features

- Single HTML file, zero dependencies, fully offline
- 8 CSS themes, trilingual EN/FR/AR with RTL, Bismillah splash, help sidebar
- Web Audio API sounds, localStorage progress, keyboard navigation

## Chapters

| # | Chapter | Key Fact |
|---|---------|----------|
| 1 | Early Life | When offered half of everything by his Medinan brother, Abdur Rahman only asked: 'Show me where the market is.' |
| 2 | Master Merchant | Starting from nothing in Medina, Abdur Rahman built one of Arabia's largest trading empires. |
| 3 | Greatest Charity | Abdur Rahman once donated an entire caravan of 700 loaded camels in a single act of charity. |
| 4 | Legacy | Abdur Rahman's will included 50,000 dinars for charity, even in death his generosity continued. |
