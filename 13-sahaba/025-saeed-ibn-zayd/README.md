# Sa'eed ibn Zayd — Interactive Educational App

The biography of Sa'eed ibn Zayd — one of the ten promised Paradise, whose father Zayd ibn Amr was a pre-Islamic monotheist.

## Features

- Single HTML file, zero dependencies, fully offline
- 8 CSS themes, trilingual EN/FR/AR with RTL, Bismillah splash, help sidebar
- Web Audio API sounds, localStorage progress, keyboard navigation

## Chapters

| # | Chapter | Key Fact |
|---|---------|----------|
| 1 | Son of a Seeker | Sa'eed's father Zayd ibn Amr will be resurrected as a nation by himself on the Day of Judgment. |
| 2 | Early Muslim | It was in Sa'eed's house that Umar heard Quran recitation that led to his dramatic conversion. |
| 3 | Warrior and Conqueror | Sa'eed was a commander at the conquest of Damascus and the decisive Battle of Yarmouk. |
| 4 | Legacy | Sa'eed was one of the ten promised Paradise and was known for his answered prayers. |
