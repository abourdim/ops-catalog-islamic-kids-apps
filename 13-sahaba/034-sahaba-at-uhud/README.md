# Companions at Uhud — Interactive Educational App

The companions who stood firm at the Battle of Uhud when others fled — stories of extraordinary courage and sacrifice.

## Features

- Single HTML file, zero dependencies, fully offline
- 8 CSS themes, trilingual EN/FR/AR with RTL, Bismillah splash, help sidebar
- Web Audio API sounds, localStorage progress, keyboard navigation

## Chapters

| # | Chapter | Key Fact |
|---|---------|----------|
| 1 | The Battle Turns | When most fled at Uhud, a small group stood firm around the Prophet, defending him with their lives. |
| 2 | Those Who Stood Firm | About 30 companions stood firm at Uhud, with Talha taking 70+ wounds to protect the Prophet. |
| 3 | The Martyrs of Uhud | Hanzalah was martyred just after his wedding and was washed by the angels. |
| 4 | Lessons of Uhud | Uhud taught that victory requires discipline, and the Quran addressed it extensively as a lesson in patience. |
