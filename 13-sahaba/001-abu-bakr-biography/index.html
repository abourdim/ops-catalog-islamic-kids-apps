Abu Bakr as-Siddiq

Abdullah ibn Abi Quhafah, known as Abu Bakr as-Siddiq (573-634 CE). The first adult man to accept Islam, the closest companion and father-in-law of the Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him), and the first Caliph after the Prophet's passing.

Title: as-Siddiq

Meaning "the truthful" — given because he immediately believed the Prophet's account of the Night Journey (Isra and Mi'raj) without any hesitation.
