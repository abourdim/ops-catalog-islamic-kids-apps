# Abu Bakr as-Siddiq — Interactive Biography

Interactive biography of Abu Bakr as-Siddiq (may Allah be pleased with him) — the first man to accept Islam, the closest companion of the Prophet (peace be upon him), and the first Caliph.

## Concept

Explore 8 chapters covering Abu Bakr's entire life: his early character, acceptance of Islam, sacrifices, the Hijrah, his title as-Siddiq, life in Medina, his caliphate, and his lasting legacy. Each chapter includes key historical facts and authentic narrations.

## Features

- Single HTML file, zero dependencies, fully offline
- 8 CSS themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Arctic, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Sand
- Trilingual: English, French, Arabic (with RTL support)
- Splash screen with Bismillah
- Sidebar help panel with FAQ, How-To, and Wiki tabs
- Web Audio API sound effects (no audio files)
- Keyboard navigation (Arrow keys for chapters)
- Reading progress persistence via localStorage
- Chapter progress tracker with visual dots

## Chapters

| # | Chapter | Content |
|---|---------|---------|
| 1 | Early Life & Character | Birth, pre-Islamic character, merchant career |
| 2 | First Man to Accept Islam | Immediate acceptance, inviting others |
| 3 | Sacrifices for Islam | Freeing slaves, spending wealth |
| 4 | The Cave of Thawr | Hijrah companion, Quran 9:40 |
| 5 | The Title as-Siddiq | Night Journey, unwavering faith |
| 6 | Life in Medina | Battles, Hajj leadership, leading prayers |
| 7 | The First Caliph | Riddah Wars, Quran compilation |
| 8 | Legacy & Passing | Death, burial, lasting impact |

## Lesson

Abu Bakr's life teaches that true faith is demonstrated through action — immediate belief, selfless sacrifice, unwavering loyalty, and humble service to the truth.
