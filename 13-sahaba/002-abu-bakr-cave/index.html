What is this app?

An interactive educational app about Abu Bakr in the Cave. Explore chapters covering key events and lessons from their life.

Is the content authentic?

Yes, all content is based on authentic Islamic historical sources.

Does it track my progress?

Yes. Chapters you read are saved in your browser and persist between sessions.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once loaded, everything runs locally with no internet needed.
