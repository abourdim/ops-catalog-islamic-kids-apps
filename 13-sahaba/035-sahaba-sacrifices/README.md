# Greatest Sacrifices — Interactive Educational App

The most moving stories of sacrifice among the companions — what they gave up for their faith in Allah and His Messenger.

## Features

- Single HTML file, zero dependencies, fully offline
- 8 CSS themes, trilingual EN/FR/AR with RTL, Bismillah splash, help sidebar
- Web Audio API sounds, localStorage progress, keyboard navigation

## Chapters

| # | Chapter | Key Fact |
|---|---------|----------|
| 1 | Giving Up Wealth | Multiple companions gave their entire wealth for Islam, understanding that everything belongs to Allah. |
| 2 | Giving Up Family | Multiple companions faced their own families in opposition, choosing faith over the strongest human bonds. |
| 3 | Giving Up Homeland | Suhayb al-Rumi gave up his entire fortune just for permission to emigrate to Medina. |
| 4 | Giving Up Life | Anas ibn al-Nadr received over 80 wounds at Uhud, only recognizable by his sister from a birthmark. |
