# Khalid ibn al-Walid — Interactive Educational App

The biography of Khalid ibn al-Walid — Sword of Allah, the undefeated military commander who fought against Islam and then became its greatest champion.

## Concept

Khalid ibn al-Walid (خالد بن الوليد) — explore 7 interactive chapters with trilingual content and key facts.

## Features

- Single HTML file, zero dependencies, fully offline
- 8 CSS themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Arctic, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Sand
- Trilingual: English, French, Arabic (with RTL support)
- Splash screen with Bismillah
- Sidebar help panel with FAQ, How-To, and Wiki tabs
- Web Audio API sound effects
- Keyboard navigation (Arrow keys)
- Reading progress via localStorage

## Chapters

| # | Chapter | Key Fact |
|---|---------|----------|
| 1 | The Enemy Commander | At Uhud, Khalid's cavalry charge from behind the Muslim lines turned a Muslim victory into a costly setback. |
| 2 | The Conversion | The Prophet's personal inquiry about Khalid was the catalyst that led to his conversion to Islam. |
| 3 | Sword of Allah | Nine swords broke in Khalid's hand during the Battle of Mu'tah, showing the intensity of his fighting. |
| 4 | Conquest of Arabia | Khalid's campaign against Musaylimah at Yamama was one of the fiercest battles in Islamic military history. |
| 5 | Defeating Two Empires | Khalid's 5-day march across the waterless desert from Iraq to Syria is studied in military academies today. |
| 6 | Never Defeated | Khalid never lost a single battle in over 100 engagements — one of the most remarkable records in military history. |
| 7 | Humble End | On his deathbed, Khalid wept because he was dying in bed rather than on the battlefield, saying every inch of his body bore a battle scar. |
