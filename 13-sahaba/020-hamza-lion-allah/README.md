# Hamza ibn Abdul-Muttalib — Interactive Educational App

The life of Hamza ibn Abdul-Muttalib — the Lion of Allah and the Chief of Martyrs. The Prophet's uncle whose bravery and sacrifice became legendary.

## Concept

Hamza ibn Abdul-Muttalib (حمزة بن عبد المطلب) — explore 6 interactive chapters with trilingual content and key facts.

## Features

- Single HTML file, zero dependencies, fully offline
- 8 CSS themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Arctic, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Sand
- Trilingual: English, French, Arabic (with RTL support)
- Splash screen with Bismillah
- Sidebar help panel with FAQ, How-To, and Wiki tabs
- Web Audio API sound effects
- Keyboard navigation (Arrow keys)
- Reading progress via localStorage

## Chapters

| # | Chapter | Key Fact |
|---|---------|----------|
| 1 | The Warrior Uncle | Hamza and the Prophet were the same age and grew up together as foster brothers. |
| 2 | The Dramatic Conversion | Hamza's conversion began as an act of protective anger but became a sincere and deep faith commitment. |
| 3 | Shield of the Believers | Hamza's conversion was a turning point — the persecution of Muslims decreased significantly afterward. |
| 4 | Lion of Badr | Hamza earned the title 'Asadullah' (Lion of Allah) at the Battle of Badr for his extraordinary valor. |
| 5 | Martyrdom at Uhud | Hamza was martyred at Uhud and the Prophet was deeply grieved, calling him 'the Chief of the Martyrs.' |
| 6 | Chief of Martyrs | The Prophet gave Hamza the title 'Chief of the Martyrs' (Sayyid al-Shuhada) — the highest rank of martyrdom. |
