# Fitrah Garden

Grow a garden representing natural human disposition (fitrah). Nurture with good deeds.

## Concept

A garden simulator where children plant seeds of fitrah and nurture them with good deeds. Each good deed helps plants grow, while neglect causes them to wither.

## Features

- Single HTML file, zero dependencies, fully offline
- 8 CSS themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Arctic, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Sand
- Trilingual: English, French, Arabic (with RTL support)
- Splash screen with Bismillah
- Sidebar help panel with FAQ, How-To, and Wiki tabs
- Web Audio API sound effects (no audio files)
- High score persistence via localStorage

## Lesson

Every child is born upon the fitrah (natural disposition toward good). Like a garden, our faith needs nurturing through good deeds and remembrance of Allah.
