Faith needs all parts working together — just like a tree needs roots, trunk, branches, and sun.

🌳 Tree of Faith

The Prophet ﷺ compared faith to a tree with many branches, the highest being La ilaha illallah.

🌱 Roots

Strong aqeedah (belief) is the foundation. Without it, nothing else can grow.

🍎 Fruits

The rewards of faith: peace of heart, answered duas, Paradise, and Allah's pleasure.
