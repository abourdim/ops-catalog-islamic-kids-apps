❓ Help
What is this game?

Learn about the events of the Day of Judgment (Yawm al-Qiyamah) by ordering them chronologically and answering quiz questions about Akhirah concepts.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once loaded, everything runs in your browser with no internet needed.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Works on any device with a modern browser — phone, tablet, laptop, desktop. Tap and hold to drag cards on touch devices.

How do I change the theme?

Click any theme button (🖥 🌌 🔥 etc.) in the controls bar.

How do I change the language?

Click EN / FR / ع. Arabic enables right-to-left layout automatically.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser. No data is sent anywhere.

What are the difficulty levels?

Easy: 7 main events. Medium: 11 events. Hard: all 15 events plus quiz questions.

🎯 Purpose

Learn the events of the Day of Judgment in order, and understand key Akhirah concepts through interactive quizzes. Kid-friendly, emphasizing Allah's mercy and hope.

📖 Yawm al-Qiyamah

The Day of Judgment is one of the six pillars of Iman (faith). Every Muslim must believe in it. It is the day when all people will be resurrected and held accountable.

🎨 Themes

8 built-in themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Arctic, Sand.

🌐 Languages

Trilingual: English, Français, العربية. Arabic auto-enables RTL layout.

🔊 Sound

Web Audio API — no files needed. Correct = rising tone, wrong = buzz, win = melody. Toggle with 🔊 button.

🔒 Privacy

Local-first. No tracking, no analytics, no external calls. All data stays in your browser.
