# Six Pillars of Faith

Interactive exploration of the 6 pillars of iman.

## Concept

Step through the six articles of faith: belief in Allah, Angels, Divine Books, Prophets, Day of Judgment, and Divine Decree.

## Features

- Single HTML file, zero dependencies, fully offline
- 8 CSS themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Arctic, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Sand
- Trilingual: English, French, Arabic (with RTL support)
- Splash screen with Bismillah
- Sidebar help panel with FAQ, How-To, and Wiki tabs
- Web Audio API sound effects (no audio files)
- 6-step guided exploration of iman
- High score persistence via localStorage

## Lesson

Iman (faith) has six pillars. Understanding each one strengthens the Muslim's relationship with Allah and gives purpose to life.
