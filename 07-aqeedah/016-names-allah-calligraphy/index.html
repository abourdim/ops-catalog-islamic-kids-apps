❓ Help
How many names are there?

99 Names (Asma ul-Husna) are mentioned in the Sunnah.

Can I trace the names?

Yes! Click 'Show Guide' to display the name faintly on the canvas, then trace over it.

What tool am I using?

A canvas drawing tool — draw with your mouse or finger on touch devices.

1

Navigate through the 99 Names using the arrows

2

Click Show Guide to display the name as a tracing template

3

Draw over the guide to practice calligraphy

4

Use Clear to start fresh

5

Adjust brush size with the slider

✨ Asma ul-Husna
