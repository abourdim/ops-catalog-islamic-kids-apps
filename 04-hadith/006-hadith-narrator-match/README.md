# Hadith Narrator Match

Interactive matching game where kids connect famous hadiths to the Sahabi (companion) who narrated them.

## Concept

Children see a well-known hadith and must identify which companion of the Prophet (peace be upon him) narrated it. This teaches both the hadith content and the important narrators of prophetic traditions.

## Featured Narrators

| # | Narrator | Known For |
|---|----------|-----------|
| 1 | Abu Hurairah | Most prolific hadith narrator (5,374 hadiths) |
| 2 | Abdullah ibn Umar | Son of Umar, known for following Sunnah precisely |
| 3 | Aisha bint Abu Bakr | Major source of hadiths about private life of Prophet |
| 4 | Anas ibn Malik | Served the Prophet for 10 years |
| 5 | Abdullah ibn Abbas | Scholar of the Ummah, Quran interpreter |
| 6 | Jabir ibn Abdullah | Attended many battles and gatherings |

## Features

- Single HTML file, zero dependencies, fully offline
- 8 CSS themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Arctic, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Sand
- Trilingual: English, French, Arabic (with RTL support)
- Splash screen with Bismillah
- Sidebar help panel with FAQ, How-To, and Wiki tabs
- Web Audio API sound effects (no audio files)
- High score persistence via localStorage
- 15 hadith-narrator pairs shuffled each playthrough

## Lesson

Behind every hadith is a trustworthy companion who carefully preserved the Prophet's words. Learning the narrators helps us appreciate the chain of transmission that kept Islam authentic.
