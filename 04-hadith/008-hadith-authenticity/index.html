What is hadith grading?

Scholars classify hadiths based on the reliability of their chain of narration. Sahih means authentic, Hasan means good, and Da'if means weak.

What makes a hadith Sahih?

A continuous chain of trustworthy, precise narrators with no hidden defects or irregularities.

Can we act on Da'if hadiths?

Scholars differ, but weak hadiths are generally not used for deriving rulings. They may be mentioned for encouragement with conditions.

✅ Sahih (Authentic)

Continuous chain, all narrators trustworthy and precise, no hidden defects.

🟡 Hasan (Good)

Similar to Sahih but narrators have slightly less precision. Still acceptable.

🔴 Da'if (Weak)

Has a problem in the chain — a missing link, an unreliable narrator, or a contradiction.
