What is Hadith Qudsi?

A hadith where Allah speaks in the first person through the Prophet. The meaning is from Allah, the wording from the Prophet.

How is it different from Quran?

The Quran is Allah's word in both meaning AND wording. Hadith Qudsi has Allah's meaning but the Prophet's wording.
