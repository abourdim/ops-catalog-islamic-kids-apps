❓ Help
What is 40 Nawawi?

An interactive game to learn Imam Nawawi's famous collection of 42 foundational hadiths. Browse them as flashcards or test your knowledge in quiz mode!

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once loaded, everything runs in your browser with no internet needed.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Works on any device with a modern browser — phone, tablet, laptop, desktop.

How do I change the theme?

Click any theme button (🖥 🌌 🔥 etc.) in the controls bar.

How do I change the language?

Click EN / FR / ع. Arabic enables right-to-left layout automatically.

What are the difficulty levels?

Easy: 10 most famous hadiths. Medium: 20 hadiths. Hard: all 42 hadiths of the collection.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser. No data is sent anywhere.

🎯 Purpose

Learn the 42 hadiths of Imam Nawawi's collection — foundational texts every Muslim should know. Each hadith includes Arabic text, English meaning, topic, and a practical lesson for kids.

📖 Learn Mode

Browse hadiths as flashcards. Each card shows the hadith number, Arabic excerpt, English meaning, topic, and a practical lesson for kids.

✏️ Quiz Mode

Test your knowledge! Given a topic or partial text, pick the correct hadith from 4 options. Correct answers reveal the full hadith and lesson.

🎨 Themes

8 built-in themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Arctic, Sand.

🌐 Languages

Trilingual: English, Français, العربية. Arabic auto-enables RTL layout.

🔊 Sound

Web Audio API — no files needed. Correct = rising tone, wrong = buzz, win = melody. Toggle with 🔊 button.

🔒 Privacy

Local-first. No tracking, no analytics, no external calls. All data stays in your browser.
