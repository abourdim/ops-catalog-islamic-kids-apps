❓ Help
What is Hadith Sciences?

A learning game about hadith classification and the major hadith collections. You'll learn about types like Sahih, Hasan, Da'if, and the famous books like Sahih Bukhari and Sahih Muslim.

What are the game modes?

Classification: You are shown a description of a hadith type and must pick the correct term. Books Match: You are shown a fact about a hadith collection and must pick the correct book.

What are the difficulty levels?

Easy: Basic types (Sahih, Hasan, Da'if) + 2 books. Medium: More types + 4 books. Hard: All 9 types + all 8 books.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once loaded, everything runs in your browser with no internet needed.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Works on any device with a modern browser.

How do I change the theme?

Click any theme button in the controls bar.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser. No data is sent anywhere.

📖 Hadith Classification

Scholars classify hadiths by authenticity: Sahih (authentic), Hasan (good), Da'if (weak), Mawdu' (fabricated). They also classify by transmission: Mutawatir (mass-transmitted) and Ahad (single-chain). And by attribution: Marfu' (to the Prophet), Mawquf (to a Companion), Maqtu' (to a Tabi'i).

📚 The Six Major Books

The Kutub al-Sittah: Sahih al-Bukhari, Sahih Muslim, Sunan Abu Dawud, Jami' at-Tirmidhi, Sunan an-Nasa'i, and Sunan Ibn Majah. Plus Muwatta Malik and Musnad Ahmad.

🎨 Themes

🌐 Languages

Trilingual: English, Francais, العربية. Arabic auto-enables RTL layout.

🔊 Sound

Web Audio API — no files needed. Correct = rising tone, wrong = buzz, win = melody. Toggle with the sound button.

🔒 Privacy

Local-first. No tracking, no analytics, no external calls. All data stays in your browser.
