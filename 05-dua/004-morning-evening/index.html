❓ Help
What is Morning & Evening?

A daily adhkar practice app for Muslim kids. Read and memorize your morning and evening remembrances of Allah.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once loaded, everything runs in your browser with no internet needed.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Works on any device with a modern browser — phone, tablet, laptop, desktop.

How do I change the theme?

Click any theme button (🖥 🌌 🔥 etc.) in the controls bar.

How do I change the language?

Click EN / FR / ع. Arabic enables right-to-left layout automatically.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser. No data is sent anywhere.

What are the two modes?

READ mode: scroll through adhkar cards and tap to count repetitions. QUIZ mode: match the dhikr to its description from 4 options.

🎯 Purpose

Practice daily morning and evening adhkar. Build the habit of remembering Allah every day.

🎨 Themes

8 built-in themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Arctic, Sand.

🌐 Languages

Trilingual: English, Français, العربية. Arabic auto-enables RTL layout.

🔊 Sound

Web Audio API — no files needed. Toggle with 🔊 button.

📖 Read Mode

Scroll through each dhikr with Arabic text, transliteration, meaning, and repetition counter.

❓ Quiz Mode

Match the benefit/description to the correct Arabic text from 4 options.

🔒 Privacy

Local-first. No tracking, no analytics, no external calls. All data stays in your browser.
