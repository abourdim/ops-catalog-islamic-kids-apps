❓ Help
What is Dua Practice?

An interactive tool to learn and memorize daily Islamic supplications (duas). Browse duas organized by time of day, or test yourself with quizzes.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once loaded, everything runs in your browser with no internet needed.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Works on any device with a modern browser — phone, tablet, laptop, desktop.

How do I change the theme?

Click any theme button in the controls bar.

How do I change the language?

Click EN / FR / ع. Arabic enables right-to-left layout automatically.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser. No data is sent anywhere.

What do the difficulty levels mean?

Easy = 10 common duas. Medium = 15 duas. Hard = all 25+ duas including less common ones.

Purpose

Learn and memorize daily Islamic supplications (duas) organized by time of day. Practice with interactive flashcards and test knowledge in quiz mode.

Themes

8 built-in themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Arctic, Sand.

Languages

Trilingual: English, Français, العربية. Arabic auto-enables RTL layout.

Sound

Web Audio API — no files needed. Correct = rising tone, wrong = buzz, win = melody. Toggle with sound button.

Duas

25+ authentic daily duas organized into Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Night, and Anytime categories. Each includes Arabic text with tashkeel, transliteration, and English meaning.

Privacy

Local-first. No tracking, no analytics, no external calls. All data stays in your browser.
