❓ Help
What is Adhkar Counter?

A digital tasbeeh (counter) to help you keep track of your daily adhkar (remembrance of Allah). Select a dhikr, tap to count, and stay consistent.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once loaded, everything runs in your browser with no internet needed.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. Works on any device with a modern browser. Tap the circle to count. Vibration feedback on supported devices.

How does auto-advance work?

After completing SubhanAllah (33), it auto-switches to Alhamdulillah (33), then Allahu Akbar (34) — the post-salah sequence.

How do I change the theme?

Click any theme button in the controls bar.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser. No data is sent anywhere. Your daily count is saved in localStorage.

🎯 Purpose

A digital tasbeeh to help Muslim kids and adults track their daily adhkar consistently.

📿 Post-Salah Sequence

SubhanAllah ×33, Alhamdulillah ×33, Allahu Akbar ×34 — auto-advances through the full sequence.

🎨 Themes

8 built-in themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Arctic, Sand.

🌐 Languages

Trilingual: English, Francais, Arabic. Arabic auto-enables RTL layout.

🔊 Sound

Web Audio API click sound on each tap. Toggle with the sound button.

📳 Vibration

Haptic feedback on each tap if your device supports it.

🔒 Privacy

Local-first. No tracking, no analytics, no external calls. All data stays in your browser.
