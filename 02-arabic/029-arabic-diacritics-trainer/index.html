\u2753 Help
What are harakat?

Harakat are the vowel marks placed above or below Arabic letters: fatha (\u064e), damma (\u064f), kasra (\u0650), sukun (\u0652), shadda (\u0651), and tanwin forms.

Why are they important?

Harakat change the meaning and pronunciation of words. They are essential for correct Quran recitation (tajweed).

Fatha \u064e

A small line above the letter. Produces the 'a' sound.

Damma \u064f

A small waw above the letter. Produces the 'u' sound.

Kasra \u0650

A small line below the letter. Produces the 'i' sound.

Sukun \u0652

A small circle above. Indicates no vowel after the letter.

Shadda \u0651

A small w-shape above. Indicates the letter is doubled.
