\u2753 Help
What is this app?

A handwriting practice tool for Arabic letters. Draw on the canvas and get accuracy feedback.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser.

How is accuracy calculated?

Your drawn strokes are compared pixel-by-pixel against the reference letter pattern.

1

Select an Arabic letter from the grid

2

Use the Guide button to see/hide the reference letter

3

Draw the letter on the canvas with your finger or mouse

4

Click Check to see your accuracy score

5

Click Clear to try again and improve your score

Arabic Script

Arabic is written right-to-left and has 28 letters. Each letter has different forms depending on its position in a word.

Handwriting

Correct letter formation builds a strong foundation for reading and writing Arabic, especially for Quran recitation.
