What is a root?

Most Arabic words come from a 3-letter root. The root carries the core meaning, and patterns (awzan) create related words.

Why is this important?

Understanding roots helps you guess word meanings and build vocabulary quickly. It is key to understanding Quranic Arabic.

Arabic Root System

Arabic has a trilateral root system. Most words derive from 3 consonants that carry the base meaning.

Patterns (Awzan)

Patterns are templates applied to roots. For example, the pattern fa'il means 'doer' - from k-t-b you get kaatib (writer).
