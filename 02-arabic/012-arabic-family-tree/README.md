# Arabic Family Tree — Learn Arabic Family Terms

Interactive game to learn Arabic family relationship terms by building a family tree (شجرة العائلة).

## Concept

Children see a family member emoji with their English role and must pick the correct Arabic term from four options. Covers 12 key family relationships including paternal and maternal aunts and uncles.

## The 12 Family Members

| # | Arabic | Transliteration | English | French |
|---|--------|----------------|---------|--------|
| 1 | جَدّ | Jadd | Grandfather | Grand-père |
| 2 | جَدَّة | Jaddah | Grandmother | Grand-mère |
| 3 | أَب | Ab | Father | Père |
| 4 | أُمّ | Umm | Mother | Mère |
| 5 | اِبْن | Ibn | Son | Fils |
| 6 | بِنْت | Bint | Daughter | Fille |
| 7 | أَخ | Akh | Brother | Frère |
| 8 | أُخْت | Ukht | Sister | Sœur |
| 9 | عَمّ | 'Amm | Uncle (paternal) | Oncle paternel |
| 10 | عَمَّة | 'Ammah | Aunt (paternal) | Tante paternelle |
| 11 | خَال | Khal | Uncle (maternal) | Oncle maternel |
| 12 | خَالَة | Khalah | Aunt (maternal) | Tante maternelle |

## Features

- Single HTML file, zero dependencies, fully offline
- 8 CSS themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Arctic, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Sand
- Trilingual: English, French, Arabic (with RTL support)
- Splash screen with Bismillah
- Sidebar help panel with FAQ, How-To, and Wiki tabs
- Web Audio API sound effects
- High score persistence via localStorage

## Lesson

Islam places great emphasis on family bonds. Maintaining ties of kinship (silat ar-rahim) is among the most important duties.
