What is Arabic Animals?

A vocabulary game to learn animal names in Arabic. Match emojis to Arabic names and vice versa. Some animals are mentioned in the Quran!

يعمل بدون إنترنت؟

Yes. Once loaded, everything runs in your browser with no internet needed.

يعمل على الهاتف؟

Yes. Works on any device with a modern browser — phone, tablet, laptop, desktop.

What are the game modes?

Emoji→Arabic: see an emoji, pick the Arabic name. Arabic→Emoji: see Arabic text, pick the emoji. Mixed: alternates both.

What are the difficulty levels?

Easy: 10 common animals. Medium: 18 animals. Hard: all 27 animals including less common ones.

كيف أغير المظهر؟

Click any theme button (🖥 🌌 🔥 etc.) in the controls bar.

How do I change the language?

Click EN / FR / ع. Arabic enables right-to-left layout automatically.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser. No data is sent anywhere.

🎯 Purpose

Learn Arabic animal vocabulary through an interactive matching game. Features animals mentioned in the Quran with references.

🕌 Quranic Animals

Several animals in this game are mentioned in the Quran: Camel, Elephant (Surah Al-Fil), Bee (Surah An-Nahl), Ant (Surah An-Naml), Spider (Surah Al-Ankabut), Whale (Surah As-Saffat), Horse, Cow (Surah Al-Baqarah), Dog (Surah Al-Kahf), Hoopoe (Surah An-Naml).

🎨 Themes

8 built-in themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Arctic, Sand.

🌐 Languages

Trilingual: English, Français, العربية. Arabic auto-enables RTL layout.

🔊 Sound

Web Audio API — no files needed. Correct = rising tone, wrong = buzz, win = melody. Toggle with 🔊 button.

🔒 Privacy

Local-first. No tracking, no analytics, no external calls. All data stays in your browser.
