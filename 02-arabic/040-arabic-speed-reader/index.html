What is this?

A speed reading trainer for Arabic. Words flash on screen one at a time. Adjust the speed and practice reading faster!

What is WPM?

Words Per Minute - a measure of how fast you are reading. The app calculates it based on words shown and time elapsed.

1

Adjust the speed slider (left=slow, right=fast)

2

Press Play to start flashing words

3

Read each word as it appears

4

Press Stop to see your reading stats

Speed Reading

Speed reading Arabic improves fluency and comprehension. Start slow and gradually increase speed as you improve.
