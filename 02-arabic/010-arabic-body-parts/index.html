❓ Help
What is this app?

An interactive drag-and-drop game to learn body part names in Arabic. Labels appear and you drag them onto the correct spots on a body diagram.

Is it suitable for kids?

Yes! Designed for Muslim kids to learn Arabic vocabulary in a fun, interactive way.

Does it work offline?

Yes, once loaded everything runs locally in your browser.

How do I change the theme?

Click any theme icon in the controls bar to switch between 8 themes.

How do I switch language?

Click EN / FR / ع to change the interface language. Arabic enables RTL.

Arabic Vocabulary

Learning body parts in Arabic is fundamental vocabulary. The Quran and Hadith mention many body parts in context of health, prayer, and wudu.

Wudu Connection

Many body parts in this game are washed during wudu (ablution): face, hands, arms, feet.

Themes

8 built-in themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Arctic, Sand.
