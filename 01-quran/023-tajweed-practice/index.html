What is Tajweed?

Tajweed means beautification. It is the set of rules for proper pronunciation and recitation of the Quran, ensuring each letter is given its right.

What do the colors mean?

Each tajweed rule has its own color, matching the color-coding system used in many printed Qurans (like the Tajweed Mushaf).
