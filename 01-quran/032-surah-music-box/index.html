What is Surah Music Box?

An interactive tool that represents each of the 114 Quran surahs as unique tonal patterns for exploration.

Is this music?

No. These are pure tones representing rhythmic patterns of tajweed, not musical instruments.

Does it work offline?

Yes, fully self-contained in your browser.

Tajweed Rhythms

Tajweed rules give Quran recitation its distinctive rhythmic quality. Each surah has natural cadence patterns.
