What is Memorize Helper?

A progressive memorization tool for short surahs of the Quran. Words are gradually hidden and you fill them in from memory.

How does progressive hiding work?

Step 1: Full text shown. Step 2: 25% hidden. Step 3: 50% hidden. Step 4: 75% hidden. Step 5: All words hidden. You pick correct words from a word bank at each step.

What do the difficulty levels do?

Easy: Steps 1-2 only, short surahs highlighted. Medium: Steps 1-4. Hard: All 5 steps with all surahs available.

🎯 Purpose

Help Muslim kids memorize short surahs of the Quran using a progressive hiding technique. Words are gradually removed and the child reconstructs the surah from memory.

📖 Surahs

12 short surahs: Al-Fatiha, Al-Ikhlas, Al-Falaq, An-Nas, Al-Kawthar, Al-Asr, Al-Fil, Quraysh, Al-Maun, An-Nasr, Al-Masad, Al-Kafirun.

🎨 Themes

8 built-in themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Arctic, Sand.

🌐 Languages

Trilingual: English, Français, العربية. Arabic auto-enables RTL layout.

🔊 Sound

Web Audio API sounds. Correct = rising tone, wrong = buzz, complete = melody. Toggle with the speaker button.

🔒 Privacy

Local-first. No tracking, no analytics, no external calls. All data stays in your browser.
