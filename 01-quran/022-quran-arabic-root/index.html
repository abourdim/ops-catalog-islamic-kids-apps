What is a root word?

Arabic words are built from 3-letter roots. From one root, many related words are derived. For example, from K-T-B come kitab (book), katib (writer), maktub (written).

Why learn roots?

Understanding roots lets you recognize word families in the Quran, making it easier to understand Arabic vocabulary.
