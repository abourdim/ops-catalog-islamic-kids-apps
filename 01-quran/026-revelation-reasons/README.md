# Revelation Reasons — Asbab al-Nuzul

Interactive catalog of famous Quranic verses paired with the historical events that prompted their revelation.

## Concept

Browse 12 famous Quranic verses with their asbab al-nuzul (reasons/occasions of revelation). Click each card to reveal the story behind why that verse was sent down. Understanding context deepens understanding of meaning.

## Verses Included

- "No compulsion in religion" (2:256)
- "O mankind, We created you from male and female" (49:13)
- First revelation: "Read!" (96:1-5)
- Innocence of Aisha (24:11-20)
- Ad-Duha consolation (93:1-11)
- He Frowned (80:1-10)
- Al-Kawthar (108:1-3)
- And many more...

## Features

- Single HTML file, zero dependencies, fully offline
- 8 CSS themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Arctic, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Sand
- Trilingual: English, French, Arabic (with RTL support)
- Splash screen with Bismillah
- Sidebar help panel with FAQ, How-To, and Wiki tabs
- Web Audio API sound effects (no audio files)
- 12 verses with detailed revelation stories
- Expandable cards
- Preferences persisted via localStorage

## Lesson

Knowing why verses were revealed (asbab al-nuzul) is a key Quranic science that helps understand the context, meaning, and application of Allah's words.
