# Surah Benefits — Virtues and Rewards of Specific Surahs

Interactive catalog of special virtues and benefits of specific surahs with authentic hadith references.

## Concept

10 surahs (and Ayat al-Kursi) are presented with their known virtues from authentic hadith. Each card shows the surah name, its special status, the hadith reference, source, and a bullet list of benefits.

## Surahs Covered

Al-Fatihah, Al-Baqarah, Al-Kahf, Ya-Sin, Ar-Rahman, Al-Waqi'ah, Al-Mulk, Al-Ikhlas, Al-Falaq & An-Nas, Ayat al-Kursi

## Features

- Single HTML file, zero dependencies, fully offline
- 8 CSS themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Arctic, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Sand
- Trilingual: English, French, Arabic (with RTL support)
- Splash screen with Bismillah
- Sidebar help panel with FAQ, How-To, and Wiki tabs
- Web Audio API sound effects (no audio files)
- Hadith sources cited (Bukhari, Muslim, etc.)
- Expandable cards with benefits lists
- Preferences persisted via localStorage

## Lesson

Knowing the special virtues of certain surahs motivates children to memorize and regularly recite them, building a lifelong connection with the Quran.
