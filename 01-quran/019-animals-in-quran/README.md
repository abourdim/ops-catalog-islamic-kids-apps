# Animals in Quran — Interactive Catalog of Animals Mentioned in the Quran

Interactive catalog of 16 animals mentioned in the Quran with their stories, references, and lessons.

## Concept

Browse cards for animals mentioned in the Quran — from the cow of Bani Israel to the hoopoe of Sulayman. Each card shows the animal's name, emoji icon, Quranic reference, and the story/lesson associated with it.

## Animals Included

Cow, Bee, Ant, Hoopoe, Elephant, Whale, Horse, Camel, Spider, Mosquito, Crow, Snake, Sheep/Ram, Birds, Locust/Louse, Fish

## Features

- Single HTML file, zero dependencies, fully offline
- 8 CSS themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Arctic, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Sand
- Trilingual: English, French, Arabic (with RTL support)
- Splash screen with Bismillah
- Sidebar help panel with FAQ, How-To, and Wiki tabs
- Web Audio API sound effects (no audio files)
- Expandable animal cards with stories
- Quranic references for each animal
- Preferences persisted via localStorage

## Lesson

Animals in the Quran teach us about Allah's wisdom in creation, the importance of kindness to all creatures, and provide powerful parables for human behavior.
