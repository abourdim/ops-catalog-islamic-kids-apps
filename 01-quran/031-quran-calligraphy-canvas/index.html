What is this app?

A canvas drawing app for practicing Quran calligraphy. Write Arabic verses with different pen styles.

Do I need a stylus?

No, finger or mouse works. A stylus gives better control on tablets.

What are the pen styles?

Naskh (thin, standard), Thuluth (bold, decorative), Diwani (medium, flowing).

How does the guide work?

A faint overlay shows the verse text. Adjust opacity with the slider. Trace over it to practice.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Everything runs in your browser.

Arabic Calligraphy

Islamic calligraphy is a revered art form. Writing Quran verses beautifully is considered an act of worship and devotion.

Naskh Script

The most common script for printing the Quran. Clear, readable, and elegant.

Thuluth Script

A grand decorative script used for headings and architectural inscriptions in mosques.

Diwani Script

A flowing, cursive script developed in the Ottoman period. Elegant and expressive.
