What is Rhythm Tapper?

A rhythm game where beats scroll across the screen and you tap when they reach the target circle. Rhythms are based on tajweed patterns.

How do I score?

Tap when a beat is within the hit zone. Consecutive hits build combos for higher scores.

1

Press Play to start beats scrolling

2

Watch beats move from right to left

3

Tap the button (or press Space) when a beat reaches the circle

4

Build combos by hitting consecutive beats accurately

Tajweed Rhythm

Tajweed rules create natural rhythmic patterns in Quran recitation. Short vowels, long vowels, and stops each have specific timing.
