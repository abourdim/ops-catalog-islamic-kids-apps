What is Surah Info Cards?

An interactive catalog of all 114 surahs of the Quran. Each card shows the surah name, number, revelation place, verse count, and key themes.

Can I search for a surah?

Yes! Use the search box to find surahs by name (English or Arabic) or number.

Can I filter Makki/Madani?

Yes. Click the filter buttons to show only Makki or Madani surahs.

📖 Surah

A surah is a chapter of the Quran. There are 114 surahs, varying in length from 3 to 286 verses.

🕊 Makki vs Madani

Makki surahs were revealed in Mecca (before Hijrah) and often discuss faith and the hereafter. Madani surahs were revealed in Medina and often contain laws and social guidance.

🌈 Themes

8 built-in themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Arctic, Sand.
