What is Surah Color Painter?

A painting app where each surah provides a themed color palette inspired by its meanings and themes.

Why these colors?

Colors are chosen to evoke the themes: green for paradise, blue for sky/water, gold for light, etc.

1

Select a surah to load its color palette

2

Click a color swatch to select it

3

Choose brush size and opacity

4

Paint on the canvas freely

Color in the Quran

The Quran mentions colors: green for paradise garments, white for radiant faces, and describes nature's vivid palette as signs of Allah.
