What is this game?

Famous Quranic ayahs are shown with one word missing. You choose the correct Arabic word to complete the verse.

How many questions?

10 questions per round from a pool of famous ayahs.

1

Read the ayah with the blank (______)

2

Choose the correct missing word from 4 options

3

Learn the complete ayah and its reference

📖 Hifz

Memorizing the Quran (hifz) is one of the greatest acts of worship. This game helps reinforce memorization of key verses.
