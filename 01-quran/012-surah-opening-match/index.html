What is this game?

You see the opening verse of a surah in Arabic and must identify which surah it belongs to from 4 choices.

📖 Opening Verses

Many surahs have distinctive opening verses — some start with Huruf Muqatta'at (disconnected letters), some with praise, and some with oaths.
