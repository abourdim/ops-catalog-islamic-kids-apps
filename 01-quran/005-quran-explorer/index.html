    ❓ Help
    What is Quran Explorer?

    An interactive tool to browse and learn about the surahs of Juz Amma (the 30th part of the Quran). Explore surah facts, themes, and test your knowledge!

    Does it work offline?

    Yes. Once loaded, everything runs in your browser with no internet needed.

    Can I play on my phone?

    Yes. Works on any device with a modern browser — phone, tablet, laptop, desktop.

    How do I change the theme?

    Click any theme button (🖥 🌌 🔥 etc.) in the controls bar.

    How do I change the language?

    Click EN / FR / ع. Arabic enables right-to-left layout automatically.

    What are the difficulty levels?

    Easy: explore surahs freely. Medium: quiz appears after exploring each surah. Hard: quiz questions without seeing the card first.

    Is my data private?

    Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser. No data is sent anywhere.

    🎯 Purpose

    Help Muslim kids discover and memorize facts about Juz Amma surahs in an interactive, fun way.

    📖 Juz Amma

    The 30th part of the Quran, containing 37 surahs from An-Naba (#78) to An-Nas (#114). These are the most commonly memorized surahs.

    🎨 Themes

    8 built-in themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Arctic, Sand.

    🌐 Languages

    Trilingual: English, Francais, العربية. Arabic auto-enables RTL layout.

    🔊 Sound

    Web Audio API — no files needed. Toggle with 🔊 button.

    🔒 Privacy

    Local-first. No tracking, no analytics, no external calls. All data stays in your browser.
