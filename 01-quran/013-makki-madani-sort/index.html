What does Makki mean?

Makki surahs were revealed before the Hijrah (migration to Medina). They typically focus on faith, monotheism, and the hereafter.

What does Madani mean?

Madani surahs were revealed after the Hijrah in Medina. They often cover laws, social rules, and community matters.

How many of each?

There are approximately 86 Makki surahs and 28 Madani surahs in the Quran.

🕊 Makki Characteristics

Short verses, emotional tone, focus on aqeedah (creed), stories of previous nations, addresses "Ya ayyuha al-nas".

🍃 Madani Characteristics

Longer verses, legal rulings, social legislation, addresses "Ya ayyuha alladhina amanu".
