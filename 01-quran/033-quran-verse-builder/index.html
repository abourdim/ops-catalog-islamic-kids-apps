What is Verse Builder?

A word-ordering game where you reconstruct Quran verses from scattered Arabic words.

How does difficulty work?

Starts with short verses (3-4 words) and progresses to longer ones as you level up.

Can I remove a placed word?

Yes! Click any word in the drop zone to send it back to the word bank.
