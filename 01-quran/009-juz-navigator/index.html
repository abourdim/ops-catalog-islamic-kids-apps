What is a Juz?

A juz (plural: ajza) is one of 30 equal parts of the Quran. This division helps organize reading and memorization.

Why 30 parts?

The 30-part division allows Muslims to complete the entire Quran in one month by reading one juz per day, especially during Ramadan.

📖 Juz

Each juz contains roughly 20 pages of the Quran. The first juz starts with Al-Fatihah and the beginning of Al-Baqarah.

🕊 Hizb

Each juz is further divided into 2 hizb, and each hizb into 4 quarters, for a total of 240 quarters in the Quran.
