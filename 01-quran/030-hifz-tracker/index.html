❓ Help
How does tracking work?

Click any surah to cycle through 4 states: Not Started, Learning, Reviewing, and Memorized. Your progress is saved automatically.

What is spaced repetition?

The tracker encourages you to cycle through Learning and Reviewing stages before marking as Memorized, reinforcing retention over time.

Is my progress saved?

Yes! All progress is stored in your browser's localStorage and persists between sessions.

📚 Hifz

Hifz means memorization of the Quran. A hafiz/hafiza is someone who has memorized the entire Quran by heart — a tradition spanning over 1400 years.

🔁 Spaced Repetition

Reviewing at increasing intervals strengthens long-term memory. This is why the Quran must be regularly revised after memorization.
