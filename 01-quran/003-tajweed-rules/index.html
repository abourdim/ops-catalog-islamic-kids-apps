What is Tajweed Rules?

A game to learn tajweed — the rules of proper Quran recitation. You see a verse with highlighted text and pick which tajweed rule applies.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once loaded, everything runs in your browser with no internet needed.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Works on any device with a modern browser — phone, tablet, laptop, desktop.

How do I change the theme?

Click any theme button (🖥 🌌 🔥 etc.) in the controls bar.

How do I change the language?

Click EN / FR / ع. Arabic enables right-to-left layout automatically.

What are the difficulty levels?

Easy: 5 basic rules (Izhar, Idgham, Iqlab, Ikhfa, Qalqalah). Medium: 10 rules (adds Meem Sakinah, Madd, Ghunnah). Hard: 15+ rules (all rules including Lam rules and Madd subtypes).

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser. No data is sent anywhere.

Noon Sakinah & Tanween

Rules for ن with sukoon or tanween before other letters: Izhar (clear), Idgham (merge), Iqlab (convert to meem), Ikhfa (hide).

Meem Sakinah

Rules for م with sukoon: Ikhfa Shafawi (hide before ب), Idgham Shafawi (merge with م), Izhar Shafawi (clear before others).

Madd (Elongation)

Madd Tabii (2 counts), Madd Muttasil (connected, 4-5 counts), Madd Munfasil (separated, 4-5 counts), Madd Lazim (6 counts).

Qalqalah

Echoing sound on sukoon letters: ق ط ب ج د (Qaf, Taa, Ba, Jim, Dal).

Ghunnah

Nasalization — a sound from the nose held for 2 counts on noon and meem with shaddah.

Lam Rules

Lam Shamsiyyah: lam of ال merges into sun letters. Lam Qamariyyah: lam is pronounced before moon letters.

Themes

Privacy

Local-first. No tracking, no analytics, no external calls. All data stays in your browser.
