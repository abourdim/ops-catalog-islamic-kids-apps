# Surah Theme Explorer — Explore Surahs by Theme

Interactive explorer showing surahs grouped by their major themes: stories, laws, promises, warnings, and more.

## Concept

The Quran covers many themes across its 114 surahs. This app organizes surahs into 8 thematic categories, letting kids explore which surahs relate to which topics. Click any surah chip to learn more about its specific content under that theme.

## Themes

1. Stories of Prophets (7 surahs)
2. Laws and Rulings (5 surahs)
3. Monotheism / Tawhid (4 surahs)
4. Day of Judgment (5 surahs)
5. Nature and Creation (4 surahs)
6. Manners and Ethics (4 surahs)
7. Duas and Prayers (4 surahs)
8. Promises and Warnings (4 surahs)

## Features

- Single HTML file, zero dependencies, fully offline
- 8 CSS themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Arctic, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Sand
- Trilingual: English, French, Arabic (with RTL support)
- Splash screen with Bismillah
- Sidebar help panel with FAQ, How-To, and Wiki tabs
- Web Audio API sound effects (no audio files)
- Tab-based theme navigation
- Clickable surah chips with details
- Preferences persisted via localStorage

## Lesson

Exploring the Quran thematically helps children understand its breadth — from stories of prophets to laws, from prayers to descriptions of creation.
