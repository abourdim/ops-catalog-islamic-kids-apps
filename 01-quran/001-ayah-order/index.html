❓ Help
What is Ayah Order?

A Quran word-ordering game. The words of an ayah are shuffled and you must tap them in the correct order (right-to-left).

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once loaded, everything runs in your browser with no internet needed.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Works on any device with a modern browser.

How do I change the theme?

Click any theme button in the controls bar.

How do I change the language?

Click EN / FR / ع. Arabic enables right-to-left layout automatically.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser. No data is sent anywhere.

Which surahs are included?

Short surahs from Juz Amma: Al-Fatiha, Al-Ikhlas, Al-Falaq, An-Nas, Al-Kawthar, Al-Asr, Al-Fil, Quraysh, Al-Maun, An-Nasr, and more.

🎯 Purpose

Help Muslim kids memorize Quran by practicing word order of short ayahs from Juz Amma.

🎨 Themes

8 built-in themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Arctic, Sand.

🌐 Languages

Trilingual: English, Français, العربية. Arabic auto-enables RTL layout.

🔊 Sound

Web Audio API. Correct = rising tone, wrong = buzz, win = melody. Toggle with sound button.

📖 Surahs

Al-Fatiha, Al-Ikhlas, Al-Falaq, An-Nas, Al-Kawthar, Al-Asr, Al-Fil, Quraysh, Al-Maun, An-Nasr.

🔒 Privacy

Local-first. No tracking, no analytics, no external calls. All data stays in your browser.
