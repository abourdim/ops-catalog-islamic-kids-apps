# Quran Timeline — Revelation Events Through History

Interactive timeline of major revelation events linked to specific surahs over the 23-year period of Quranic revelation.

## Concept

A visual timeline spanning from 610 CE to 632 CE, divided into Early Meccan, Late Meccan, and Medinan periods. Each event on the timeline shows the year, the historical event, which surahs were connected to it, and a detailed description. Click events to expand.

## Events Covered

- First Revelation in Cave Hira (Al-Alaq)
- Public Dawah begins
- Migration to Abyssinia (Surah Maryam)
- Year of Sorrow (Ad-Duha, Ash-Sharh)
- Night Journey (Al-Isra)
- Hijrah to Medina
- Battle of Badr (Al-Anfal)
- Battle of Uhud (Aal-Imran)
- Battle of the Trench (Al-Ahzab)
- Treaty of Hudaybiyyah (Al-Fath)
- Conquest of Mecca (An-Nasr)
- Farewell Sermon (Al-Ma'idah 5:3)

## Features

- Single HTML file, zero dependencies, fully offline
- 8 CSS themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Arctic, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Sand
- Trilingual: English, French, Arabic (with RTL support)
- Splash screen with Bismillah
- Sidebar help panel with FAQ, How-To, and Wiki tabs
- Web Audio API sound effects (no audio files)
- Expandable timeline entries
- Era-based grouping
- Preferences persisted via localStorage

## Lesson

Understanding when and why verses were revealed deepens appreciation for the Quran's wisdom and its connection to the life of Prophet Muhammad.
