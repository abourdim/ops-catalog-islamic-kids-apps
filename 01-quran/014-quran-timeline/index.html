How long did revelation take?

The Quran was revealed over approximately 23 years — 13 years in Mecca and 10 years in Medina.

Was it revealed all at once?

No. It was revealed gradually, often in response to specific events, which helped the community understand and apply its teachings.

📚 Wahy

Wahy means divine revelation. Angel Jibreel brought Allah's words to Prophet Muhammad over 23 years.

📅 Hijrah

The migration from Mecca to Medina in 622 CE marks the turning point between Makki and Madani revelations.
