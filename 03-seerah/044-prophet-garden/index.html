What is this?

A virtual garden where you plant, water, and harvest plants mentioned in the Quran and sunnah. Each plant teaches a lesson.

1. Select a seed from the toolbar. 2. Click empty soil on the grid to plant. 3. Water your plants regularly. 4. Harvest when they are fully grown.
The Quran mentions many plants: date palms, olives, grapes, pomegranates, and herbs. The Prophet ﷺ loved gardening and encouraged planting trees as charity.
