🕋 The Ka'bah

The sacred house of Allah, originally built by Prophet Ibrahim and Ismail. It is the qiblah (direction of prayer) for all Muslims.

⛰ Safa and Marwa

The two hills between which Hajar ran searching for water for baby Ismail. This became the ritual of Sa'i in Hajj.

🏠 Dar al-Arqam

The house where the Prophet ﷺ secretly taught Islam to early converts during the Meccan period.
