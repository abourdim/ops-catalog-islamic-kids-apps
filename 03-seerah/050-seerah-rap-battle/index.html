What is this?

A word-selection poetry game inspired by Hassan ibn Thabit, the official poet of the Prophet ﷺ. Complete verses about seerah events by choosing the right rhyming words.

Who was Hassan ibn Thabit?

He was the Prophet's ﷺ poet who defended Islam through his eloquent verses. The Prophet ﷺ set up a pulpit for him in the mosque and said the Holy Spirit (Jibreel) supports him.

1. Read the verse with a missing word. 2. Choose the correct word from the bank. 3. Build streaks for bonus points. 4. Complete all 8 rounds to win.
Poetry was the most powerful media in ancient Arabia. The Prophet ﷺ used poetry to defend Islam, inspire companions, and preserve the seerah in beautiful verse.
