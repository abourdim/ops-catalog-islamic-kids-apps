What is Shura?

Shura means consultation. The Prophet ﷺ regularly consulted his companions before making important decisions, showing that wisdom comes from collective thinking.

How does this work?

You hear different companions present their views on a real historical issue, then vote for the decision that was actually taken.

1

Read the topic being debated in the shura council

2

Listen to each companion's argument

3

Vote for the position you think was adopted

4

Learn which decision was actually made and why

🤝 The Principle of Shura

Allah says in the Quran: 'And whose affair is determined by consultation among themselves' (42:38). The Prophet ﷺ practiced shura throughout his life.
