What is this?

A city builder game where you construct Medina by placing buildings on a grid. Manage gold, food, wood, and population.

How do I earn resources?

Farms produce food each turn, markets generate gold, and wells support more buildings. Click Next Turn to advance.

What's the goal?

Build a thriving Medina with a masjid at its center, homes for the people, and markets for trade.
