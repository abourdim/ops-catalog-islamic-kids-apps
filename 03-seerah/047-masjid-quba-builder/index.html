What is Masjid Quba?

The first mosque ever built in Islam. The Prophet ﷺ laid its foundation stones upon arriving near Medina during the Hijrah. Allah mentions it in the Quran as a mosque founded on piety.

What reward does visiting it give?

The Prophet ﷺ said: Whoever purifies himself at home then goes to Masjid Quba and prays there, he will have a reward like that of an Umrah.
