What is this?

An interactive canvas map where you trace the Hijrah route from Mecca to Medina, marking important stops and landmarks along the way.

Is this historically accurate?

Yes. The stops are based on authentic seerah sources about the Hijrah journey in 622 CE (1 AH).

How do I play?

Click the glowing landmarks in the correct order. Each landmark represents a real stop on the Hijrah route.

🕌 The Hijrah

The migration from Mecca to Medina in 622 CE marks the beginning of the Islamic calendar. The Prophet and Abu Bakr traveled a southern route to avoid Quraysh pursuers.

🏔 Cave Thawr

The Prophet and Abu Bakr hid here for three days. A spider web and bird nest at the entrance concealed them from Quraysh trackers.

🐪 The Journey

The journey took about 8 days, following an unusual southern coastal route before heading north to Medina.
