What is this?

A trading simulation where you buy and sell goods between ancient Arabian cities, following the trade routes the Prophet ﷺ traveled.

How does trust work?

Honest dealings increase trust. Selling at fair prices and being generous earns you trust, reflecting the Prophet's ﷺ title of Al-Amin (The Trustworthy).

1

🐪 The Prophet as Merchant

Before prophethood, Muhammad ﷺ was known as Al-Amin (The Trustworthy) and Al-Sadiq (The Truthful) for his honest trading practices.

💍 Khadijah's Trade

Khadijah was a wealthy merchant who hired Muhammad ﷺ to lead her caravan to Syria. His honesty and skill impressed her greatly.
