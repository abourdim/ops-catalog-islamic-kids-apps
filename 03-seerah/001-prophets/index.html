What is this app?

A fun quiz game to learn about the noble prophets mentioned in the Holy Quran. Test your knowledge!

How many prophets are covered?

The quiz covers all 25 prophets mentioned by name in the Quran, peace be upon them all.

Is this educational?

Yes! Each question teaches you about a prophet's story, miracles, and importance in Islam.

The Prophets

Allah sent prophets to guide humanity. 25 are mentioned by name in the Quran, from Adam (AS) to Muhammad (SAW).

Ulul Azm

The five greatest prophets: Nuh, Ibrahim, Musa, Isa, and Muhammad (peace be upon them all).

Our Duty

Muslims believe in all prophets without distinction and follow the final message of Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
