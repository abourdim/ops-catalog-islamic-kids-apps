# Cave Hira Meditation

Peaceful canvas scene of Cave Hira at night with twinkling stars, tap to reveal moments of reflection

## Concept

Peaceful canvas scene of Cave Hira at night with twinkling stars, tap to reveal moments of reflection. An interactive educational app for Muslim kids in the Seerah category.

## Features

- Single HTML file, zero dependencies, fully offline
- 8 CSS themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Arctic, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Sand
- Trilingual: English, French, Arabic (with RTL support)
- Splash screen with Bismillah
- Sidebar help panel with FAQ, How-To, and Wiki tabs
- Web Audio API sound effects (no audio files)
- High score persistence via localStorage
- Theme/language/sound preferences persisted

## Lesson

Reflection and contemplation were central to the Prophet's spiritual life before and after revelation.
