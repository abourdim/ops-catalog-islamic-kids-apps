What is this?

An interactive letter-writing game where you compose diplomatic letters as the Prophet ﷺ did to kings and rulers, choosing the right phrases.

Are these real letters?

Yes! The Prophet ﷺ sent letters to rulers including Heraclius of Byzantium, Negus of Abyssinia, and Muqawqis of Egypt.
