What is this?

A simulation where you perform acts of kindness inspired by the Prophet ﷺ and watch the community happiness meter rise. Each scenario is based on real stories from the seerah.

1. Read each scenario. 2. Choose the kindest response. 3. Watch community happiness grow. 4. Reach 100% to complete the simulation.
The Prophet ﷺ said: 'The most beloved people to Allah are those who are most beneficial to people.' Every small kindness matters.
