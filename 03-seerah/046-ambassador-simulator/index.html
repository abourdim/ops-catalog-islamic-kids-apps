What is this?

A diplomatic simulation where you represent the Prophet ﷺ in negotiations with various tribes and nations, choosing responses that embody Islamic values.

1. Read the diplomatic scenario. 2. Choose your response wisely. 3. Best responses build peace, trust, and open doors to da'wah. 4. Complete all 4 missions.
The Prophet ﷺ was the greatest diplomat. He used wisdom, patience, and respect to build alliances and spread the message of Islam peacefully.
