❓ Help
What is this app?

A word-by-word Arabic reading tool. Each sentence is shown in Arabic with tap-to-learn vocabulary.

Who is this for?

Muslim kids beginning to read Arabic beyond Quran recitation into real-world comprehension.

How do I use it?

Tap any word in the sentence to see its translation, transliteration, and grammar info.

Are these real sentences?

Yes. Sentences are based on Islamic contexts like daily life, mosque, family, and nature.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Everything runs in your browser with no internet needed after loading.

Arabic Reading

Learning to read Arabic involves recognizing letter forms, connecting words, and understanding sentence structure.

Word-by-Word Method

Islamic Context

All sentences use Islamic-friendly contexts: family, mosque, nature, kindness, and daily Muslim life.
