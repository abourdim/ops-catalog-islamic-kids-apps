❓ Help
What is this app?

An interactive game to learn proper Islamic greetings for different situations, based on the Sunnah of the Prophet ﷺ.

How many scenarios are there?

10 unique greeting scenarios covering meeting people, entering homes, phone calls, and more.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once loaded, everything runs in your browser with no internet needed.

How do I change the theme?

Click any theme button in the controls bar.

How do I change the language?

Click EN / FR / ع. Arabic enables right-to-left layout.

🤝 As-Salamu Alaykum

The standard Islamic greeting meaning "Peace be upon you." The Prophet ﷺ encouraged Muslims to spread Salam widely.

🏠 Entering the Home

Muslims greet with Salam when entering any home, even their own, as taught in the Quran (24:61).

🌟 The Best Greeting

The longer the Salam, the more reward: "As-Salamu Alaykum wa Rahmatullahi wa Barakatuh" is the most complete form.
