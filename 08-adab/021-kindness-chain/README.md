# Kindness Chain — Chain Reaction Simulator

Interactive chain reaction game where one act of kindness triggers another. Watch the chain grow across the canvas.

## Concept

Click to place acts of kindness on a canvas full of people. Each act inspires nearby people, creating a growing chain reaction. The Prophet ﷺ said: "Every act of kindness is charity" (Sahih Bukhari).

## Features

- Single HTML file, zero dependencies, fully offline
- Canvas-based chain reaction animation with 60 interactive nodes
- 6 kindness types: Smile, Help, Share, Listen, Forgive, Pray
- Chain propagation with generation tracking
- 8 CSS themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Arctic, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Sand
- Trilingual: English, French, Arabic (with RTL support)
- Splash screen with Bismillah
- Sidebar help panel with FAQ, How-To, and Wiki tabs
- Web Audio API sound effects (no audio files)
- High score persistence via localStorage
- Theme/language/sound preferences persisted

## Lesson

Kindness is contagious. One small act of goodness can inspire a chain reaction that touches many lives. In Islam, even a smile is charity.
