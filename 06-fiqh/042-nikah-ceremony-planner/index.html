What is a nikah?

The Islamic marriage contract — a sacred agreement between bride and groom.

What are the key elements?

Consent, wali (guardian), mahr (dowry), witnesses, and the aqd (contract).

Is this a complete guide?

This covers the main steps. Details vary by culture and school of thought.

The Islamic marriage contract is a solemn covenant recognized as an act of worship.

The obligatory gift from the groom to the bride. It is her right and property.

The wedding feast hosted after the marriage. The Prophet ﷺ encouraged it even if with just one sheep.
