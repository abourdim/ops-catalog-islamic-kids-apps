What is this?

An animated wudu (ablution) simulator with canvas water physics. Learn the correct order and method of wudu.

Is this accurate?

Yes, the steps follow the Sunnah method of performing wudu as taught by the Prophet (peace be upon him).

Does it work offline?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser.

Complete all 7 steps to finish your wudu

💧 Wudu

Wudu (ablution) is the Islamic ritual purification performed before prayer. It involves washing specific body parts in a prescribed order.

🌊 Water Conservation

The Prophet (peace be upon him) used to perform wudu with one mudd of water (about 750mL). He taught us not to waste water even at a river.
