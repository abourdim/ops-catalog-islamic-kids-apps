# Wudu Flow Simulator

Interactive animated wudu (ablution) simulator with canvas water physics. Learn each step of the Islamic ritual purification with flowing water effects.

## Concept

Wudu is the Islamic ritual of purification performed before prayer. This simulator guides children through the correct sequence of washing, with animated water particle effects on a canvas body diagram. Each step must be performed in the correct order.

## The 9 Steps

| # | Step | Arabic | Water (mL) |
|---|------|--------|------------|
| 1 | Niyyah (Intention) | النية | 0 |
| 2 | Wash Hands (3x) | غسل اليدين | 120 |
| 3 | Rinse Mouth (3x) | المضمضة | 100 |
| 4 | Clean Nose (3x) | الاستنشاق | 80 |
| 5 | Wash Face (3x) | غسل الوجه | 200 |
| 6 | Wash Arms (3x) | غسل اليدين إلى المرفقين | 250 |
| 7 | Wipe Head | مسح الرأس | 50 |
| 8 | Clean Ears | مسح الأذنين | 30 |
| 9 | Wash Feet (3x) | غسل القدمين | 200 |

## Features

- Single HTML file, zero dependencies, fully offline
- Canvas-based body diagram with water particle physics
- 8 CSS themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Arctic, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Sand
- Trilingual: English, French, Arabic (with RTL support)
- Splash screen with Bismillah
- Sidebar help panel with FAQ, How-To, and Wiki tabs
- Web Audio API sound effects (no audio files)
- Water usage tracking
- High score persistence via localStorage

## Lesson

Wudu purifies both body and soul. The Prophet (peace be upon him) taught us to perform wudu carefully while conserving water — even when at a flowing river.
