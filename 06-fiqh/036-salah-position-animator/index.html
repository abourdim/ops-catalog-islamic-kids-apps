What positions are shown?

All main salah positions: Qiyam, Ruku, I'tidal, Sujud, Juloos, and Tasleem.

Can I slow it down?

Yes! Use the speed slider to control animation speed.

What is Loop mode?

Loop mode repeats the current position instead of advancing to the next one.

🕌 Salah

The five daily prayers are the second pillar of Islam, performed at Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, and Isha.

🧎 Sujud

Prostration — the closest a servant is to Allah. Place forehead, nose, palms, knees, and toes on the ground.

📐 Positions

Each position has specific postures and supplications that form the physical worship of salah.
