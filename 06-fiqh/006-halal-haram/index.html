❓ Help
What is Halal or Haram?

An Islamic sorting game. A food, drink, action, or behavior appears and you decide whether it is Halal (permissible) or Haram (forbidden) in Islam.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once loaded, everything runs in your browser with no internet needed.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Works on any device with a modern browser. You can swipe left for Haram and right for Halal, or use the buttons.

How do I change the theme?

Click any theme button in the controls bar.

How do I change the language?

Click EN / FR / ع. Arabic enables right-to-left layout automatically.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser. No data is sent anywhere.

What are the difficulty levels?

Easy (10 items): obvious foods and actions. Medium (15 items): includes trickier items. Hard (20 items): edge cases with detailed explanations.

Purpose

Teach Muslim kids the basics of Halal and Haram through an interactive sorting game with Islamic references.

Themes

8 built-in themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Arctic, Sand.

Languages

Trilingual: English, Français, العربية. Arabic auto-enables RTL layout.

Sound

Web Audio API — no files needed. Correct = rising tone, wrong = buzz, win = melody. Toggle with the sound button.

Controls

Keyboard: H = Haram, L = Halal, Enter = New Game. Touch: swipe left = Haram, swipe right = Halal.

Privacy

Local-first. No tracking, no analytics, no external calls. All data stays in your browser.
