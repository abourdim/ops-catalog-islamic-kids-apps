What is Zakat Calculator?

An interactive tool to learn about Zakat, the third pillar of Islam. Calculate Zakat on your savings and test your knowledge with quizzes.

What is Zakat?

Zakat is obligatory charity — one of the five pillars of Islam. Muslims with wealth above Nisab must give 2.5% annually to those in need.

What is Nisab?

Nisab is the minimum wealth threshold. It equals 85 grams of gold or 595 grams of silver. If your savings exceed this for one lunar year, Zakat is due.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once loaded, everything runs in your browser with no internet needed.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Works on any device with a modern browser.

How do I change the theme?

Click any theme button in the controls bar.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally. No data is sent anywhere.
