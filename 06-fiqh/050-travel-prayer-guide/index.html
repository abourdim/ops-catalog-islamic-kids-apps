When can I shorten prayers?

Most scholars say when traveling 80+ km (about 48 miles) from your city.

Which prayers are shortened?

Dhuhr, Asr, and Isha are shortened from 4 to 2 rakat. Fajr and Maghrib stay the same.

Can I combine prayers?

Yes, Dhuhr+Asr can be combined, and Maghrib+Isha can be combined during travel.

✈️ Musafir

A traveler in Islamic law who qualifies for prayer concessions based on distance and duration.

📏 Distance

Most scholars set the minimum at about 80 km (the distance of two marhalah/stages of travel).

⏱ Duration

If staying more than 4 days at one location, most scholars say you should pray in full.
