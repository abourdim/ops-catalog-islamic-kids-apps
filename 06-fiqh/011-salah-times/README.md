# Salah Times — Learn the 5 Prayer Times

Interactive app to learn about the 5 daily prayer times with an animated sun position indicator.

## Concept

Muslims pray 5 times daily at specific times linked to the sun's position. This app teaches children when each prayer occurs, its Arabic name, the number of rakaat, and its significance.

## The 5 Prayers

| # | Prayer | Arabic | Time | Rakaat |
|---|--------|--------|------|--------|
| 1 | Fajr | الفجر | Before sunrise | 2 |
| 2 | Dhuhr | الظهر | After midday | 4 |
| 3 | Asr | العصر | Afternoon | 4 |
| 4 | Maghrib | المغرب | After sunset | 3 |
| 5 | Isha | العشاء | Night | 4 |

## Features

- Single HTML file, zero dependencies, fully offline
- 8 CSS themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Arctic, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Sand
- Trilingual: English, French, Arabic (with RTL support)
- Splash screen with Bismillah
- Sidebar help panel with FAQ, How-To, and Wiki tabs
- Web Audio API sound effects (no audio files)
- Interactive sun position animation
- Quiz mode to test knowledge
- High score persistence via localStorage

## Lesson

Salah is the second pillar of Islam. It structures the Muslim's day around remembrance of Allah, from dawn to night.
