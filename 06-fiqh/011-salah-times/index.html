What is this app?

Learn the 5 daily prayer times and their relation to the sun's position.

🕌 Salah

The second pillar of Islam. Muslims pray 5 times daily.

🌞 Sun Position

Each prayer is tied to the sun: Fajr (dawn), Dhuhr (noon), Asr (afternoon), Maghrib (sunset), Isha (night).
