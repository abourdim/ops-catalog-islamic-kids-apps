What is janazah prayer?

A special prayer performed for a deceased Muslim. It is a communal obligation (fard kifayah).

How many takbeers?

Four takbeers, each followed by specific recitations and duas.

Does it have ruku or sujud?

No. Janazah prayer is performed entirely while standing.

🕌 Janazah Prayer

A communal obligation — if some Muslims perform it, the obligation is lifted from the rest.

📿 Four Takbeers

1st: Al-Fatiha, 2nd: Salawat, 3rd: Dua for deceased, 4th: Dua and salam.

🤲 Intention

The intention specifies the prayer is for the deceased person being prayed over.
