# Trade Ethics

Islamic rules of fair trade and business ethics — scenario game.

## Concept

Scenario-based quiz: hiding defects (haram), riba/interest (haram), fair weighing, and mutual consent in transactions.

## Features

- Single HTML file, zero dependencies, fully offline
- 8 CSS themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Arctic, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Sand
- Trilingual: English, French, Arabic (with RTL support)
- Splash screen with Bismillah
- Sidebar help panel with FAQ, How-To, and Wiki tabs
- Web Audio API sound effects (no audio files)
- Scenario-based ethics quiz
- High score persistence via localStorage

## Lesson

Islam established ethical business practices 1400 years ago. Honesty, fairness, and mutual consent are the foundations of Islamic commerce.
