❓ Help
What is Wudu Steps?

An interactive trainer to learn the correct order of wudu (ablution) before prayer. Tap the steps in order!

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once loaded, everything runs in your browser with no internet needed.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Works on any device with a modern browser — phone, tablet, laptop, desktop.

How do I change the theme?

Click any theme button in the controls bar.

How do I change the language?

Click EN / FR / ع. Arabic enables right-to-left layout automatically.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser. No data is sent anywhere.

What are the difficulty levels?

Easy = 4 steps, Medium = 6 steps, Hard = all 8 steps of wudu.

🎯 Purpose

Teach Muslim children the correct order and method of wudu (ritual ablution) performed before salah (prayer).

💦 What is Wudu?

Wudu is the Islamic ritual purification using water. It is required before performing salah. The Prophet (peace be upon him) taught us the steps in a specific order.

🎨 Themes

8 built-in themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Arctic, Sand.

🌐 Languages

Trilingual: English, Français, العربية. Arabic auto-enables RTL layout.

🔊 Sound

Web Audio API — no files needed. Correct = rising tone, wrong = buzz, win = melody. Toggle with sound button.

🔒 Privacy

Local-first. No tracking, no analytics, no external calls. All data stays in your browser.
