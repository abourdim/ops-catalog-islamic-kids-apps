🥗 Halal Food

Food that is permissible under Islamic law. Includes most vegetables, fruits, grains, and properly slaughtered meat.

⚠️ Doubtful (Mushbooh)

Foods with uncertain status. The Prophet ﷺ taught us to avoid doubtful matters.

🍖 Dhabiha

Islamic method of slaughtering animals with a sharp blade, saying Bismillah, and draining the blood.
