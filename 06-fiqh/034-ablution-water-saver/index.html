❓ Help
How do I play?

Hold the tap button to flow water. Release when you think you have enough for each wudu step.

What is the goal?

Use as little water as possible while washing enough for each step.

What did the Prophet use?

The Prophet (peace be upon him) typically used about 750mL — roughly one mudd.

💧 Water in Islam

Islam teaches us not to waste water even when performing wudu at a flowing river.

📏 One Mudd

The Prophet used about one mudd (750mL) for wudu — a remarkable example of conservation.

🌍 Conservation

Water conservation is an Islamic duty. Being wasteful (israf) is forbidden.
