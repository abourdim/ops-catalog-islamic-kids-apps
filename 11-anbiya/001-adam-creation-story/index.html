What is this app?

An interactive story about how Allah created Prophet Adam (peace be upon him), based on the Quran.

Is it historically accurate?

Yes, all scenes are based on Quranic verses and authentic Islamic sources.

Works offline?

Yes, fully self-contained in your browser.

Yes, responsive design works on all devices.

🕌 Adam in the Quran

Prophet Adam (peace be upon him) is mentioned in multiple surahs including Al-Baqarah, Al-A'raf, Al-Hijr, and Ta-Ha.

🌍 Creation from Clay

Allah created Adam from clay (teen), then shaped him and breathed His spirit into him.

👼 Angels' Prostration

Allah commanded all angels to prostrate before Adam. All obeyed except Iblis.

🔥 Iblis's Refusal

Iblis refused out of arrogance, claiming fire is superior to clay. He was expelled.

🌳 The Forbidden Tree

Adam and Hawwa were told not to approach one tree, but Shaytan deceived them.

🤲 Repentance

Adam and Hawwa sincerely repented: 'Our Lord, we have wronged ourselves...' (7:23)
