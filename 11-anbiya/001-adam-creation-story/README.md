# The Creation of Adam

Interactive story of Prophet Adam's creation — from clay, the angels' prostration, Iblis's refusal, life in Jannah, and descent to Earth.

## Concept

An interactive, scene-by-scene narrative that takes children through the Quranic account of Adam's creation. Each scene includes Quran references, Arabic text, and vivid descriptions. Children navigate through the story at their own pace, learning about the first human, the honor Allah gave him, and the lessons of humility and repentance.

## Features

- Single HTML file, zero dependencies, fully offline
- 8 CSS themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Arctic, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Sand
- Trilingual: English, French, Arabic (with RTL support)
- Splash screen with Bismillah
- Sidebar help panel with FAQ, How-To, and Wiki tabs
- Web Audio API sound effects (no audio files)
- localStorage persistence for preferences
- 10 interactive scenes with Quran references
- Arabic Quranic verses displayed
- Visual scenes with animated elements
- Progress tracking through the story

## Lesson

Allah created humans with honor and knowledge. Even after making a mistake, sincere repentance (tawbah) is always accepted. Arrogance (like Iblis) leads to ruin, while humility leads to Allah's mercy.
