🎯 Purpose

Learn about the Ghazwah of Banu Mustaliq (6 AH) — a swift raid that showcases strategic planning, decisive action, and mercy in victory.

📜 Historical Context

The Prophet ﷺ received intelligence that Banu Mustaliq, led by al-Harith ibn Abi Dirar, were gathering forces near Al-Muraysi well to attack Medina. The Prophet ﷺ moved swiftly and surprised them.

💒 Juwayriyah bint al-Harith

The chief's daughter embraced Islam and married the Prophet ﷺ. The Companions then freed all captives, saying they were now relatives of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ. Over 100 families were freed.

📖 Lessons

Swift decisive action prevents greater conflict. Mercy in victory wins hearts. One righteous marriage freed an entire tribe. Diplomacy and compassion are powerful tools.
