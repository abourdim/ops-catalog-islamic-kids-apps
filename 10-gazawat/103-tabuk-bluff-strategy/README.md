# Tabuk Bluff Strategy — Show of Force

Interactive strategy game about the Prophet's (peace be upon him) Tabuk expedition (9 AH / 630 CE), where 30,000 Muslim soldiers caused the Byzantine forces to retreat without fighting.

## Concept

The Tabuk expedition was the largest Muslim military campaign. Reports of Byzantine forces massing in Syria led the Prophet to assemble 30,000 men — called Jaysh al-Usra (Army of Hardship) — during extreme summer heat. The player manages mobilization, logistics, diplomacy, and the show of force.

## The 8 Phases

| # | Phase | Description | Key Lesson |
|---|-------|-------------|------------|
| 1 | Mobilization Challenge | Motivate army during heat and harvest | Transparency builds commitment |
| 2 | Hypocrites' Excuses | Handle those who refuse to march | Actions reveal true character |
| 3 | Desert March Logistics | Manage supplies for 30,000 in the desert | Spiritual and practical leadership |
| 4 | Camp at Tabuk | Decide how long to stay | Diplomacy over battle |
| 5 | Dumatul-Jandal | Handle a hostile northern fortress | Surgical precision, not brute force |
| 6 | Treaty Terms | Set fair treaty conditions | Coexistence creates stability |
| 7 | Return to Medina | Organize the return march | Establish lasting presence |
| 8 | Lesson of Tabuk | Reflect on the expedition's meaning | Strength serves peace |

## Features

- Single HTML file, zero dependencies, fully offline
- 8 CSS themes with canvas map and force stats (Army/Morale/Diplomacy)
- Trilingual: English, French, Arabic (with RTL support)
- Splash screen with Bismillah, sidebar help panel
- Web Audio API sound effects, localStorage persistence

## Lesson

Overwhelming preparation and deterrence can achieve peace without bloodshed. True victory is when no blood is shed.
