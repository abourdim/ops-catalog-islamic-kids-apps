What is this game?

A strategy game about the Tabuk expedition (9 AH / 630 CE), the largest Muslim military campaign during the Prophet's ﷺ lifetime. 30,000 soldiers marched north and the Byzantines retreated without fighting.

Why is it called a bluff?

The massive show of force made the Byzantine army retreat. Victory was achieved through deterrence, not bloodshed — the ultimate strategic success.

What are the stats?

Army size, Morale, and Diplomacy affect your success. Good decisions improve them.

The Tabuk Expedition

In 9 AH (630 CE), reports reached the Prophet ﷺ that the Byzantine Empire was massing forces in Syria. He assembled the largest Muslim army — 30,000 men — and marched north.

The Army of Hardship

Called Jaysh al-Usra (Army of Hardship) because it was organized during extreme summer heat and famine. The Companions donated generously to fund it.

Outcome

The Byzantines retreated without engagement. The Prophet ﷺ secured treaties with northern tribes, establishing Muslim influence in the region.
