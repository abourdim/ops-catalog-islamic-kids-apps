❓ Help
What is Water Wars?

A canvas-based strategy game where you control a Muslim army competing for desert water sources. Secure wells, guard them, and deny water to the enemy. Inspired by the strategy at Badr.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once loaded, everything runs in your browser with no internet needed.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Works on any device with a modern browser — phone, tablet, laptop, desktop. Tap to select and act.

How do I change the theme?

Click any theme button (🖥 🌌 🔥 etc.) in the controls bar.

How do I change the language?

Click EN / FR / ع. Arabic enables right-to-left layout automatically.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser. No data is sent anywhere.

🎯 Purpose

Learn desert warfare strategy through water resource control. Based on the historical wells strategy at Badr, where the Muslim army secured water sources before battle.

💧 Water Sources

Wells (single point), Oases (larger area, more water), Rivers (long source, hardest to defend). Each provides water per turn to whoever controls it.

⚔️ Units

Each unit has health and attack. Without water, units lose health each turn. Guarding a well makes it harder for enemies to capture.

🎨 Themes

8 built-in themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Arctic, Sand.

🌐 Languages

Trilingual: English, Francais, al-Arabiya. Arabic auto-enables RTL layout.

🔊 Sound

Web Audio API — no files needed. Toggle with the sound button.

📜 Historical Reference

At Badr, the Prophet's companion al-Hubab ibn al-Mundhir advised positioning the army near the wells and filling/blocking the rest to deny water to the Quraysh. This strategic water control was key to victory.

🔒 Privacy

Local-first. No tracking, no analytics, no external calls. All data stays in your browser.
