What is Battlefield Medic?

A triage game inspired by Rufaydah al-Aslamiyyah, the first Muslim nurse. Treat wounded soldiers on the battlefield by prioritizing the most critical cases first.

How do I triage?

Click on a wounded soldier to select them. Critical (red) patients should be treated first — they lose health fastest. Apply the correct treatment, then transport to the hospital tent.

What treatments are available?

Bandage (for bleeding wounds), Splint (for broken bones), and Herbal medicine (for fever/infection). Each patient needs a specific treatment shown by their icon.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once loaded, everything runs in your browser with no internet needed.

How do I change the theme?

Click any theme button in the controls bar.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser. No data is sent anywhere.

Rufaydah al-Aslamiyyah

Rufaydah bint Sa'ad al-Aslamiyyah was the first female Muslim nurse. She set up field hospital tents near the battlefield during the time of Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him) and treated wounded soldiers with herbal remedies and bandaging techniques. She is considered a pioneer of Islamic medicine and nursing.

Triage System

Triage means sorting patients by severity. Critical (red) = life-threatening, treat immediately. Moderate (yellow) = serious but stable for a short time. Light (green) = minor injuries, can wait. Good medics prioritize the most critical cases first.

Themes

8 built-in themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Arctic, Sand.

Languages

Trilingual: English, Francais, al-Arabiyyah. Arabic auto-enables RTL layout.

Sound

Web Audio API — no files needed. Toggle with the speaker button.

Privacy

Local-first. No tracking, no analytics, no external calls. All data stays in your browser.
