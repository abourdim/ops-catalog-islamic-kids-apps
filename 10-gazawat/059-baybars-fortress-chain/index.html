❓ Help
What is Fortress Chain?

A strategy game where you build Sultan Baybars' legendary defense network from Egypt to Syria. Place fortresses, connect them with roads, and set up pigeon post communications to defend against Crusaders and Mongols.

How do I build?

Select a tool from the toolbar, then click on highlighted locations on the map. Forts go on city dots, roads connect two forts, pigeon posts link forts for communication, and garrisons add troops.

Why can't I build everywhere?

Your budget is limited! Just like the real Sultan Baybars, you must choose strategically. You can only fortify 6-8 of the 10 locations depending on difficulty.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once loaded, everything runs in your browser with no internet needed.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Works on any device with a modern browser. Touch controls are supported.

How is the score calculated?

Chain completeness (connected forts), territory coverage (spread of forts), and communication speed (pigeon network). All three combine for the final score.

🏰 Sultan Baybars

Al-Zahir Baybars (1223-1277) was a Mamluk Sultan of Egypt and Syria. He built a legendary network of fortresses and communication posts that defended the Muslim world against both Crusader and Mongol invasions for centuries.

🕊 Pigeon Post

Baybars perfected the barid (postal system) using carrier pigeons. Messages could travel from Damascus to Cairo in a single day — faster than any horse messenger. This gave the Mamluks a massive strategic advantage.

🛡 The Defense Chain

Key fortresses included Cairo, Gaza, Kerak, Safad, Damascus, Aleppo, and many others. Each fortress protected trade routes and could reinforce its neighbors, creating an interconnected defense network.
