What is Cavalry vs Infantry?

A tactical battle simulator. Choose your army type (cavalry-heavy or infantry-heavy), formation, and terrain, then watch how the battle plays out. Learn which combinations win.

How does terrain affect battle?

Open plains favor cavalry charges. Hills and forests favor infantry defense. Rivers slow cavalry. Each terrain modifies attack and defense values differently.

How do formations work?

Wedge gives attack bonus to cavalry. Shield wall gives defense bonus to infantry. Crescent formation provides flanking bonuses.

🐎 Muslim Cavalry

Khalid ibn al-Walid was called the Sword of Allah for his cavalry mastery. At Yarmouk, his mobile cavalry reserve turned the battle by flanking the Byzantine army.

🛡 Infantry Tactics

At Badr, the Muslim infantry held a disciplined line. The Prophet (peace be upon him) arranged them in rows, showing that even a smaller force could prevail with good formation.

🌙 Crescent Formation

Khalid ibn al-Walid used a crescent (hilal) formation at several battles, allowing the wings to envelop enemy flanks while the center held firm.
