What is Qadisiyyah Artillery?

A canvas-based artillery game where you command Muslim forces at the Battle of Qadisiyyah (636 CE). Position catapults and archers to defeat the Persian war elephants, just as Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas directed the battle from a building while ill.

How do I defeat the elephants?

Use catapults to hit elephants directly with boulders, and switch to archers to target the drivers sitting on top. Historically, the Muslims discovered that targeting the mahouts (drivers) was far more effective than attacking the elephants themselves.

What are the two modes?

Catapult mode launches boulders that damage elephants on direct hit. Archer mode fires arrows that target the drivers — if you hit the driver zone (top of elephant), the elephant becomes uncontrolled and flees!

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once loaded, everything runs in your browser with no internet needed.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Works on any device with a modern browser. Use the angle/power sliders and tap FIRE.

How do I change the theme?

Click any theme button in the controls bar.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser. No data is sent anywhere.

Battle of Qadisiyyah (636 CE)

A decisive battle between the Muslim forces led by Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas and the Sassanid Persian army. The Persian army deployed war elephants which initially terrified the Muslim cavalry.

Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas

One of the ten companions promised Paradise. He was ill during the battle and commanded the army from a building overlooking the battlefield. Despite his illness, his strategic commands led to a decisive Muslim victory.

The Elephant Tactic

The Muslims discovered that targeting the mahouts (elephant drivers) was the key. Without their drivers, the elephants became uncontrollable and fled the battlefield, turning the tide of battle.

Themes

8 built-in themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Arctic, Sand.

Languages

Trilingual: English, Francais, Al-Arabiyya. Arabic auto-enables RTL layout.

Sound

Web Audio API — no files needed. Toggle with the speaker button.

Privacy

Local-first. No tracking, no analytics, no external calls. All data stays in your browser.
