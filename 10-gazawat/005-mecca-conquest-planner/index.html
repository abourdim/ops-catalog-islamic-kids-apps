What is this game?

A strategic planner game about the peaceful conquest of Mecca (Fath Makkah) in 8 AH / 630 CE. Assign commanders, divide troops, and witness the mercy of the Prophet ﷺ.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once loaded, everything runs in your browser with no internet needed.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Works on any device with a modern browser — phone, tablet, laptop, desktop.

How do I change the theme?

Click any theme button (🖥 🌌 🔥 etc.) in the controls bar.

How do I change the language?

Click EN / FR / ع. Arabic enables right-to-left layout automatically.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser. No data is sent anywhere.

🕋 The Event

The Conquest of Mecca (Fath Makkah) took place in Ramadan 8 AH (January 630 CE). The Prophet ﷺ marched with 10,000 companions and entered Mecca almost entirely without bloodshed.

⚔️ The Commanders

Khalid ibn al-Walid (south), Zubayr ibn al-Awwam (north), Abu Ubaidah ibn al-Jarrah (west), Sa'd ibn Ubadah (east) — each led a division through a different pass.

🕊️ The Order

"Do not fight unless you are attacked." This was the standing order — a conquest of peace, not war.

🏛️ Idol Removal

360 idols around the Kaaba were removed, restoring it to pure monotheism as built by Ibrahim (AS). The Prophet ﷺ recited: "Truth has come, and falsehood has departed."

🤝 General Amnesty

The Prophet ﷺ declared: "Go, you are free." Even those who had persecuted Muslims for years were forgiven — a supreme act of mercy.

🔒 Privacy

Local-first. No tracking, no analytics, no external calls. All data stays in your browser.
