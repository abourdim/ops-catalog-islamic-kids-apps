What is this game?

An interactive encyclopedia and quiz about weapons and armor from the early Islamic period. Learn about swords, shields, bows, and armor, then identify them from descriptions.

Are these real historical items?

Yes. All items are historically documented weapons and armor used during the Prophet's time and the early Islamic period.

⚖ The Prophet's Swords

The Prophet owned several swords including Dhul-Fiqar (given to Ali), al-Battar, and Qal'i. These were double-edged Arabian swords.

🛡 Islamic Armor

Chain mail (dir') was the primary armor. The Prophet had several coats of mail including Dhat al-Fudul and al-Sa'diyyah.
