❓ Help
What is this game?

A canvas strategy game about Sultan Mehmed II's historic conquest of Constantinople in 1453 CE. Play through 5 phases of the siege!

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once loaded, everything runs in your browser with no internet needed.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Works on any device with a modern browser. Touch controls are supported.

How do I change the theme?

Click any theme button in the controls bar.

How do I change the language?

Click EN / FR / ع. Arabic enables right-to-left layout automatically.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser. No data is sent anywhere.

What are the 5 phases?

1) Position the Orban Cannon. 2) Move ships over land past the Golden Horn chain. 3) Position land and sea forces. 4) Final assault — breach the walls. 5) Mehmed enters the city, prays at Hagia Sophia, declares mercy.

🎯 Purpose

Learn about the conquest of Constantinople — a prophesied event in Islamic history. Understand the military innovation and mercy shown by Sultan Mehmed II.

📖 The Hadith

"You will conquer Constantinople. What a wonderful commander and army that will be." — Prophet Muhammad ﷺ

🏰 Historical Context

Constantinople (modern Istanbul) was the capital of the Byzantine Empire. Sultan Mehmed II besieged it for 53 days in 1453 CE. He used innovative tactics including moving ships overland on greased logs.

🎨 Themes

8 built-in themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Arctic, Sand.

🌐 Languages

Trilingual: English, Francais, العربية. Arabic auto-enables RTL layout.

🔊 Sound

Web Audio API — no files needed. Sound effects for cannon fire, ships, victory.

🔒 Privacy

Local-first. No tracking, no analytics, no external calls. All data stays in your browser.
