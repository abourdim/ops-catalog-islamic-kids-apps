What is Camel Caravan Loader?

A logistics puzzle where you load supply camels for a military campaign. Balance food, water, weapons, medicine, and tools within the camel's weight capacity.

Why camels?

Camels were essential to desert military campaigns. They could carry heavy loads across vast distances. The Prophet (peace be upon him) used camel caravans for all major expeditions.

How is the score calculated?

Score depends on having enough of each essential supply, staying within weight, and matching the mission requirements. Missing essentials lowers your score significantly.

Camel in Islam

The Quran mentions camels as signs of Allah's creation (88:17). They were vital for trade, travel, and military logistics in early Islamic history.

Military Logistics

The Prophet (peace be upon him) carefully planned supplies for every expedition. At Badr, despite limited resources, he ensured water and provisions were distributed fairly.

Desert Travel

A camel can carry up to 200kg and travel 40km/day in desert conditions. Proper loading and balanced supplies were critical for survival on long campaigns.
