What is Khandaq Supply Line?

A resource management game set during the Battle of the Trench (Khandaq). Manage food, water, firewood, and morale to survive the 27-day siege of Medina.

How do I win?

Survive all 27 days without any resource hitting zero. On the final day, Allah sends a divine wind to scatter the enemy (Quran 33:9).

How do I lose?

If any resource (Food, Water, Firewood, Morale, or Trench integrity) drops to zero, the siege is lost.

What are the difficulty levels?

Easy: resources drain slower, more starting supplies. Normal: balanced. Hard: faster drain, tougher events.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once loaded, everything runs in your browser with no internet needed.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser. No data is sent anywhere.

🏰 Battle of the Trench

In 5 AH, a coalition of 10,000 soldiers besieged Medina. The Prophet (peace be upon him) ordered a trench dug on the northern approach — an idea from Salman al-Farisi. 3,000 Muslims defended behind it for nearly a month.

🌾 Resources

Food (dates and grain), Water, Firewood (for warmth and cooking), Morale (the spirit of the defenders), and Trench integrity (must be maintained against collapse and enemy probes).

🕊 Nu'aym ibn Mas'ud

A secret Muslim among the enemy who offered to sow discord between Quraysh and Banu Qurayza. His clever diplomacy was a turning point — weakening enemy unity from within.

🌬 The Divine Wind

Allah sent a fierce wind and unseen forces that scattered the enemy camp, extinguished their fires, and overturned their tents (Quran 33:9). The coalition retreated in disarray.

📖 Lesson

Patience (sabr) under hardship, trust in Allah (tawakkul), unity of the community, and clever strategy can overcome overwhelming odds.

🎨 Themes

8 built-in themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Arctic, Sand.

🔊 Sound

Web Audio API — no files needed. Toggle with the speaker button.

🔒 Privacy

Local-first. No tracking, no analytics, no external calls. All data stays in your browser.
