❓ Help
What is Treaty Workshop?

An interactive game where you draft treaty clauses for historical Islamic scenarios. Learn about diplomacy, justice, and peaceful agreements from the Prophet's era.

How is my treaty scored?

Each treaty is scored on three criteria: Fairness (are the terms balanced?), Completeness (did you address all clauses?), and Historical Accuracy (how close to the real treaty?).

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once loaded, everything runs in your browser with no internet needed.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Works on any device with a modern browser.

How do I change the theme?

Click any theme button (🖥 🌌 🔥 etc.) in the controls bar.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally. No data is sent anywhere.

🎯 Purpose

Learn about Islamic diplomacy and treaty-making through interactive scenarios based on real historical agreements from the Prophet's era and early Islamic period.

📜 Historical Treaties

Scenarios are inspired by: The Constitution of Medina, the Treaty of Hudaybiyyah, tribal peace agreements, trade pacts, and inter-community covenants.

⚖️ Scoring

Fairness: Are terms balanced for both sides? Completeness: Did you fill every clause? Historical Accuracy: How close are your choices to the real treaty?

🎨 Themes

8 built-in themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Arctic, Sand.

🌐 Languages

Trilingual: English, Français, العربية. Arabic auto-enables RTL layout.

🔊 Sound

Web Audio API — no files needed. Toggle with 🔊 button.

🔒 Privacy

Local-first. No tracking, no analytics, no external calls. All data stays in your browser.
