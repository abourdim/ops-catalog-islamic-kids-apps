What is Salat al-Khawf?

The Fear Prayer — a special way of praying during battle or danger. The army splits into two groups that take turns praying while the other guards. Mentioned in Quran 4:102.

What was Dhat al-Riqa?

A military expedition in 4 AH where the Prophet first performed Salat al-Khawf. Named because the companions' feet were so blistered they wrapped them in rags (riqa).

Is this prayer still valid today?

Yes. Salat al-Khawf is a legitimate prayer method for Muslims in danger situations, showing Islam's emphasis that prayer should never be abandoned.

📖 Quran 4:102

"When you are among them and lead the prayer, let one group stand with you while the other guards. Then let the second group pray while the first guards."

🕊 Why It Matters

Salat al-Khawf shows that prayer is so important it is never abandoned — even in the middle of a battlefield. Allah provided a special method to maintain worship during danger.
