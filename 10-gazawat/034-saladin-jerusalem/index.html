What is this game?

A canvas strategy game about Saladin's liberation of Jerusalem in 1187 CE. Learn about the Battle of Hattin, Saladin's mercy, and the importance of justice.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once loaded, everything runs in your browser with no internet needed.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Works on any device with a modern browser. Touch or click to interact.

How do I change the theme?

Click any theme button in the controls bar.

How do I change the language?

Click EN / FR / ع. Arabic enables right-to-left layout automatically.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser. No data is sent anywhere.

Purpose

Learn about Saladin's liberation of Jerusalem through interactive gameplay. Understand mercy, chivalry, justice, and protecting holy sites.

Historical Context

In 1187 CE, Sultan Salahuddin Al-Ayyubi (Saladin) united the Muslim forces and defeated the Crusaders at the Battle of Hattin, then liberated Jerusalem after 88 years of Crusader occupation.

Themes

8 built-in themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Arctic, Sand.

Languages

Trilingual: English, Français, العربية. Arabic auto-enables RTL layout.

Sound

Web Audio API — no files needed. Toggle with the sound button.

Key Lesson

When the Crusaders captured Jerusalem in 1099, they massacred the inhabitants. When Saladin retook it in 1187, he let everyone leave safely and even paid ransom for the poor. Mercy conquers vengeance.

Privacy

Local-first. No tracking, no analytics, no external calls. All data stays in your browser.
