❓ Help
What is this game?

A strategy game about the Battle of Uhud (3 AH / 625 CE). You command the Muslim archers on the hill and experience the critical decision that changed the battle.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once loaded, everything runs in your browser with no internet needed.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Works on any device with a modern browser — phone, tablet, laptop, desktop.

How do I change the theme?

Click any theme button in the controls bar.

How do I change the language?

Click EN / FR / ع. Arabic enables right-to-left layout automatically.

What is the lesson?

The Prophet (peace be upon him) ordered 50 archers to hold the hill no matter what. When most left to collect spoils, Khalid ibn al-Walid's cavalry flanked the Muslims. The lesson: obedience to the leader and discipline even in victory.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser. No data is sent anywhere.

🏔 Battle of Uhud

Fought in 3 AH (625 CE) near Mount Uhud, north of Madinah, between the Muslims led by Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him) and the Quraysh of Makkah.

🏹 The Archers' Hill

The Prophet (peace be upon him) placed 50 archers on a strategic hill with orders: "Do not leave this position even if you see birds snatching our flesh." Their role was to protect the army's flank.

⚔ The Turning Point

When the Muslims began winning and the Quraysh retreated, most archers left the hill to collect spoils despite their orders. Khalid ibn al-Walid saw the gap and led a cavalry charge around the hill.

📖 The Lesson

Obedience to leadership and discipline in victory. Even when things look good, following the leader's orders is essential. The Quran addresses this in Surah Aal Imran (3:152).
