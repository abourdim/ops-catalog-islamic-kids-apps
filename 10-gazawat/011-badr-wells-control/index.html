❓ Help
What is Wells of Badr?

A strategy game where you control the wells at the Battle of Badr. Secure water for the Muslim army and block Quraysh from accessing it, following Hubab ibn al-Mundhir's advice.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once loaded, everything runs in your browser with no internet needed.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Works on any device with a modern browser — phone, tablet, laptop, desktop. Use touch to drag scouts.

How do I change the theme?

Click any theme button (🖥 🌌 🔥 etc.) in the controls bar.

How do I change the language?

Click EN / FR / ع. Arabic enables right-to-left layout automatically.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser. No data is sent anywhere.

🎯 Purpose

Learn about the Battle of Badr's strategic water control. Hubab ibn al-Mundhir advised the Prophet (peace be upon him) to move to the nearest well, secure it, and block the rest — a key factor in the Muslim victory.

📖 The Shura

Before the battle, Hubab asked: "Is this position from divine revelation, or is it strategy?" When told it was strategy, he advised moving to the wells. The Prophet (peace be upon him) accepted this advice — teaching us the value of consultation (shura).

🎨 Themes

8 built-in themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Arctic, Sand.

🌐 Languages

Trilingual: English, Français, العربية. Arabic auto-enables RTL layout.

🔊 Sound

Web Audio API — no files needed. Toggle with 🔊 button.

💡 Lessons

Shura (consultation), accepting good advice regardless of rank, strategic thinking, and resource control in conflict.

🔒 Privacy

Local-first. No tracking, no analytics, no external calls. All data stays in your browser.
