❓ Help
What is The Great Cannon?

A physics/engineering game about the conquest of Constantinople in 1453. Build Orban's cannon, position it, fire at the walls, move ships overland, and lead the final assault!

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once loaded, everything runs in your browser with no internet needed.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Works on any device with a modern browser. Touch and mouse both supported.

How do I change the theme?

Click any theme button in the controls bar.

How do I change the language?

Click EN / FR / ع. Arabic enables right-to-left layout automatically.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser. No data is sent anywhere.

How is the score calculated?

Score = accuracy percentage. Fewer shots to breach = higher score. Best score is saved as high score.

🎯 Purpose

Experience the historic conquest of Constantinople (1453 CE / 857 AH) through an interactive physics game. Learn about Orban's great cannon and Sultan Muhammad al-Fatih's brilliant strategies.

🏰 Historical Context

Sultan Muhammad al-Fatih fulfilled the prophecy of Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him) by conquering Constantinople at the age of 21. He commissioned the Hungarian engineer Orban to build the largest cannon ever made.

🚢 Ships Over Land

When the chain across the Golden Horn blocked the Ottoman fleet, Mehmed ordered ships to be dragged overland on greased logs behind the Genoese colony of Galata — one of history's greatest military maneuvers.

🎨 Themes

8 built-in themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Arctic, Sand.

🔊 Sound

Web Audio API — no files needed. Cannon fire, impacts, victory fanfare. Toggle with the sound button.

🔒 Privacy

Local-first. No tracking, no analytics, no external calls. All data stays in your browser.
