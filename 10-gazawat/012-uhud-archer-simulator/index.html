❓ Help
What is Archers of Uhud?

A canvas-based archer simulator set during the Battle of Uhud. You play as one of the 50 archers stationed on the hill by the Prophet (peace be upon him).

What is the lesson?

Obedience and discipline. The archers who left the hill to collect spoils caused a devastating reversal. Staying on the hill, even when tempted, is the key to victory.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once loaded, everything runs in your browser with no internet needed.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Tap on the battlefield to shoot arrows. Works on any device with a modern browser.

How do I change the theme?

Click any theme button in the controls bar.

How do I change the language?

Click EN / FR / ع. Arabic enables right-to-left layout automatically.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser. No data is sent anywhere.

🎯 Purpose

Teach the lesson of obedience from the Battle of Uhud. The Prophet (peace be upon him) stationed 50 archers on a hill with strict orders not to leave.

⚔ Battle of Uhud

Fought in 3 AH (625 CE) near Mount Uhud outside Madinah. The Muslim army initially had the upper hand, but when archers left their post to collect spoils, Khalid ibn al-Walid's cavalry flanked them.

🌈 Themes

8 built-in themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Arctic, Sand.

🌐 Languages

Trilingual: English, Français, العربية. Arabic auto-enables RTL layout.

🔊 Sound

Web Audio API — no files needed. Arrow shot, hit, victory, and defeat sounds. Toggle with the sound button.

🔒 Privacy

Local-first. No tracking, no analytics, no external calls. All data stays in your browser.
