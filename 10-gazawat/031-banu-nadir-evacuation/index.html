🎯 Purpose

Learn about the siege of Banu Nadir — how treachery led to consequences, how difficult military decisions were justified by revelation, and how the Prophet ﷺ cared for the Muhajirun refugees.

📖 Historical Context

In 4 AH (625 CE), Banu Nadir — a Jewish tribe in Madinah — plotted to assassinate Prophet Muhammad ﷺ by dropping a boulder on him. Allah warned His Prophet through revelation. The Muslims then besieged their fortresses for 15 days.

🌴 The Palm Tree Dilemma

During the siege, the Muslims cut some palm trees to pressure the surrender. This was controversial as destroying crops was generally forbidden. Quran 59:5 confirmed it was by Allah's permission.
