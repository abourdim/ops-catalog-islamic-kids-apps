# Coastal Battle — Naval-Land Warfare

Interactive canvas strategy game combining naval and land forces to defend a coastline, inspired by early Muslim naval campaigns.

## Concept

Command both warships and coastal defenses. Position ships at sea and ground forces on the beach to repel enemy invasion fleets. Inspired by the Battle of the Masts and early Muslim maritime campaigns during the caliphate of Uthman ibn Affan.

## Gameplay

- Place warships in the sea zone for long-range naval combat
- Deploy archers and infantry on the beach for coastal defense
- Build watchtowers that extend archer range by 40 units
- Projectile-based combat with visible shots flying between units
- Enemy ships approach from the sea toward the shore
- Survive 5 waves of increasing fleet sizes

## Features

- Single HTML file, zero dependencies, fully offline
- 8 CSS themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Arctic, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Sand
- Trilingual: English, French, Arabic (with RTL support)
- Splash screen with Bismillah
- Sidebar help panel with FAQ, How-To, and Wiki tabs
- Web Audio API sound effects (no audio files)
- High score persistence via localStorage
- Animated sea waves and projectile system
- Theme/language/sound preferences persisted

## Lesson

The development of Muslim naval power was a strategic necessity. From the first fleet under Mu'awiyah to the Ribat coastal fortresses, Muslims understood that guarding the coast was both a military duty and a spiritual act.
