What is Coastal Battle?

A naval-land combo strategy game where you coordinate ships and ground forces to defend a coastline against enemy invasion fleets.

How do ships work?

Warships patrol the sea zone and fire long-range projectiles at approaching enemy ships. They deal heavy damage but are expensive.

What are watchtowers?

Watchtowers are stationary coastal defenses with long range. They spot enemies early and provide bonus range to nearby archers.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Fully self-contained in your browser.

⛵ Muslim Naval History

The first Muslim naval campaign was during the caliphate of Uthman ibn Affan. Mu'awiyah built a fleet that achieved the historic Battle of the Masts (34 AH / 655 CE).

🌊 Battle of the Masts

This was the first major Muslim naval victory. The fleet tied their ships to the enemy's, turning the sea battle into hand-to-hand combat where Muslim soldiers excelled.

🏰 Coastal Fortifications

Ribat coastal fortresses were built along Muslim coastlines for defense and spiritual retreat. Guarding the coast was considered a form of worship.
