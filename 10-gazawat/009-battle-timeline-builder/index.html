❓ Help
What is Battle Timeline?

An interactive timeline builder where you drag battles and key events from the Prophet's time in Medina (622-632 CE) onto their correct positions on a timeline.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once loaded, everything runs in your browser with no internet needed.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Works on any device with a modern browser. On touch devices, tap and drag cards onto the timeline.

How do I change the theme?

Click any theme button in the controls bar.

How do I change the language?

Click EN / FR / ع. Arabic enables right-to-left layout automatically.

What are the difficulty levels?

Easy has 8 major battles, Medium has 14, and Hard includes 20+ events including key treaties and milestones.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser. No data is sent anywhere.

Purpose

Learn the chronological order of the Prophet's military campaigns and key events during his time in Medina (622-632 CE).

Strategy Evolution

The timeline shows how strategy evolved: defensive at Badr, siege defense at Khandaq, offensive campaigns at Khaybar, and the peaceful Conquest of Mecca.

Themes

8 built-in themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Arctic, Sand.

Languages

Trilingual: English, Français, العربية. Arabic auto-enables RTL layout.

Sound

Web Audio API — no files needed. Correct = rising tone, wrong = buzz, win = melody. Toggle with the sound button.

Privacy

Local-first. No tracking, no analytics, no external calls. All data stays in your browser.
