# Civilian Protection — Islamic Ethics of Warfare

Interactive scenario-based game teaching Islamic rules for protecting civilians during conflict, based on the Prophet's (peace be upon him) commands and Abu Bakr's famous 10 rules.

## Concept

Islam established the world's first comprehensive code of conduct for warfare — centuries before the Geneva Conventions. The player faces 10 battlefield scenarios and must choose the correct action according to Islamic ethics of warfare.

## The 10 Scenarios

| # | Scenario | Correct Action |
|---|----------|----------------|
| 1 | Children in the Village | Protect and feed them |
| 2 | Elderly Man on the Road | Help him to safety |
| 3 | Women Found After Battle | Ensure their safety and dignity |
| 4 | Place of Worship | Leave monks in peace |
| 5 | Surrendering Families | Accept surrender and guarantee safety |
| 6 | Wounded Enemy Soldier | Give water and treat wounds |
| 7 | Farmland and Crops | Refuse to destroy crops or trees |
| 8 | Merchant Ship Captured | Release — civilians are protected |
| 9 | Enemy Envoy Arrives | Guarantee safety — envoys are protected |
| 10 | Prisoners of War | Treat with dignity — feed and shelter |

## Features

- Single HTML file, zero dependencies, fully offline
- 8 CSS themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Arctic, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Sand
- Trilingual: English, French, Arabic (with RTL support)
- Splash screen with Bismillah
- Sidebar help panel with FAQ, How-To, and Wiki tabs
- Web Audio API sound effects (no audio files)
- Keyboard shortcuts (1-3 for choices, Enter to continue)
- High score persistence via localStorage
- Theme/language/sound preferences persisted
- Scenarios shuffled each playthrough
- Score tracking with progress bar

## Lesson

Islam commands the protection of civilians, women, children, elderly, religious people, trees, crops, and animals during warfare. True strength is shown through mercy and justice, not through destruction.
