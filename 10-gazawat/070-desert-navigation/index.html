What is Desert Navigation?

Navigate your army across a procedurally generated desert map. Manage water and morale while finding oases and avoiding sandstorms to reach the destination marker.

How do I find the destination?

The compass at the top of the map points toward your destination. The distance indicator shows how far you are. Stars at night also help orient direction.

What happens if water runs out?

Without water, morale drops rapidly. If morale reaches 0, the army collapses and the mission fails. Find oases (green dots) to refill water.

What does Rest do?

Resting uses less water and recovers some morale, but costs a day without moving. Use it strategically when morale is low.

🧭 Desert Navigation

Arab navigators used stars, wind patterns, and sand dune formations to cross vast deserts. The North Star (Polaris) was essential for nighttime orientation.

💧 Water Management

Water was the most precious resource in desert campaigns. Armies carried water in leather bags and knowledge of oases was strategic intelligence guarded carefully.

🏜 Khalid's Desert Crossing

Khalid ibn al-Walid's legendary march from Iraq to Syria crossed the waterless Syrian desert in just 5 days — a feat considered impossible by his enemies. They carried water in camel stomachs.
