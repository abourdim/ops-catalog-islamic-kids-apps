What is Commander Assignment?

A matching game where you assign historical Muslim commanders to missions based on their unique skills and strengths.

Are these real commanders?

Yes. All commanders are real historical companions of the Prophet with documented skills and achievements.

⚖ Khalid ibn al-Walid

Known as "The Sword of Allah." Undefeated in over 100 battles. Master tactician known for lightning cavalry charges and flanking maneuvers.

🛡 Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas

Expert archer, one of the first Muslims. Commanded the army at Qadisiyyah. Known for precision and patience in battle.

🎯 Usama ibn Zayd

Appointed commander at age 18 by the Prophet himself. Known for his courage and the trust the Prophet placed in youth.
