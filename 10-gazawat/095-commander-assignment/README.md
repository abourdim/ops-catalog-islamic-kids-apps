# Commander Assignment

Interactive matching game where players assign historical Muslim commanders to military missions based on their unique skills and strengths.

## Concept

The Prophet (peace be upon him) chose commanders based on their specific talents — placing the right person in the right role. Players learn about 8 legendary companions and match them to 8 missions requiring their particular skills.

## The 8 Commanders & Missions

| # | Commander | Skill | Mission |
|---|-----------|-------|---------|
| 1 | Khalid ibn al-Walid | Cavalry tactics, rapid assault | Rapid cavalry strike |
| 2 | Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas | Expert archery, siege patience | Fortress siege |
| 3 | Usama ibn Zayd | Youth, courage, raids | Youth raiding party |
| 4 | Ali ibn Abi Talib | Sword mastery, single combat | Champion duel at Khaybar |
| 5 | Zubayr ibn al-Awwam | Intelligence, covert ops | Secret intelligence mission |
| 6 | Abu Ubayda ibn al-Jarrah | Diplomacy, trust, command | Governing conquered region |
| 7 | Hamza ibn Abdul-Muttalib | Fearless vanguard, morale | Leading vanguard at Badr |
| 8 | Amr ibn al-As | Diplomacy, desert navigation | Mission to Egypt |

## Features

- Single HTML file, zero dependencies, fully offline
- 8 CSS themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Arctic, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Sand
- Trilingual: English, French, Arabic (with RTL support)
- Splash screen with Bismillah
- Sidebar help panel with FAQ, How-To, and Wiki tabs
- Web Audio API sound effects (no audio files)
- High score persistence via localStorage
- 4-option grid layout with randomized distractors
- Missions shuffled each playthrough

## Lesson

Leadership in Islam is about matching talent to responsibility. The Prophet recognized each companion's unique strengths and assigned them roles accordingly — teaching us that effective leadership means knowing your team.
