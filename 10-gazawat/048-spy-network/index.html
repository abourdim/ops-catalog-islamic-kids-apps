❓ Help
What is Spy Network?

A canvas-based intelligence gathering game. Send scouts to gather intel on enemy positions, then report your findings to the commander through a quiz.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once loaded, everything runs in your browser with no internet needed.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Works on any device with a modern browser — phone, tablet, laptop, desktop. Supports touch drag.

How do I change the theme?

Click any theme button (🖥 🌌 🔥 etc.) in the controls bar.

How do I change the language?

Click EN / FR / ع. Arabic enables right-to-left layout automatically.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser. No data is sent anywhere.

What is the Islamic reference?

The Prophet ﷺ sent scouts before every major battle to gather intelligence. This was a key part of military planning in early Islamic history.

🎯 Purpose

Learn strategic thinking and observation. Gather intelligence by placing scouts wisely, then prove you understood the intel by answering questions.

🗺 Map Zones

Green = Friendly camp (your base). Yellow = Neutral territory (low risk). Red = Enemy territory (high risk, better intel).

🕵 Scouts

Each scout has a detection risk based on placement. Enemy territory scouts have higher risk but gather better intel. Too many scouts increases overall detection.

📋 Intel Types

Troop Count, Supply Level, Movement Direction, and Morale. Each piece of intel has a confidence level based on scout quality.

🎨 Themes

8 built-in themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Arctic, Sand.

🌐 Languages

Trilingual: English, Français, العربية. Arabic auto-enables RTL layout.

🔊 Sound

Web Audio API — no files needed. Toggle with 🔊 button.

🔒 Privacy

Local-first. No tracking, no analytics, no external calls. All data stays in your browser.
