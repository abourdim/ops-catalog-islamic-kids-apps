🎯 Purpose

Teach children about the historical event of Banu Qurayza — the importance of keeping treaties, the consequences of treason in wartime, and how justice was applied even in difficult circumstances.

📖 Historical Context

Year 5 AH (627 CE). During the Battle of the Trench, a coalition of 10,000 soldiers besieged Medina. Banu Qurayza, who had a mutual defense treaty with the Muslims, broke their pact and allied with the attackers — opening a second front from inside the city while Muslim families were defenseless.

⚖️ The Arbitration

After the siege, Banu Qurayza surrendered and chose Sa'd ibn Muadh as their arbitrator. Sa'd judged them according to their own Torah law for wartime treason (Deuteronomy 20:12-14), which they accepted before hearing the verdict.
