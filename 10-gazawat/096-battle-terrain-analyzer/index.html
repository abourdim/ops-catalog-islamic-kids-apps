What is Battle Terrain Analyzer?

A strategic game where you analyze terrain maps from historical Islamic battles and identify key strategic features like high ground, water, and chokepoints.

How does it work?

Each battle presents a canvas map with terrain features. You must answer questions about which strategic advantage applies to each location on the map.
