❓ Help
What is Shuhada Garden?

An interactive memorial to learn about the famous shuhada (martyrs) of early Islam. Walk through a garden where each tree represents a shaheed.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once loaded, everything runs in your browser with no internet needed.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Works on any device with a modern browser — phone, tablet, laptop, desktop.

How do I change the theme?

Click any theme button in the controls bar.

How do I change the language?

Click EN / FR / ع. Arabic enables right-to-left layout automatically.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser. No data is sent anywhere.

What is Quiz mode?

Test your knowledge! Match each shaheed to their story, battle, or unique fact. Your best score is saved locally.

🌳 Purpose

Learn about the companions who gave their lives for Islam. Each tree in the garden represents a shaheed with a unique story.

🎨 Themes

8 built-in themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Arctic, Sand.

🌐 Languages

Trilingual: English, Francais, العربية. Arabic auto-enables RTL layout.

🔊 Sound

Web Audio API — no files needed. Toggle with the sound button.

📝 Quiz Mode

10 questions matching shuhada to their stories. Your best percentage is saved as a high score.

🔒 Privacy

Local-first. No tracking, no analytics, no external calls. All data stays in your browser.
