What is Khalid's Tactics?

A canvas-based tactical training game where you learn three famous cavalry maneuvers used by Khalid ibn al-Walid (RA), the legendary Muslim commander who never lost a battle.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once loaded, everything runs in your browser with no internet needed.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Works on any device with a modern browser — phone, tablet, laptop, desktop. Touch and mouse supported.

How do I change the theme?

Click any theme button in the controls bar.

How do I change the language?

Click EN / FR / ع. Arabic enables right-to-left layout automatically.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser. No data is sent anywhere.

Battle of Mu'tah (629 CE)

3,000 Muslims faced 200,000 Romans. After three commanders fell as martyrs, Khalid took command and reorganized the army at night — swapping wings to confuse the enemy into thinking reinforcements arrived — then executed a brilliant tactical withdrawal saving the entire army.

Battle of Yarmouk (636 CE)

Khalid placed a mobile cavalry reserve in the center. When Roman attacks pressed one wing, he rapidly redeployed cavalry to reinforce it. He moved his cavalry over 40 times in a single day, always appearing where the enemy least expected.

Battle of Walaja (633 CE)

Khalid hid two cavalry groups behind hills on each flank. His main force engaged the Persian center. When the Persians were fully committed, the hidden cavalry sprang the trap from both sides — a double envelopment that destroyed the enemy force.
