# Khalid's Tactics

**Learn from the Sword of Allah**

A canvas-based tactical training game for Muslim kids. Master three legendary cavalry maneuvers from the battles of Khalid ibn al-Walid (RA) — the commander who never lost a single battle.

## Maneuvers

### 1. Mu'tah Retreat (629 CE)
3,000 Muslims vs 200,000 Romans. After three commanders fell as martyrs, Khalid took command at night and:
- **Setup:** Swap left and right wings to confuse the enemy into thinking reinforcements arrived
- **Execute:** Activate the rear guard to create a dust cloud masking the retreat
- **Result:** Execute an orderly withdrawal — the entire army is saved

### 2. Yarmouk Rotation (636 CE)
Khalid placed a mobile cavalry reserve in the center of the battlefield:
- **Execute:** When a wing comes under attack (highlighted in red), drag the cavalry reserve to reinforce it
- **Challenge:** Khalid moved his cavalry over 40 times in one day — you must be fast or lose points
- **Result:** The decisive victory that opened Greater Syria to Islam

### 3. Walaja Double Envelopment (633 CE)
Khalid hid two cavalry groups behind hills on each flank:
- **Setup:** Drag the hidden cavalry units behind the hills on left and right flanks
- **Engage:** Watch the infantry engage the Persian center
- **Spring the trap:** At the right moment, tap both hidden cavalry groups to complete the envelopment

## Scoring
- Each maneuver scores 0-100 points
- Fewer mistakes = higher score
- 0 mistakes = PERFECT (100 points)
- High scores are saved locally

## Features
- Single HTML file, zero dependencies, fully offline
- Canvas-based battlefield with drag-and-drop unit control
- Touch and mouse support (works on phone, tablet, desktop)
- 8 CSS themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Arctic, Sand
- 3 languages: English, French, Arabic (with RTL support)
- Web Audio API sound effects (no audio files needed)
- Persistent preferences (theme, language, sound, high score)
- Sidebar help with FAQ, How-To, and historical Wiki
- Keyboard shortcuts: 1/2/3 to select maneuver, Enter to start, H for help, Esc to close help

## Storage Keys
- `wdiy-hs-khalid-tactics` — High score
- `wdiy-theme` — Selected theme
- `wdiy-lang` — Selected language
- `wdiy-mute` — Sound preference
