What is this game?

A tracking and pursuit game about the Prophet's ﷺ expedition against Banu Lihyan after they treacherously killed 70 Muslim Quran reciters at Bi'r Ma'una.

Is it historically accurate?

Yes. Based on authentic historical accounts of the Banu Lihyan expedition in 6 AH.

What is the stealth meter?

It tracks how well your army remains undetected. Good decisions keep stealth high.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Everything runs in your browser with no internet needed.
