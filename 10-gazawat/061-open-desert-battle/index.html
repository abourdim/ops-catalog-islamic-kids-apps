What is Open Desert Battle?

A canvas strategy game where you position troops on flat desert terrain to defeat enemy waves using flanking and cavalry charges, inspired by early Islamic desert battles.

Is this historically accurate?

The tactics are inspired by real desert warfare strategies used in early Islamic battles such as Badr and Hunayn.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once loaded, everything runs in your browser with no internet needed.

How do units differ?

Infantry is strong in defense, archers deal ranged damage, and cavalry excels at flanking for bonus damage.

🏜 Desert Warfare

Early Muslim armies mastered desert combat, using the open terrain for cavalry charges and flanking maneuvers. The Battle of Badr (2 AH) demonstrated superior tactical positioning.

⚔ Flanking Tactics

Khalid ibn al-Walid was famous for using cavalry flanking at the Battle of Yarmouk, surrounding enemies from the sides while infantry held the center.

🏹 Archery in Islam

The Prophet (peace be upon him) encouraged archery practice. Archers played key roles at Uhud defending strategic positions.
