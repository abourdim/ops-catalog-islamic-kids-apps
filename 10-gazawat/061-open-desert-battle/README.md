# Open Desert Battle — Desert Warfare Tactics

Interactive canvas strategy game where players position troops on flat desert terrain, managing flanking and cavalry charges inspired by early Islamic desert battles.

## Concept

Command your forces across open desert plains. Position infantry, archers, and cavalry units strategically to outmaneuver enemy waves. Learn about desert warfare tactics used by early Muslim armies at battles like Badr and Yarmouk.

## Gameplay

- Select unit type: Infantry (defense), Archers (ranged), Cavalry (flanking)
- Click on the bottom half of the canvas to place units
- Start battle to watch combat unfold with auto-targeting AI
- Cavalry gains 1.8x damage bonus when flanking enemies
- Survive 5 increasingly difficult enemy waves
- Score points for each enemy defeated

## Features

- Single HTML file, zero dependencies, fully offline
- 8 CSS themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Arctic, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Sand
- Trilingual: English, French, Arabic (with RTL support)
- Splash screen with Bismillah
- Sidebar help panel with FAQ, How-To, and Wiki tabs
- Web Audio API sound effects (no audio files)
- High score persistence via localStorage
- Theme/language/sound preferences persisted
- Canvas-based battlefield with real-time combat simulation

## Lesson

Desert warfare required strategic thinking and positioning. Early Muslim commanders like Khalid ibn al-Walid mastered flanking tactics and cavalry charges, turning the open terrain into a strategic advantage.
