What is Battle of Qadisiyyah?

A canvas strategy game about the historic battle (636 CE) where the Muslim army defeated the Sasanian Persian Empire. Play through 4 phases of the battle.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once loaded, everything runs in your browser with no internet needed.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Works on any device with a modern browser. Tap on canvas elements to interact.

How do I change the theme?

Click any theme button in the controls bar.

How do I change the language?

Click EN / FR / ع. Arabic enables right-to-left layout automatically.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser. No data is sent anywhere.

🎯 Purpose

Learn about the Battle of Qadisiyyah (636 CE), one of the most important battles in Islamic history that ended the Sasanian Persian Empire.

⚔️ Historical Context

Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas (may Allah be pleased with him) commanded the Muslim forces. Though ill, he directed the battle from a building overlooking the plain. The Persians, led by Rustam, deployed war elephants to terrify the Muslim cavalry.

🐘 The Elephant Challenge

Muslim warriors discovered that targeting the howdah straps and elephant handlers neutralized the elephant threat. Al-Qa'qa' ibn Amr played a key role in this.

🌙 Night of Howling

Laylat al-Harir — the third night saw intense fighting. Neither side retreated. The Muslims' faith kept them steadfast through the darkness.

🌪️ The Sandstorm

On the final day, a sandstorm blew into the faces of the Persians, giving the Muslims an advantage for the decisive charge.

💬 The Famous Speech

Before the battle, the Muslim envoy Rib'i ibn Amir entered the Persian court and delivered a powerful message about the purpose of the Muslim mission.

📖 Lesson

Faith vs empire, simplicity vs luxury. A small army of believers, trusting in Allah, overcame the greatest empire of their time.

🎨 Themes

8 built-in themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Arctic, Sand.

🔊 Sound

Web Audio API — no files needed. Toggle with the sound button.

🔒 Privacy

Local-first. No tracking, no analytics, no external calls. All data stays in your browser.
