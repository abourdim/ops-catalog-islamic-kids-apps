❓ Help
What is Ambush Planner?

A strategy game where you plan ambushes using terrain. Place hidden units behind hills and in trees, then trigger the ambush when the enemy column enters your kill zone. Inspired by how the Prophet ﷺ used terrain advantages in battles.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once loaded, everything runs in your browser with no internet needed.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Works on any device with a modern browser — phone, tablet, laptop, desktop. Tap to place units and trigger.

How do I change the theme?

Click any theme button in the controls bar.

How do I change the language?

Click EN / FR / ع. Arabic enables right-to-left layout automatically.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser. No data is sent anywhere.

🎯 Purpose

Learn strategic thinking through terrain-based ambush planning. Understand how the Prophet ﷺ and the Sahabah used geography and timing in battles like Badr, Uhud, and the Trench.

🎨 Themes

8 built-in themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Arctic, Sand.

🌐 Languages

Trilingual: English, Français, العربية. Arabic auto-enables RTL layout.

🔊 Sound

Web Audio API — no files needed. Placement = click, trigger = boom, win = melody. Toggle with sound button.

⚔️ Terrain Types

Hills (green mounds): hide units behind them. Trees (green clusters): conceal archers. Road (brown path): enemy route. Valley: the kill zone where ambush is most effective.

🔒 Privacy

Local-first. No tracking, no analytics, no external calls. All data stays in your browser.
