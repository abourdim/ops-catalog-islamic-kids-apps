What is Pre-Battle Dua?

A matching game where you learn the actual duas made before historical battles and match them to their context.

Why dua before battle?

Muslims always turned to Allah first before any undertaking. The Prophet spent the night before Badr in prayer and supplication.

🙏 Dua in Islam

Dua (supplication) is the weapon of the believer. The Prophet said: 'Dua is the essence of worship.'

⚔ Battles of Islam

Each major battle had specific duas and prayers associated with it, showing total reliance on Allah.
