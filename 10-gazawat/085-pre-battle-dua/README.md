# Pre-Battle Dua — Prayers Before Battle

Interactive matching game where players learn authentic duas made before historical Islamic battles and match each dua to its battle context.

## Concept

Muslims always turned to Allah first before any undertaking. The Prophet spent the night before Badr in intense prayer. Players see Arabic duas with translations and must identify which battle or context they belong to.

## The 10 Duas

| # | Dua Context | Battle |
|---|-------------|--------|
| 1 | O Allah, fulfill Your promise to me | Battle of Badr |
| 2 | O Allah, defeat the confederates | Battle of the Trench |
| 3 | We place You against their throats | General dua against enemies |
| 4 | Allah is sufficient for us | Said by Ibrahim (AS) and at Uhud |
| 5 | O Allah, grant us victory | Battle of Uhud |
| 6 | He fulfilled His promise | After Conquest of Mecca |
| 7 | By You I attack, advance, fight | General battle dua |
| 8 | O Allah, open Khaybar for me | Battle of Khaybar |
| 9 | No life but the Hereafter | Digging the Trench |
| 10 | Refuge from worry and weakness | Daily dua before campaigns |

## Features

- Single HTML file, zero dependencies, fully offline
- 8 CSS themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Arctic, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Sand
- Trilingual: English, French, Arabic (with RTL support)
- Splash screen with Bismillah
- Arabic duas displayed in beautiful Noto Sans Arabic font
- Sidebar help panel with FAQ, How-To, and Wiki tabs
- Web Audio API sound effects (no audio files)
- High score persistence via localStorage
- Duas shuffled each playthrough

## Lesson

Dua is the weapon of the believer. The Prophet and companions never faced any situation without first turning to Allah in supplication, showing complete reliance on the Creator.
