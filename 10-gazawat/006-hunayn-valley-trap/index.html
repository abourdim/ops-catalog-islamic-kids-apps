❓ Help
What is the Battle of Hunayn?

A major battle in 8 AH (630 CE) where the Muslim army of 12,000 was ambushed in a narrow valley by the Hawazin tribe. Despite initial chaos, the Prophet ﷺ stood firm and the Muslims regrouped to win.

What is the lesson?

Numbers alone don't guarantee victory — only faith in Allah and steadfastness matter. The Quran reminds us in Surah At-Tawbah (9:25).

🎯 Purpose

Teach kids the story and lessons of the Battle of Hunayn through an interactive canvas strategy game.

⚔️ Historical Context

After the conquest of Makkah, 12,000 Muslims marched to fight the Hawazin and Thaqif tribes. Some soldiers boasted about their large numbers. In the narrow valley of Hunayn, the enemy ambushed them with arrows from the hills. The army scattered in chaos. The Prophet ﷺ stood firm with about 100 companions. Abbas (RA) called out to the Ansar, who regrouped and won the battle.
