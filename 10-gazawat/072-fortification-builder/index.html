What is Fortification Builder?

A grid-based strategy game where you design defensive fortifications. Place walls, towers, gates, moats, and archer positions to create the strongest fortress.

Is this historically accurate?

Yes. Islamic cities like Medina used trench defenses (Battle of the Trench, 5 AH). Fortifications were crucial in Islamic military history.

How is defense scored?

Your score depends on wall coverage, tower placement, moat positioning, gate protection, and budget efficiency. The attack simulation tests weak points.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser.

Battle of the Trench (Khandaq)

In 5 AH (627 CE), Salman al-Farisi suggested digging a trench around Medina. This innovative fortification prevented the confederate army from invading, showing the importance of defensive engineering.

Islamic Fortifications

Muslim engineers built impressive fortress cities (ribats) across North Africa and the Middle East. These featured thick walls, watchtowers, and strategic gate placements.

Defensive Strategy

The Prophet (peace be upon him) emphasized preparation and planning. Using terrain advantages and engineering solutions was considered wise, not cowardly.
