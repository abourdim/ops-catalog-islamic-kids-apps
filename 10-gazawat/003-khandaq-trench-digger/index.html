❓ Help
What is this game?

Dig the trench that protected Medina during the Battle of the Trench (Khandaq) in 5 AH. Draw the trench path and assign workers to complete it before the enemy arrives!

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once loaded, everything runs in your browser with no internet needed.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Works on any device with a modern browser — phone, tablet, laptop, desktop. Use touch to draw the trench.

How do I change the theme?

Click any theme button (🖥 🌌 🔥 etc.) in the controls bar.

How do I change the language?

Click EN / FR / ع. Arabic enables right-to-left layout automatically.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser. No data is sent anywhere.

🏰 Battle of the Trench

In 5 AH (627 CE), a coalition of 10,000 Quraysh and allied forces marched on Medina. Salman Al-Farisi suggested digging a trench on the unprotected north side — a strategy never used in Arabia before.

⛏ The Trench

The trench was approximately 5.5 km long, 5 meters wide, and 5 meters deep. Around 3,000 Muslims dug it in about 6 days. Mountains and buildings protected the other sides.

🤝 Shura (Consultation)

The Prophet (peace be upon him) consulted his companions. Salman Al-Farisi, originally from Persia, suggested the trench — a Persian military tactic. This shows the value of diverse ideas and consultation.

🎨 Themes

🌐 Languages

Trilingual: English, Francais, العربية. Arabic auto-enables RTL layout.
