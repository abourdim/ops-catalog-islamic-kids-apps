What is Battle Flag Maker?

A creative design tool where you create battle flags with Islamic calligraphy, colors, and patterns inspired by historical Islamic banners.

What were Islamic battle flags?

The Prophet (peace be upon him) had two main banners: Al-Liwa (white) and Ar-Raya (black). These were carried by standard bearers in battle as rallying points.

Can I save my designs?

The flag count and best score are saved. The canvas itself is created fresh each time for a new creative experience.

Al-Liwa (The Banner)

The Prophet's white banner, often carried by senior companions. It served as the command flag and rallying point during battles.

Ar-Raya (The Standard)

The Prophet's black standard, given to the commander of the army. It was a symbol of authority and leadership in battle.

Islamic Calligraphy

Arabic calligraphy is one of the highest art forms in Islamic culture. Banners often featured Quranic verses or declarations of faith.
