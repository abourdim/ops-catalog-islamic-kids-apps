# Army Recruitment

Interactive strategy game where kids recruit and organize an army from different skill pools, balancing budget and troop diversity.

## Concept

Islamic armies were carefully organized with distinct unit types. At the Battle of Badr (2 AH), the Prophet (peace be upon him) strategically positioned archers, infantry, and cavalry despite being outnumbered. Players learn about military organization by building their own balanced army.

## Gameplay

1. Start with a budget of 60 gold
2. Choose from 6 unit types: Infantry, Archers, Cavalry, Scouts, Engineers, Medics
3. Each unit has different cost, attack, and defense stats
4. Use + and - buttons to recruit soldiers of each type
5. Balance diversity (using multiple types) for bonus points
6. Deploy the army to calculate total power score

## Unit Types

| Unit | Cost | ATK | DEF | Role |
|------|------|-----|-----|------|
| Infantry | 3 | 5 | 6 | Front-line fighters |
| Archers | 4 | 7 | 3 | Ranged attackers |
| Cavalry | 6 | 8 | 5 | Fast mounted warriors |
| Scouts | 2 | 2 | 2 | Reconnaissance |
| Engineers | 5 | 3 | 7 | Field fortifications |
| Medics | 3 | 0 | 4 | Heal wounded |

## Features

- Single HTML file, zero dependencies, fully offline
- 8 CSS themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Arctic, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Sand
- Trilingual: English, French, Arabic (with RTL support)
- Splash screen with Bismillah
- Sidebar help panel with FAQ, How-To, and Wiki tabs
- Web Audio API sound effects (no audio files)
- Budget management with real-time tracking
- Diversity and balance bonus scoring
- High score persistence via localStorage

## Lesson

Strategic organization: The Prophet (peace be upon him) taught that preparation and wise planning are essential. A balanced, well-organized force is stronger than a larger but disorganized one. The lesson of the archers at Uhud shows the importance of discipline and following orders.
