# Dua of Taif

**When mercy defeated anger**

An interactive story experience for Muslim kids about the Prophet Muhammad's (peace be upon him) journey to Taif, the hardship he endured, and the powerful dua he made — teaching patience, mercy, and trust in Allah.

## Features

- **6-phase interactive story** with canvas-illustrated scenes
- **Decision point**: kids choose between mercy and punishment, then learn the Prophet's answer
- **Full Dua of Taif** in Arabic with transliteration and English meaning
- **Canvas illustrations** for each scene: the journey, rejection, stoning, the garden dua, the angel's offer, and Taif's eventual acceptance of Islam
- **Web Audio** ambient sounds for scene transitions (no external files)
- **8 CSS themes**: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Arctic, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Sand
- **3 languages**: English, French, Arabic (with RTL support)
- **Keyboard shortcuts**: Enter/Space to advance, 1/2 for choices, Escape to close help
- **Sidebar help** with FAQ, How-To, and Wiki tabs
- **Persisted preferences**: theme, language, sound, and best completion time via localStorage
- **Single HTML file**, zero dependencies, fully offline

## Story Phases

1. **The Journey** — Prophet walks 70km from Mecca to Taif with Zayd ibn Harithah
2. **Rejection** — Three leaders of Taif mock and refuse his message
3. **Stoned** — Children and slaves throw stones; blood runs to his sandals
4. **The Dua** — Exhausted in a garden, he makes the famous Dua of Taif
5. **The Angel's Offer** — Angel of Mountains offers to crush Taif; kid makes a choice
6. **The Prophet's Answer** — He chooses mercy; Taif later accepts Islam
7. **Lessons** — Patience, mercy, long-term vision, never giving up on people

## Lessons

- Patience in adversity
- Mercy even to those who harm you
- Long-term vision over short-term anger
- Never giving up on people
- Trust in Allah above all
