What is Dua of Taif?

An interactive story about the Prophet's journey to Taif, his famous dua, and the powerful lesson of mercy over anger.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once loaded, everything runs in your browser with no internet needed.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Works on any device with a modern browser — phone, tablet, laptop, desktop.

How do I change the theme?

Click any theme button in the controls bar.

How do I change the language?

Click EN / FR / ع. Arabic enables right-to-left layout automatically.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser. No data is sent anywhere.

What is the decision point?

At one point in the story, you will be asked to make a choice — just like the Prophet was given a choice by the Angel of Mountains. Choose wisely!

1

Click Start Mission on the splash screen

2

Purpose

Learn about the Prophet's journey to Taif, the hardship he faced, and how he chose mercy. Memorize the famous Dua of Taif.

Themes

8 built-in themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Arctic, Sand.

Languages

Trilingual: English, Francais, Arabic. Arabic auto-enables RTL layout.

Sound

Web Audio API — no files needed. Ambient sounds for scene transitions. Toggle with sound button.

Historical Context

The journey to Taif happened in the 10th year of prophethood, known as the Year of Grief, after the deaths of Khadijah and Abu Talib.

Privacy

Local-first. No tracking, no analytics, no external calls. All data stays in your browser.
