❓ Help
What is Siege of Khaybar?

A canvas-based strategy game where you lead the Muslim army to conquer the seven fortresses of Khaybar. Plan your approach, manage resources, and learn about this important event in Islamic history.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once loaded, everything runs in your browser with no internet needed.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Works on any device with a modern browser — phone, tablet, laptop, desktop. Tap fortresses to interact.

How do I change the theme?

Click any theme button (🖥 🌌 🔥 etc.) in the controls bar.

How do I change the language?

Click EN / FR / ع. Arabic enables right-to-left layout automatically.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser. No data is sent anywhere.

What is the best strategy?

Conquer the outer fortresses first to cut supply lines to the inner ones. Use siege for strong fortresses to save troops, and negotiate when possible.

🏰 The Battle

The Siege of Khaybar (7 AH / 628 CE) was a series of battles against fortified settlements. The Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him) led 1,400 Muslims to conquer seven fortresses.

🦁 Ali's Bravery

Ali ibn Abi Talib (may Allah be pleased with him) famously carried the heavy fortress gate as a shield during the battle, demonstrating extraordinary courage and strength.

🕊 Justice in Victory

After victory, the people of Khaybar were allowed to stay on their land and continue farming, paying a portion as tax. This showed the justice of Islamic governance.
