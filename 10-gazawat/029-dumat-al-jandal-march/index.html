❓ Help
What is this game?

Lead the Muslim expedition from Medina to Dumat al-Jandal (modern Sakaka), 800km north through the desert. Manage resources and make decisions to reach the destination before the tribal coalition assembles.

How do I win?

Reach Dumat al-Jandal within 15 days with enough soldiers, water, morale, and stamina. The tribes scatter when they see the Muslim army — victory through deterrence!

What do the pace options do?

Fast March: covers more distance but drains stamina and water faster. Normal Pace: balanced speed and resource usage. Cautious: slower but conserves resources and avoids ambushes.

What do scouts do?

Scouts reveal what lies ahead — they may find oases, spot hostile tribes, or warn of sandstorms. Scouting costs one day but can save you from disaster.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once loaded, everything runs in your browser with no internet needed.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser. No data is sent anywhere.

🎯 Historical Background

The expedition to Dumat al-Jandal (5 AH / 626 CE) was one of the earliest long-range Muslim expeditions. Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him) led 1000 soldiers 800km north from Medina toward the Syrian border to deter a hostile tribal coalition supported by the Byzantines.

🗺 Dumat al-Jandal

Modern-day Sakaka in northern Saudi Arabia, near the border with Syria and Iraq. It was a strategic oasis town on trade routes linking Arabia to the Levant. The name means "Dumat of the Rocks."

⚔ Outcome

When the Muslim army arrived, the hostile tribes scattered without fighting. Victory was achieved through deterrence — showing that Muslim reach extended all the way to the Byzantine border.

📖 Lesson

Projection of power and long-range expeditions can achieve strategic goals without bloodshed. Preparation, logistics, and determination made this possible.
