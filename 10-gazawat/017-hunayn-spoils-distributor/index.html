What is this game?

After the Battle of Hunayn (8 AH), the Prophet ﷺ distributed massive spoils among the Muslims. This game teaches you how he wisely allocated them to win hearts and strengthen the Ummah.

What were the spoils?

24,000 camels, 40,000 sheep, and 4,000 ounces of silver — one of the largest spoils in early Islamic history.

Who were the recipients?

New Muslims of Mecca (Muallafat al-Quloob), the Ansar of Medina, the Muhajirun, and Bayt al-Mal (state treasury).

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once loaded, everything runs in your browser with no internet needed.

How do I change the theme?

Click any theme button in the controls bar.

How do I change the language?

Click EN / FR / ع. Arabic enables right-to-left layout automatically.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser. No data is sent anywhere.

Battle of Hunayn

Fought in 8 AH (630 CE) in the valley of Hunayn between Mecca and Ta'if. The Muslim army of 12,000 faced the Hawazin and Thaqif tribes. After an initial ambush, the Muslims rallied and won decisively.

Muallafat al-Quloob

"Those whose hearts are to be reconciled" — new Muslims and tribal leaders who received generous shares to strengthen their faith and loyalty to Islam.

The Ansar's Response

When the Ansar felt the distribution was unfair, the Prophet ﷺ gathered them and asked if they would prefer to go home with him rather than with material wealth. They wept and declared they were content with the Messenger of Allah as their share.
