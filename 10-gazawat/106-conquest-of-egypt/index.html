What is this game?

A campaign planner for Amr ibn al-As's conquest of Egypt (19-21 AH / 640-642 CE), liberating the Copts from Byzantine oppression.

Who was Amr ibn al-As?

A brilliant Companion and military commander. He convinced Caliph Umar to allow the Egyptian campaign and led it with only 4,000 men initially.

Were the Copts treated well?

Yes. The Muslims freed the Copts from Byzantine religious persecution. Coptic Christians were given freedom of worship and fair governance.

Byzantine Egypt

Egypt under Byzantine rule suffered heavy taxation and religious persecution of Coptic Christians, who were considered heretics by Constantinople.

The Campaign

Amr ibn al-As entered Egypt with 4,000 men, later reinforced to 12,000. Key battles at Pelusium, Heliopolis, and the siege of Babylon fortress.

Founding of Fustat

Amr founded the city of Fustat (old Cairo) and built the first mosque in Africa — the Mosque of Amr ibn al-As, which still stands today.
