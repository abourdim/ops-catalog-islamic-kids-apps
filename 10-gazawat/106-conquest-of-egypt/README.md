# Conquest of Egypt — Campaign Planner

Interactive strategic campaign planner for Amr ibn al-As's conquest of Egypt (19-21 AH / 640-642 CE).

## Concept

Amr ibn al-As convinced Caliph Umar to allow the Egyptian campaign. With only 4,000 men initially (later reinforced to 12,000), he liberated Egypt from Byzantine oppression. The player manages the campaign from Palestine through Sinai to the Nile Delta.

## The 8 Phases

| # | Phase | Key Lesson |
|---|-------|------------|
| 1 | Convince the Caliph | Strategic logic wins approval |
| 2 | Entry into Sinai | Coastal route provides water and speed |
| 3 | Battle of Pelusium | Siege and supply line cuts preserve forces |
| 4 | Battle of Heliopolis | Flanking overcomes numerical disadvantage |
| 5 | Reinforcements | Momentum is critical after reinforcement |
| 6 | Siege of Babylon | Bold courage can decide battles |
| 7 | Treatment of Copts | Religious freedom wins hearts |
| 8 | Founding of Fustat | New city symbolizes new era |

## Features

- Single HTML file, zero dependencies, fully offline
- 8 CSS themes with canvas map showing route from Palestine to Egypt
- Trilingual with RTL support, Bismillah splash screen
- Nile, Mediterranean, and city markers on interactive map
- Web Audio API sounds, localStorage persistence

## Lesson

Small, well-led forces achieve great victories. Military brilliance combined with just governance frees the oppressed and builds civilization.
