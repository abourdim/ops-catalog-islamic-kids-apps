What is the Fear Prayer?

Salat al-Khawf is a special way to perform congregational prayer when there is danger (e.g., in battle). It was revealed by Allah in Surah An-Nisa (4:102) during the expedition of Dhat al-Riqa.

Purpose

Learn how Salat al-Khawf (Fear Prayer) works — a special congregational prayer performed when there is danger, so the Muslims never miss their prayer even during battle.

Historical Context

During the expedition of Dhat al-Riqa, the enemy was close and prayer time came. Allah revealed how to pray in this situation (Surah An-Nisa 4:102). The army splits into two groups that take turns praying and guarding.
