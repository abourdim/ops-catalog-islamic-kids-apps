# Conquest of Persia — Campaign Strategy

Interactive campaign strategy game about the Muslim conquest of the Sassanid Empire (14-23 AH / 636-644 CE).

## Concept

The Muslim conquest of Persia was one of history's most remarkable campaigns. Small but dedicated Muslim armies defeated the Sassanid superpower through faith, creative tactics, and just governance. Key battles include Qadisiyyah and Nahavand (Victory of Victories).

## The 8 Phases

| # | Phase | Key Lesson |
|---|-------|------------|
| 1 | Rustam's Challenge | Moral strength inspires confidence |
| 2 | War Elephants at Qadisiyyah | Creative tactics overcome technology |
| 3 | Night of Fury | Decisive persistence wins battles |
| 4 | Fall of Ctesiphon | Bold river crossing terrifies enemies |
| 5 | Distribution of Spoils | Fair distribution according to Islamic law |
| 6 | Advance to Isfahan | Systematic advance ensures stability |
| 7 | Battle of Nahavand | Feigned retreat draws out fortified enemy |
| 8 | Legacy of the Conquest | Respect local systems, add Islamic justice |

## Features

- Single HTML file, zero dependencies, fully offline
- 8 CSS themes with canvas map of Mesopotamia and Persia
- Troops/Morale/Supply resource management
- Trilingual with RTL support, Bismillah splash screen
- Web Audio API sounds, localStorage persistence

## Lesson

Faith, creativity, and just governance overcome the mightiest empires. Respecting conquered peoples' culture enriches civilization.
