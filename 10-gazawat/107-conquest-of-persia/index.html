What is this game?

A campaign strategy game about the Muslim conquest of the Sassanid Persian Empire (14-23 AH / 636-644 CE), featuring key battles like Qadisiyyah and Nahavand.

Who were the key commanders?

Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas led at Qadisiyyah, Nu'man ibn Muqarrin at Nahavand. Both under the guidance of Caliph Umar ibn al-Khattab.

What were war elephants?

The Sassanid army used war elephants to terrify opponents. The Muslims devised creative tactics to counter them.

Sassanid Empire

One of the two superpowers alongside Byzantium. Ruled a vast territory from Mesopotamia to Central Asia. Internal instability weakened it before the Muslim conquest.

Battle of Qadisiyyah

In 14 AH (636 CE), 30,000 Muslims under Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas defeated 60,000+ Sassanid troops with war elephants. A 3-day battle that opened Mesopotamia.

Battle of Nahavand

In 21 AH (642 CE), called "Victory of Victories," this battle effectively ended the Sassanid Empire. Nu'man ibn Muqarrin achieved martyrdom in the victory.
