❓ Help
What is Arrow Rain?

A canvas archery physics game. Adjust your bow angle and power to arc arrows at shield targets placed at various distances. Learn about trajectory and the role of Muslim archers in early Islamic battles.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once loaded, everything runs in your browser with no internet needed.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Works on any device with a modern browser — phone, tablet, laptop, desktop.

How do I change the theme?

Click any theme button (🖥 🌌 🔥 etc.) in the controls bar.

How do I change the language?

Click EN / FR / ع. Arabic enables right-to-left layout automatically.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser. No data is sent anywhere.

What about wind?

On Medium difficulty, a light wind blows. On Hard, the wind is stronger. Wind shifts the arrow left or right during flight. Easy mode has no wind.

🏹 Muslim Archers at Badr & Uhud

At the Battle of Badr (2 AH / 624 CE), the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ positioned skilled archers strategically, helping the Muslim army achieve victory despite being outnumbered. At Uhud (3 AH / 625 CE), the Prophet ﷺ placed 50 archers on Mount Ainain to guard the rear. Their discipline was crucial — archery was a valued skill among the early Muslims. The Prophet ﷺ encouraged practicing archery, saying it is a source of strength.

🎯 Game Mechanics

The arrow follows a parabolic arc based on projectile physics. Angle controls the launch direction, power controls initial speed. Gravity pulls the arrow down over time. On harder difficulties, wind adds a horizontal force, making shots more challenging.

🎨 Themes

8 built-in themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Arctic, Sand.

🌐 Languages

Trilingual: English, Francais, العربية. Arabic auto-enables RTL layout.

🔊 Sound

Web Audio API — no files needed. Whoosh for fire, thud for hit, buzz for miss, melody for win. Toggle with 🔊 button.

🔒 Privacy

Local-first. No tracking, no analytics, no external calls. All data stays in your browser.
