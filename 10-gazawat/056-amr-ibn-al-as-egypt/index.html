❓ Help
What is Conquest of Egypt?

A canvas map strategy game where you lead Amr ibn al-As and 4,000 Muslim troops through Egypt, making strategic decisions at each city.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once loaded, everything runs in your browser with no internet needed.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Works on any device with a modern browser — phone, tablet, laptop, desktop.

How do I change the theme?

Click any theme button in the controls bar.

How do I change the language?

Click EN / FR / ع. Arabic enables right-to-left layout automatically.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser. No data is sent anywhere.

🎯 Purpose

Learn about the Muslim conquest of Egypt led by Amr ibn al-As (RA) in 640 CE. A small force of 4,000 achieved remarkable results through faith and strategy.

⚔️ Historical Context

Caliph Umar ibn al-Khattab (RA) authorized Amr ibn al-As to enter Egypt. The campaign moved from Sinai through Pelusium, Bilbeis, Heliopolis, then besieged Babylon Fortress. Reinforcements under Zubayr ibn al-Awwam arrived from Medina. After Babylon fell, Alexandria surrendered.

🏛 Fustat

After the conquest, Amr founded Fustat near Babylon Fortress as the new capital. It later grew into modern Cairo. He also built the first mosque in Africa — the Mosque of Amr ibn al-As.

📖 Lesson

A small force with strong faith can achieve great things. The founding of Fustat became the seed of one of the greatest cities in Islamic civilization.
