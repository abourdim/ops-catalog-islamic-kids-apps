What is Khaybar Food Tester?

A safety observation game where you examine food items for signs of contamination, based on the historical incident at Khaybar where poisoned meat was offered to the Prophet.

Is this historically based?

Yes. After the Battle of Khaybar (7 AH), a woman named Zaynab bint al-Harith placed poison in a lamb shoulder. The Prophet was divinely informed and stopped eating.

How do I play?

Read the observation clues carefully for each food item. Look for signs like unusual color, strange smell, or suspicious source. Then decide: Safe or Unsafe.

🏦 Battle of Khaybar

In 7 AH (628 CE), Muslims besieged the fortresses of Khaybar. After the victory, a local woman attempted to poison the Prophet through contaminated lamb meat.

🛡 Food Safety in Islam

Islam teaches: say Bismillah before eating, eat only halal and tayyib (wholesome) food, and be cautious about food from unknown sources.

💡 Divine Protection

The Prophet was informed by Allah about the poison. He stopped eating and the lamb itself spoke to him, warning of the contamination.
