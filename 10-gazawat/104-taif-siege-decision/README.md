# Taif Siege Decision — Patience & Mercy

Interactive decision game about the Prophet's (peace be upon him) siege of Taif (8 AH / 630 CE) and his famous dua for Thaqif's guidance.

## Concept

After the Battle of Hunayn, the defeated Thaqif tribe retreated behind Taif's strong walls. The Prophet besieged the city for about 20 days, making crucial decisions about tactics, mercy, and ultimately withdrawal. When Companions asked him to curse Thaqif, he instead prayed for their guidance — and they embraced Islam the following year.

## The 8 Phases

| # | Phase | Day | Key Lesson |
|---|-------|-----|------------|
| 1 | Siege Approach | 1 | Patience over force against walls |
| 2 | Arrow Defense | 3 | Protect soldiers' lives first |
| 3 | Catapult Innovation | 7 | Learn beneficial knowledge from all |
| 4 | Vine Strategy | 10 | Minimum force, spare livelihoods |
| 5 | Freed Slaves | 14 | Liberation is lived, not just preached |
| 6 | Companions' Request | 17 | Pray for guidance, not destruction |
| 7 | Decision to Withdraw | 19 | Withdrawal can be wisdom |
| 8 | Lesson of Taif | 20 | Patience and dua win hearts |

## Features

- Single HTML file, zero dependencies, fully offline
- 8 CSS themes with canvas siege visualization
- Trilingual with RTL support, Bismillah splash screen
- Day counter showing siege progress
- Web Audio API sounds, localStorage persistence

## Lesson

Patience and supplication achieve what force cannot. The Prophet prayed for Thaqif's guidance, and his prayer was answered.
