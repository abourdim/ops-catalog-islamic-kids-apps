What is this game?

A decision game about the siege of Taif (8 AH / 630 CE). After the Battle of Hunayn, the defeated Thaqif tribe retreated to their walled city. Learn about patience and the Prophet's ﷺ merciful dua.

What was the Prophet's dua?

Instead of cursing the people of Taif, the Prophet ﷺ prayed: "O Allah, guide Thaqif and bring them to Islam." They later embraced Islam voluntarily.

How long was the siege?

About 18-20 days. The Prophet ﷺ chose to withdraw rather than cause prolonged suffering, trusting that their hearts would eventually open to Islam.

Battle of Hunayn

After conquering Mecca, the Muslim army faced Hawazin and Thaqif tribes at Hunayn valley. After initial difficulty, the Muslims won decisively.

Siege of Taif

The defeated Thaqif retreated behind Taif's strong walls. The Prophet ﷺ besieged the city for about 18 days before deciding to withdraw.

Outcome

Thaqif sent a delegation to Medina the following year and embraced Islam voluntarily — proving the Prophet's patience and dua were answered.
