What is Armor Fitting?

A drag-and-drop game where you equip a warrior with historical Islamic armor. Each piece must go to the correct body position.

Is this historically accurate?

Yes. The armor types are based on real Islamic military equipment. The Quran mentions armor — Allah taught David to make chainmail (34:10-11).

What happens if I place a piece wrong?

The zone flashes red and the piece returns to the tray. Only correct placements count.

Bayda (Helmet)

The Islamic helmet, often with a nasal guard and chainmail aventail to protect the neck. Made from steel, it was essential protection for cavalry and infantry.

Dir' (Chainmail)

Chainmail armor made of interlocking iron rings. The Quran references it in Surah Saba (34:10-11), mentioning Allah teaching David to make coats of mail.

Daraqa (Shield)

Round shields made of leather or metal, used to deflect sword strikes and arrows. Carried on the left arm to leave the right hand free for weapons.

Islamic Armor Tradition

The Prophet (peace be upon him) wore two coats of chainmail at the Battle of Uhud. Islamic warriors valued practical protection that allowed mobility.
