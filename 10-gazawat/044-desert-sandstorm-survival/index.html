❓ Help
What is Sandstorm?

A canvas-based survival simulation where your army faces a desert sandstorm. Secure camels, protect soldiers, find shelter, and regroup!

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once loaded, everything runs in your browser with no internet needed.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Works on any device with a modern browser. Tap on camels, soldiers, and units to interact.

How do I change the theme?

Click any theme button in the controls bar.

How do I change the language?

Click EN / FR / ع. Arabic enables right-to-left layout automatically.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser. No data is sent anywhere.

What are the game phases?

Phase 1: Secure camels by clicking them. Phase 2: Distribute cloth to soldiers. Phase 3: Move scattered units to shelter behind dunes. Phase 4: After the storm, regroup your forces.

What is the historical context?

Desert sandstorms played significant roles in battles like Khandaq and Qadisiyyah, where Allah sent storms that helped the Muslim forces. This game teaches how armies managed these natural events.

🎯 Purpose

Learn how Muslim armies survived desert sandstorms. Practice quick thinking and resource management under pressure.

🌈 Historical Context

At the Battle of the Trench (Khandaq), Allah sent a fierce wind against the confederate armies besieging Madinah. At Qadisiyyah, sandstorms played a role in the Muslim victory over the Sassanid Empire. These events show how natural phenomena were part of Allah's plan.

🎨 Themes

8 built-in themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Arctic, Sand.

🌐 Languages

Trilingual: English, Français, العربية. Arabic auto-enables RTL layout.

🔊 Sound

Web Audio API — no files needed. Wind howling, success chimes, warning buzzes. Toggle with the sound button.

🔒 Privacy

Local-first. No tracking, no analytics, no external calls. All data stays in your browser.
