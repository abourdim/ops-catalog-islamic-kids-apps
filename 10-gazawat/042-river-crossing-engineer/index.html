❓ Help
What is River Crossing?

A canvas engineering puzzle. The river blocks the Muslim army's advance. You must build a crossing by choosing ford points, building pontoon bridges, or finding alternative routes while protecting troops with archers.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once loaded, everything runs in your browser with no internet needed.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Works on any device with a modern browser — phone, tablet, laptop, desktop. Tap to place items.

How do I change the theme?

Click any theme button (🖥 🌌 🔥 etc.) in the controls bar.

How do I change the language?

Click EN / FR / ع. Arabic enables right-to-left layout automatically.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser. No data is sent anywhere.

🎯 Purpose

Learn about historical river crossings in Islamic military campaigns. Practice engineering thinking and strategic resource placement under time pressure.

🏞 Rivers

Three historical rivers: the Euphrates at Qadisiyyah (636 CE), the Nile crossing into Egypt (639 CE), and a narrow mountain stream en route to Andalus. Each has different width, depth, and current.

🛠 Tools

Ford: mark shallow spots (light blue areas) for wading. Pontoon: place logs/boats to form a bridge. Archer: station bowmen to protect crossing troops from enemy fire.

🎨 Themes

8 built-in themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Arctic, Sand.

🌐 Languages

Trilingual: English, Francais, العربية. Arabic auto-enables RTL layout.

🔊 Sound

Web Audio API — no files needed. Splash, build, arrow, and victory sounds. Toggle with the speaker button.

🔒 Privacy

Local-first. No tracking, no analytics, no external calls. All data stays in your browser.
