What is this game?

A medical triage game set after the Battle of Uhud. Match treatments to injuries using knowledge of early Islamic medicine.

Who were the healers at Uhud?

Women like Rufaydah al-Aslamiyyah (first female Muslim surgeon) and Nusaybah bint Ka'b tended the wounded. The Prophet himself received medical treatment.

🩺 Rufaydah al-Aslamiyyah

The first female Muslim surgeon and nurse. She set up a field hospital tent at the mosque of Medina and treated the wounded after battles.

🏥 Islamic Medicine

The Prophet encouraged seeking medical treatment: 'O servants of Allah, seek treatment, for Allah has not created a disease without creating a cure for it.'
