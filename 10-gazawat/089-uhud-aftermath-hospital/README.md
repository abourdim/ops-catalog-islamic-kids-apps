# Uhud Aftermath Hospital — Early Islamic Medicine

Interactive medical triage game set after the Battle of Uhud. Match treatments to injuries and learn about early Islamic medicine, the role of women healers, and the Prophet's medical guidance.

## Concept

After the Battle of Uhud, the wounded needed urgent care. Rufaydah al-Aslamiyyah — the first female Muslim surgeon — set up a field hospital. Players triage 10 patients, choosing the correct treatment for each.

## The 10 Patients

| # | Patient | Injury | Triage |
|---|---------|--------|--------|
| 1 | The Prophet | Broken tooth, embedded helmet rings | Urgent |
| 2 | Talhah ibn Ubaydallah | 70+ wounds from shielding Prophet | Urgent |
| 3 | Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas | Arrow wound, embedded arrowhead | Moderate |
| 4 | Nusaybah bint Ka'b | Deep shoulder sword wound | Urgent |
| 5 | Heat exhaustion case | Collapsed from dehydration | Moderate |
| 6 | Broken arm | Closed fracture from mace | Moderate |
| 7 | Infected wound | Swollen, red, pus forming | Urgent |
| 8 | Psychological trauma | Shaking, unable to speak | Mild |
| 9 | Severe thirst | Badly wounded, begging for water | Urgent |
| 10 | Field hospital setup | Rufaydah's triage system | Mild |

## Features

- Single HTML file, zero dependencies, fully offline
- 8 CSS themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Arctic, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Sand
- Trilingual: English, French, Arabic (with RTL support)
- Splash screen with Bismillah
- Color-coded triage tags (Urgent/Moderate/Mild)
- Sidebar help panel with FAQ, How-To, and Wiki tabs
- Web Audio API sound effects
- High score persistence via localStorage
- Patients shuffled each playthrough

## Lesson

Early Islamic medicine was remarkably advanced. Women like Rufaydah pioneered field hospitals and triage. The Prophet encouraged seeking treatment and used honey, water, and herbal remedies.
