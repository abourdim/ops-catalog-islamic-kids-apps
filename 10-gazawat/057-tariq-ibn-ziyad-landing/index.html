What is Tariq's Landing?

A canvas strategy game about Tariq ibn Ziyad's historic crossing to Iberia in 711 CE. Lead the army across the strait, land at Gibraltar, and begin the era of Al-Andalus!

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once loaded, everything runs in your browser with no internet needed.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Works on any device with a modern browser — phone, tablet, laptop, desktop.

How do I change the theme?

Click any theme button (🖥 🌌 🔥 etc.) in the controls bar.

How do I change the language?

Click EN / FR / ع. Arabic enables right-to-left layout automatically.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser. No data is sent anywhere.

🎯 Purpose

Learn about the historic crossing of Tariq ibn Ziyad to Iberia in 711 CE, the naming of Gibraltar (Jabal Tariq), and the beginning of Al-Andalus civilization.

⚔️ Historical Context

In 711 CE, Tariq ibn Ziyad led approximately 7,000 Muslim soldiers across the Strait of Gibraltar from Morocco to the Iberian Peninsula, beginning a period that would see the flourishing of Islamic civilization in Europe.

🏔 Gibraltar

The name "Gibraltar" comes from the Arabic "Jabal Tariq" (جبل طارق), meaning "Mountain of Tariq", named after the commander who landed there.
