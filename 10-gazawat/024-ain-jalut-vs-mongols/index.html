❓ Help
What is Battle of Ain Jalut?

A canvas strategy game about the historic Battle of Ain Jalut (1260 CE), where the Mamluks of Egypt defeated the Mongol Empire for the first time in a major battle. You command the Muslim army through 4 phases of the battle.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once loaded, everything runs in your browser with no internet needed.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Works on any device with a modern browser — phone, tablet, laptop, desktop.

How do I change the theme?

Click any theme button (🖥 🌌 🔥 etc.) in the controls bar.

How do I change the language?

Click EN / FR / ع. Arabic enables right-to-left layout automatically.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser. No data is sent anywhere.

What are the 4 phases?

Phase 1: Baybars' feigned retreat lures Mongols into the valley. Phase 2: Trigger the ambush from the hills. Phase 3: Qutuz leads the main army charge. Phase 4: The rallying cry "Wa Islamah!" and final charge.

🎯 Purpose

Learn about the Battle of Ain Jalut (1260 CE) — the battle that stopped the Mongol advance into Africa and saved the Muslim world. A lesson in courage, strategy, and unity.

⚔️ Historical Context

In 1258, the Mongols destroyed Baghdad and killed the Abbasid Caliph. They seemed unstoppable — the largest empire in history. But Sultan Qutuz and Commander Baybars of the Mamluk Sultanate of Egypt rallied the Muslim forces. At Ain Jalut (Goliath's Spring) in Palestine, they used the Mongols' own feigned retreat tactic against them.

🗺️ Key Figures

Sultan Qutuz — Commander-in-chief of the Mamluk army. Threw off his helmet and shouted "Wa Islamah!" to rally his troops. Baybars — Led the vanguard. His feigned retreat lured the Mongols into the valley ambush. Kitbuqa — The Mongol general, defeated and killed at Ain Jalut.
