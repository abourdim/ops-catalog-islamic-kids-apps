What is this game?

A turn-based duel game recreating the legendary fight between Ali ibn Abi Talib and Amr ibn Abd Wudd at the Battle of the Trench (Khandaq).

Who was Amr ibn Abd Wudd?

He was the most feared warrior among the Quraysh, said to be worth 1000 horsemen. He crossed the trench and challenged the Muslims to single combat. Only Ali volunteered.

⚔ Battle of the Trench

5 AH (627 CE). A coalition of 10,000 besieged Medina. The Muslims dug a trench on Salman al-Farisi's advice. The siege lasted about a month.

🦁 Ali ibn Abi Talib

The Prophet's cousin and son-in-law. Known as 'Asadullah' (Lion of Allah) and 'Haydar' for his incredible bravery and strength.
