# Khandaq Duel Arena — Ali vs Amr ibn Abd Wudd

Turn-based combat decisions game recreating the legendary duel between Ali ibn Abi Talib and the feared warrior Amr ibn Abd Wudd at the Battle of the Trench (5 AH).

## Concept

Amr ibn Abd Wudd, said to be worth 1000 horsemen, crossed the trench and challenged the Muslims. Only Ali volunteered. Players make Ali's combat decisions through 10 rounds, with HP bars tracking the duel's progress.

## The 10 Rounds

| # | Round | Decision |
|---|-------|----------|
| 1 | The Challenge | Ali volunteers three times |
| 2 | The Prophet's Words | All of faith vs all of disbelief |
| 3 | Ali's Response | Fearless confidence in Allah |
| 4 | Three Offers | Call to Islam first |
| 5 | Dismount Request | Fight on equal ground |
| 6 | Amr's First Strike | Patient defense, wait for opening |
| 7 | The Decisive Moment | Strike with Dhul-Fiqar |
| 8 | After Victory | Honor — does not strip armor |
| 9 | Prophet's Comment | Strike worth more than all worship |
| 10 | The Lesson | True courage is standing for truth |

## Features

- Single HTML file, zero dependencies, fully offline
- 8 CSS themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Arctic, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Sand
- Trilingual: English, French, Arabic (with RTL support)
- Splash screen with Bismillah
- Fighter cards with HP bars that change during the duel
- Turn-based sequential combat narrative
- Sidebar help panel with FAQ, How-To, and Wiki tabs
- Web Audio API sound effects
- High score persistence via localStorage

## Lesson

True courage means standing for truth when everyone else is paralyzed by fear. Ali's bravery, honor, and reliance on Allah changed the course of the entire siege. One faithful servant with Allah's support is worth more than any army.
