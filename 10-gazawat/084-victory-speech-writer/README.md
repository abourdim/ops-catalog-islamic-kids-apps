# Victory Speech Writer — Humility & Gratitude in Victory

Interactive fill-in-the-blank game where players compose victory addresses following Islamic principles of humility, gratitude to Allah, and mercy — based on real speeches from the Prophet's time.

## Concept

Islam teaches that victory comes from Allah alone. The Prophet entered Mecca with his head bowed in humility, not raised in arrogance. Players complete 10 victory speeches by choosing words of humility over boasting.

## The 10 Speeches

| # | Context | Key Principle |
|---|---------|---------------|
| 1 | Conquest of Mecca address | Mercy and freedom for enemies |
| 2 | After Battle of Badr | Victory from Allah alone |
| 3 | Addressing prisoners | Feed and clothe them |
| 4 | Entering Mecca | Humble posture in triumph |
| 5 | Victory announcement | Credit to Allah, not army |
| 6 | Post-battle conduct | Mercy to all |
| 7 | Liberating a land | Justice and freedom |
| 8 | Thanksgiving after victory | Sujud al-Shukr first |
| 9 | Commemorating the fallen | Martyrs alive with Allah |
| 10 | Warning against arrogance | Allah dislikes the proud |

## Features

- Single HTML file, zero dependencies, fully offline
- 8 CSS themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Arctic, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Sand
- Trilingual: English, French, Arabic (with RTL support)
- Splash screen with Bismillah
- Sidebar help panel with FAQ, How-To, and Wiki tabs
- Web Audio API sound effects (no audio files)
- Unique fill-in-the-blank speech mechanic
- High score persistence via localStorage
- Theme/language/sound preferences persisted

## Lesson

True victory in Islam is defined by humility, gratitude to Allah, and mercy to the defeated. The greatest leaders bow lowest before Allah at the moment of their greatest triumph.
