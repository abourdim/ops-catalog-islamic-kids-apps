What is Victory Speech Writer?

A fill-in-the-blank game where you compose victory addresses following Islamic principles of humility and gratitude, based on real speeches from Islamic history.

Why humility in victory?

Islam teaches that victory comes from Allah alone. Boasting and arrogance are forbidden. The Prophet entered Mecca with his head bowed in humility.

🕌 Humility in Victory

When the Prophet entered Mecca victorious, he bowed his head so low it nearly touched his camel's back, in gratitude and humility to Allah.

🙏 Gratitude to Allah

Every victory in Islam is attributed to Allah, not to the strength of the army. The Quran says: 'Victory is only from Allah.'
