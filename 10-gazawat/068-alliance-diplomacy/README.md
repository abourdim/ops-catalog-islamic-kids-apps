# Alliance Diplomacy — The Art of Treaties

Interactive dialogue-based negotiation game where players build alliances with Arabian tribes through wise diplomatic choices.

## Concept

Negotiate alliances with 6 Arabian tribes, each with unique values and concerns. Choose dialogue options that demonstrate justice, generosity, and wisdom. Each tribe has 2 rounds of negotiation, and your responses determine whether an alliance is formed.

## The 6 Tribes

| # | Tribe | Values | Key Concern |
|---|-------|--------|-------------|
| 1 | Banu Khuzaa | Loyalty, protection | Seeking security from Quraysh |
| 2 | Banu Thaqif | Commerce, prosperity | Protecting trade and elder status |
| 3 | Banu Tamim | Honor, independence | Maintaining tribal identity |
| 4 | Banu Aws | Peace, justice | Ending conflict with Khazraj |
| 5 | Banu Ghatafan | Bravery, compassion | Protection of non-combatants |
| 6 | Banu Kalb | Trade, tolerance | Rights of Christian members |

## Features

- Single HTML file, zero dependencies, fully offline
- 8 CSS themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Arctic, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Sand
- Trilingual: English, French, Arabic (with RTL support)
- Splash screen with Bismillah
- Sidebar help panel with FAQ, How-To, and Wiki tabs
- Web Audio API sound effects (no audio files)
- Keyboard shortcuts (1-3 for choices, Enter to continue)
- High score persistence via localStorage
- Tribes shuffled each playthrough

## Lesson

The Prophet (peace be upon him) built alliances through justice, mercy, and keeping promises. The Treaty of Hudaybiyyah and the Constitution of Medina demonstrate that true strength comes from wise diplomacy, not force.
