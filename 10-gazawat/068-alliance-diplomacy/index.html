What is Alliance Diplomacy?

A dialogue-based negotiation game where you build alliances with Arabian tribes through wise choices. Each tribe presents unique challenges and values different approaches.

How does alliance strength work?

Your dialogue choices affect trust. Choose responses that show justice, generosity, and wisdom. Strong alliances provide more coalition strength.

Are the tribes historical?

The tribes are inspired by the various Arabian tribes that interacted with early Muslims, reflecting real diplomatic dynamics of the era.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Fully self-contained in your browser.

🤝 Treaty of Hudaybiyyah

The Prophet (peace be upon him) demonstrated masterful diplomacy at Hudaybiyyah (6 AH). What seemed like a compromise became a great victory, as the peace allowed Islam to spread rapidly through dialogue.

📜 Constitution of Medina

The Sahifat al-Madinah was one of the first written constitutions, establishing a multi-tribal alliance with clear rights and duties for all parties — an extraordinary diplomatic achievement.

🏳 Tribal Alliances

Building alliances was crucial for early Muslims. The Prophet sent envoys to tribes across Arabia, and many alliances were built through fair dealing and keeping promises.
