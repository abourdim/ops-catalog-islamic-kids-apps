# Uhud Shield Wall — The Lesson of the Archers

Interactive strategic formation game based on the Battle of Uhud (3 AH). Players make tactical decisions through 10 phases of the battle, learning the critical lesson of obedience and discipline.

## Concept

The Battle of Uhud was won then lost because archers abandoned their post to collect spoils. Players experience each phase of the battle and must make the correct strategic choice, learning why obedience to the leader is essential.

## The 10 Phases

| # | Phase | Decision |
|---|-------|----------|
| 1 | Pre-Battle Consultation | Accept majority opinion (shura) |
| 2 | Mount Uhud Position | Mountain protects the rear |
| 3 | Archers on Mount Ainain | Do not leave — absolute order |
| 4 | Initial Victory | Stay at post despite spoils |
| 5 | Khalid's Cavalry Attack | Muslims caught between two forces |
| 6 | Prophet is Wounded | Companions form human shield |
| 7 | Anas ibn al-Nadr | Charges alone into the enemy |
| 8 | Hamza's Martyrdom | Prophet weeps but forbids mutilation |
| 9 | Retreat to Uhud | Prayer first, even when wounded |
| 10 | The Lesson | Obedience and discipline win battles |

## Features

- Single HTML file, zero dependencies, fully offline
- 8 CSS themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Arctic, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Sand
- Trilingual: English, French, Arabic (with RTL support)
- Splash screen with Bismillah
- Sequential battle phases
- Sidebar help panel with FAQ, How-To, and Wiki tabs
- Web Audio API sound effects
- High score persistence via localStorage

## Lesson

Uhud teaches that obedience to the leader and discipline are essential. Disobedience turned certain victory into a painful lesson, but also showed incredible bravery — companions who shielded the Prophet with their own bodies.
