What is Uhud Shield Wall?

A strategic formation game based on the Battle of Uhud (3 AH). Position shields and archers, and learn the crucial lesson when archers abandoned their post.

What happened at Uhud?

Muslims were winning until archers left Mount Ainain to collect spoils, allowing Khalid ibn al-Walid's cavalry to attack from behind, turning victory into a setback.

⚔ Battle of Uhud

3 AH (625 CE). 700 Muslims vs 3000 Quraysh. The battle taught that disobedience leads to defeat, even when victory seems certain.

🏹 The 50 Archers

The Prophet placed 50 archers on Mount Ainain with strict orders: 'Do not leave this position even if you see birds eating our flesh.' Most left when they thought the battle was won.
