What is Battle of Badr?

A strategy board game where you position the Muslim army at the Battle of Badr (2 AH / 624 CE). Learn about the Prophet's strategy and trust in Allah through interactive gameplay.

How do I win?

You must: 1) Control all 3 wells (deny water to Quraysh), 2) Position units on high ground, 3) Maintain proper defensive formation. All three conditions unlock victory!

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once loaded, everything runs in your browser with no internet needed.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Touch drag works on mobile. Works on any device with a modern browser.

How do I change the theme?

Click any theme button in the controls bar.

How do I change the language?

Click EN / FR / ع. Arabic enables right-to-left layout automatically.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser. No data is sent anywhere.

The Battle

The Battle of Badr (17 Ramadan, 2 AH / March 624 CE) was the first major military victory for the Muslim community. 313 Muslims faced approximately 1000 Quraysh soldiers near the wells of Badr.

The Strategy

Hubab ibn al-Mundhir advised the Prophet to camp near the wells closest to the Quraysh, controlling the water supply. The Muslims filled their own water containers and blocked the remaining wells, denying water to the enemy.

Divine Support

The Prophet made dua to Allah, and Allah sent angels to support the Muslim army. Rain fell to firm the ground under the Muslims while making the Quraysh side muddy and slippery.
