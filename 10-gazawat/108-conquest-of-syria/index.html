What is this game?

A multi-front campaign planner about the Muslim conquest of Syria (13-15 AH / 634-636 CE) under Khalid ibn al-Walid and Abu Ubayda ibn al-Jarrah.

What was the Battle of Yarmouk?

One of history's most decisive battles. 40,000 Muslims defeated 200,000+ Byzantines, ending Roman rule in Syria forever.

Who was Khalid ibn al-Walid?

Called "The Sword of Allah," Khalid was one of history's greatest military commanders, never losing a battle in his career.

Battle of Yarmouk
