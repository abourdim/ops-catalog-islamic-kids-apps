# Conquest of Syria — Multiple Fronts

Interactive campaign planner for the Muslim conquest of Syria under Khalid ibn al-Walid and Abu Ubayda (13-15 AH / 634-636 CE).

## Concept

The Syrian campaign featured some of Islamic history's most dramatic events: Khalid's legendary desert crossing, the decisive Battle of Yarmouk, and Umar's humble entry into Jerusalem. The player manages multiple fronts, commanding decisions, and battle tactics.

## The 8 Phases

| # | Phase | Key Lesson |
|---|-------|------------|
| 1 | Khalid's Desert Crossing | Bold moves create surprise |
| 2 | Unifying Command | Concentration of force is crucial |
| 3 | Battle of Ajnadayn | Mobile cavalry flanks win battles |
| 4 | Command Change | True character accepts demotion gracefully |
| 5 | Yarmouk Day 1 | Morale systems keep lines unbroken |
| 6 | Yarmouk Decisive Day | Cut off retreat to achieve total victory |
| 7 | Surrender of Damascus | Peaceful treaties protect people's rights |
| 8 | Umar Enters Jerusalem | Humility is the greatest strength |

## Features

- Single HTML file, zero dependencies, fully offline
- 8 CSS themes with canvas map of Syria/Levant
- Trilingual with RTL support, Bismillah splash screen
- Web Audio API sounds, localStorage persistence

## Lesson

Unity of command, bold innovation, and humble governance create lasting victories. True strength lies in character.
