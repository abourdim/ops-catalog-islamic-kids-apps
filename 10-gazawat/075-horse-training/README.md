# Horse Training

Interactive mini-game collection where kids train war horses by completing reaction-based challenges to improve speed, endurance, discipline, and agility stats.

## Concept

The Prophet (peace be upon him) said: "Goodness is tied to the forelocks of horses until the Day of Judgment." Arabian horses were central to Islamic cavalry forces. Khalid ibn al-Walid's legendary cavalry maneuvers at Yarmouk demonstrate the importance of well-trained horses. Players train their own war horse through various exercises.

## Gameplay

1. Start with a horse that has base stats of 10 in each category
2. Choose from 4 training exercises, each improving a different stat
3. Each exercise is a 3-round reaction mini-game
4. Wait for the zone to turn green, then click as fast as possible
5. Faster reactions earn more stat points (up to +5 per round)
6. You have 8 total training sessions to maximize your horse's abilities
7. Final score is the sum of all four stats

## Training Exercises

| Exercise | Stat | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Sprint Training | Speed | React fast to the signal |
| Endurance Run | Endurance | Sustain quick reactions |
| Halt Drill | Discipline | Stop on command precisely |
| Obstacle Dodge | Agility | Quick reflex challenges |

## Features

- Single HTML file, zero dependencies, fully offline
- 8 CSS themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Arctic, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Sand
- Trilingual: English, French, Arabic (with RTL support)
- Splash screen with Bismillah
- Sidebar help panel with FAQ, How-To, and Wiki tabs
- Web Audio API sound effects including gallop sounds
- Reaction-time mini-games with millisecond precision
- Visual stat bars showing horse progress
- High score persistence via localStorage

## Lesson

Horses hold a special place in Islamic tradition. The Quran mentions them in Surah Al-Adiyat and Al-Anfal. Caring for and training animals well is part of Islamic ethics — the Prophet (peace be upon him) taught kindness to all creatures while also preparing for defense.
