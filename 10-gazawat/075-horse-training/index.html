What is Horse Training?

A mini-game collection where you train a war horse by completing quick reaction challenges. Each exercise improves a specific stat.

Why horses in Islam?

The Prophet (peace be upon him) said: "Goodness is tied to the forelocks of horses until the Day of Judgment." Horses held a special place in Islamic military and culture.

How do I play the mini-games?

Each training exercise is a quick reaction game. Click/tap at the right moment when the zone turns green. Faster reactions earn more stat points.

Arabian Horse

Arabian horses are one of the oldest and most prized breeds. Known for endurance, intelligence, and a strong bond with riders. They were central to Islamic cavalry forces.

Horses in the Quran

Surah Al-Adiyat (100) begins by swearing by war horses charging at dawn. Surah Al-Anfal (8:60) mentions preparing horses to deter enemies.

Cavalry in Battles

Khalid ibn al-Walid's cavalry maneuvers at the Battle of Yarmouk are considered among history's finest. Skilled horse training was essential to these victories.
