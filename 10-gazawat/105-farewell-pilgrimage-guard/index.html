Hajjat al-Wada

In 10 AH (632 CE), the Prophet ﷺ performed his only complete Hajj with over 100,000 Muslims. He taught them the rituals and delivered the Farewell Sermon.

The Farewell Sermon

Delivered at Arafat, this sermon outlined fundamental rights: sanctity of life, property, and honor; equality of all people; women's rights; and the Quran as eternal guidance.

Logistics

Managing 100,000+ pilgrims required extraordinary planning for water, shelter, routes, and safety across multiple Hajj sites.
