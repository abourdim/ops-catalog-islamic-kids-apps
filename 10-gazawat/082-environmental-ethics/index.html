What is this game?

A decision game about protecting the environment during military campaigns, based on Abu Bakr al-Siddiq's famous 10 rules given to Usamah ibn Zayd's army.

Yes. These rules are recorded in authentic historical sources and represent the first environmental code of conduct in warfare.

🌱 Abu Bakr's 10 Rules

When Abu Bakr sent Usamah ibn Zayd's army to Syria, he gave 10 commandments that form the first environmental and humanitarian rules of warfare.

🌍 Environmental Protection

Islam forbids destroying trees, poisoning wells, killing animals unnecessarily, and burning crops during war.
