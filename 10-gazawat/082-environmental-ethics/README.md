# Environmental Ethics in War — Abu Bakr's 10 Rules

Interactive decision game about protecting trees, crops, water sources, and animals during military campaigns, based on Abu Bakr al-Siddiq's famous 10 commandments.

## Concept

When Abu Bakr sent Usamah ibn Zayd's army to Syria, he gave 10 commandments that form the world's first environmental and humanitarian code of warfare. The player faces scenarios and must identify which of Abu Bakr's rules applies.

## The 10 Rules

| # | Rule | Scenario |
|---|------|----------|
| 1 | Do not cut fruit-bearing trees | Troops want to cut an orchard for camp |
| 2 | Do not poison water sources | Commander suggests poisoning enemy well |
| 3 | Do not slaughter animals except for food | Soldiers want to kill enemy livestock |
| 4 | Do not destroy crops or farmland | General orders burning wheat fields |
| 5 | Do not demolish buildings unnecessarily | Soldiers want to demolish houses |
| 6 | Do not harm monks or worship places | Troops discover a church |
| 7 | Do not kill women, children, elderly | Women accused of relaying messages |
| 8 | Do not betray a treaty | Opportunity to attack during ceasefire |
| 9 | Do not mutilate the dead | Soldiers want to display enemy bodies |
| 10 | Fight only those who fight you | Army corners town of farmers |

## Features

- Single HTML file, zero dependencies, fully offline
- 8 CSS themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Arctic, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Sand
- Trilingual: English, French, Arabic (with RTL support)
- Splash screen with Bismillah
- Sidebar help panel with FAQ, How-To, and Wiki tabs
- Web Audio API sound effects (no audio files)
- Keyboard shortcuts (1-3 for choices, Enter to continue)
- High score persistence via localStorage
- Theme/language/sound preferences persisted
- Rules shuffled each playthrough

## Lesson

Abu Bakr's 10 rules represent the first environmental code of warfare. Islam teaches that even in war, we are trustees (khulafa) of the Earth and must protect Allah's creation.
