# Battle of Mu'tah — Three Commanders, One Impossible Battle

> **Workshop-Diy** — Islamic Kids Apps Catalog
> Category: 10-gazawat / 007-mutah-3-commanders

## Overview

Canvas-based interactive battle narrative for Muslim kids about the Battle of Mu'tah (8 AH / 629 CE). Players lead the Muslim army through 4 phases with different commanders, making tactical decisions at each stage.

## Gameplay

### Phase 1: Zayd ibn Harithah
- First commander appointed by the Prophet (peace be upon him)
- Player directs troops into the charge
- Zayd fights bravely and falls as a martyr

### Phase 2: Ja'far ibn Abi Talib
- The flag passes to Ja'far
- He loses his right arm, fights with his left
- Loses both arms, holds flag with his stumps
- Falls as a martyr — Allah replaces his arms with wings in Jannah (Ja'far at-Tayyar)

### Phase 3: Abdullah ibn Rawahah
- Third appointed commander receives the flag
- Hesitates, then encourages himself and charges
- Falls as a martyr

### Phase 4: Khalid ibn Walid — The Sword of Allah
- Soldiers choose Khalid to lead
- Three tactical sub-phases:
  1. **Night:** Swap left and right flanks
  2. **Dawn:** Enemy thinks reinforcements arrived
  3. **Retreat:** Controlled withdrawal saves the entire army

### Epilogue
- The Prophet (peace be upon him) in Medina tells companions what happened through divine revelation

## Lessons
- Bravery and sacrifice in the path of Allah
- Smart leadership under impossible odds
- 3,000 vs 200,000 — faith and wisdom prevail
