⚔️ Battle of Mu'tah

Fought in Jumada al-Ula 8 AH (629 CE) in Mu'tah, southern Jordan. The Muslim army of 3,000 faced the combined Roman-Ghassanid force of 200,000. Despite the odds, the Muslims fought with immense bravery.

🏴 Three Commanders

The Prophet ﷺ appointed Zayd ibn Harithah as commander. If he fell, Ja'far ibn Abi Talib would take over. If Ja'far fell, Abdullah ibn Rawahah would lead. All three were martyred in battle.

🦅 Ja'far at-Tayyar

Ja'far held the flag after Zayd fell. He fought until he lost his right arm, then held it with his left. When he lost that too, he held the flag with his stumps. The Prophet ﷺ said Allah replaced his arms with wings in Jannah — hence his title "at-Tayyar" (the Flier).

⚔️ Khalid's Genius

After all three commanders fell, the soldiers chose Khalid ibn Walid. He swapped left and right flanks at night. At dawn, the enemy thought Muslim reinforcements had arrived. Khalid then executed a controlled retreat, saving the entire army.
