❓ Help
What is Mountain Pass?

A tower-defense game where you defend a narrow mountain pass from enemy waves. Place archers on cliffs, roll boulders, and position infantry at choke points. Inspired by the strategic use of terrain at the battles of Uhud and Khandaq.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once loaded, everything runs in your browser with no internet needed.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Works on any device with a modern browser — phone, tablet, laptop, desktop. Tap to place units.

How do I change the theme?

Click any theme button (🖥 🌌 🔥 etc.) in the controls bar.

How do I change the language?

Click EN / FR / ع. Arabic enables right-to-left layout automatically.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser. No data is sent anywhere.

🎯 Purpose

Learn how terrain shaped key battles in early Islamic history. At Uhud, archers on a hill guarded the Muslim rear. At Khandaq, a trench blocked cavalry. This game teaches strategic thinking through terrain-based defense.

🏹 Units

Archers (10g) — ranged, place on cliffs, shoot arrows at enemies. Boulders (15g) — one-time use, roll down the slope and crush enemies in a line. Infantry (20g) — melee, place on the pass to block and fight enemies at the choke point.

🎨 Themes

8 built-in themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Arctic, Sand.

🌐 Languages

Trilingual: English, Francais, العربية. Arabic auto-enables RTL layout.

🔊 Sound

Web Audio API — no files needed. Arrow shots, boulder crashes, victory melody. Toggle with 🔊 button.

🔗 Historical Reference

At Uhud, the Prophet (peace be upon him) placed 50 archers on a hill to guard the rear. At Khandaq, a trench was dug to use terrain as defense. This game captures how terrain advantage was essential in these historic encounters.

🔒 Privacy

Local-first. No tracking, no analytics, no external calls. All data stays in your browser.
