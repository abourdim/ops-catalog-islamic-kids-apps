What is this game about?

An interactive timeline of the Battle of Badr focusing on moments of divine support — the angels, the rain, the dust, and the miraculous victory.

Were angels really at Badr?

Yes. The Quran (3:123-125) confirms that Allah sent 3000 then 5000 angels to support the Muslims at Badr. Multiple companions reported seeing them.
