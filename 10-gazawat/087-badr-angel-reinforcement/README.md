# Badr: Angel Reinforcement — Divine Support at Badr

Interactive canvas-based timeline of the Battle of Badr focusing on moments of divine support — from the angels descending to the miraculous victory.

## Concept

The Quran confirms that Allah sent thousands of angels to support the Muslims at Badr. Players explore 10 key moments through a visual canvas scene that evolves as the battle progresses, answering questions about each moment of divine intervention.

## The 10 Moments

| # | Moment | Key Detail |
|---|--------|------------|
| 1 | Armies Face Each Other | 313 vs 1000+ |
| 2 | Prophet's Intense Dua | Abu Bakr comforts him |
| 3 | Angels Descend | 1000 angels promised |
| 4 | Angels with White Turbans | Visible to companions |
| 5 | Rain from Heaven | Purification and strength |
| 6 | Blessed Sleep | Security from Allah |
| 7 | Handful of Dust | Allah threw, not you |
| 8 | Strike Above the Necks | Divine command to angels |
| 9 | Enemy Flees | 70 killed, 70 captured |
| 10 | Yawm al-Furqan | Day of Distinction |

## Features

- Single HTML file, zero dependencies, fully offline
- Canvas-based battle scene that evolves with each moment
- Visual timeline with dot navigation
- 8 CSS themes with dynamic canvas rendering
- Trilingual: English, French, Arabic (with RTL support)
- Splash screen with Bismillah
- Sidebar help panel with FAQ, How-To, and Wiki tabs
- Web Audio API sound effects
- High score persistence via localStorage

## Lesson

Badr proves that victory comes from Allah alone. Despite being outnumbered 3 to 1, the Muslims won through faith, prayer, and divine support.
