❓ Help
What is Day of Mercy?

An interactive game about the Prophet's ﷺ merciful decisions after the Conquest of Mecca (Fath Makkah). You see real historical cases of former enemies and guess how the Prophet ﷺ responded.

Is this historically accurate?

Yes. All 12 cases are based on authentic historical sources about the Conquest of Mecca in 8 AH / 630 CE.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once loaded, everything runs in your browser with no internet needed.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Works on any device with a modern browser — phone, tablet, laptop, desktop.

How do I change the theme?

Click any theme button (🖥 🌌 🔥 etc.) in the controls bar.

How do I change the language?

Click EN / FR / ع. Arabic enables right-to-left layout automatically.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser. No data is sent anywhere.

🕌 Conquest of Mecca

In 8 AH (630 CE), the Prophet ﷺ entered Mecca peacefully with 10,000 companions. Despite years of persecution, he chose mercy over revenge.

🕊 The General Amnesty

The Prophet ﷺ asked Quraysh: "What do you think I will do with you?" They said: "You are a generous brother." He replied: "Go, you are all free!" (اذْهَبُوا فَأَنْتُمُ الطُّلَقَاءُ)

🎯 Purpose

This game teaches that true strength lies in mercy and forgiveness. Many former enemies became devoted Muslims and champions of Islam after being forgiven.

🎨 Themes

8 built-in themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Arctic, Sand.

🌐 Languages

Trilingual: English, Francais, العربية. Arabic auto-enables RTL layout.

🔊 Sound

Web Audio API — no files needed. Toggle with the speaker button.

🔒 Privacy

Local-first. No tracking, no analytics, no external calls. All data stays in your browser.
