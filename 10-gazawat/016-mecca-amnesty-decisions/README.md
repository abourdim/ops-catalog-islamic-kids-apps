# Day of Mercy — Mecca Amnesty Decisions

Interactive scenario-based game about the Prophet's (peace be upon him) merciful decisions after the Conquest of Mecca (Fath Makkah, 8 AH / 630 CE).

## Concept

After entering Mecca peacefully with 10,000 companions, the Prophet addressed Quraysh:

> "What do you think I will do with you?"
> They said: "You are a generous brother, son of a generous brother."
> He said: "Go, you are all free!" (اذْهَبُوا فَأَنْتُمُ الطُّلَقَاءُ)

The player then sees 12 individual cases of former enemies brought before the Prophet and must guess the verdict: **Forgive**, **Punish**, or **Conditional** forgiveness.

## The 12 Cases

| # | Person | Description | Actual Decision |
|---|--------|-------------|-----------------|
| 1 | Abu Sufyan | Leader of opposition for years | Forgiven — house declared safe zone |
| 2 | Hind bint Utbah | Mutilated Hamza's body | Forgiven — later became Muslim |
| 3 | Wahshi ibn Harb | Killed Hamza at Uhud | Conditional — forgiven upon accepting Islam |
| 4 | Ikrimah ibn Abu Jahl | Son of Islam's fiercest enemy | Forgiven — became great commander |
| 5 | Safwan ibn Umayyah | Plotted against Muslims | Conditional — given 4 months, accepted Islam willingly |
| 6 | Suhayl ibn Amr | Hudaybiyyah negotiator | Forgiven — later defended Islam with his eloquence |
| 7 | Ka'b ibn Zuhayr | Poet who mocked Islam | Forgiven — recited praise poem, given Prophet's cloak |
| 8 | Abdullah ibn Sa'd | Former scribe who apostated | Conditional — forgiven through Uthman's intercession |
| 9 | Abu Quhafah | Abu Bakr's elderly father | Forgiven — treated with great respect and kindness |
| 10 | Mu'awiyah ibn Abi Sufyan | Son of Abu Sufyan | Forgiven — later became scribe of revelation |
| 11 | Habar ibn al-Aswad | Attacked Prophet's daughter Zaynab | Forgiven — despite personal pain |
| 12 | Fudhalah ibn Umayr | Attempted assassination during Tawaf | Forgiven — heart transformed instantly |

## Features

- Single HTML file, zero dependencies, fully offline
- 8 CSS themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Arctic, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Sand
- Trilingual: English, French, Arabic (with RTL support)
- Splash screen with Bismillah
- Sidebar help panel with FAQ, How-To, and Wiki tabs
- Web Audio API sound effects (no audio files)
- Keyboard shortcuts (1-3 for choices, Enter to continue)
- High score persistence via localStorage
- Theme/language/sound preferences persisted
- Cases shuffled each playthrough
- Score tracking with progress bar

## Lesson

Mercy in power: true strength lies in forgiveness. The Prophet transformed enemies into allies through compassion, demonstrating that the greatest victory is winning hearts.
