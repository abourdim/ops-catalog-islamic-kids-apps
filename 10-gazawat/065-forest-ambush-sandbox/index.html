What is Forest Ambush Sandbox?

A stealth strategy game where you hide units in a forest and spring ambushes on enemy columns marching along a path. Units hidden near trees get a concealment bonus.

How does concealment work?

Units placed near trees are hidden until enemies come within range. Hidden units deal double damage on their first attack (ambush bonus).

What are rope traps?

Rope traps slow down enemies when triggered, giving your ambush units more time to attack. Place them on the path for maximum effect.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Fully self-contained in your browser.

🌲 Ambush Tactics

The Prophet (peace be upon him) used strategic scouts and ambush tactics. The expedition of Abdullah ibn Jahsh was one of the earliest intelligence and reconnaissance missions in Islamic history.

🎯 Surprise Element

Islamic military strategy emphasized preparation and the element of surprise. The night march before Badr and the secret approach to Mecca are examples of strategic movement and concealment.

⚔ Chokepoints

Controlling narrow passages and paths was essential. At the Battle of the Trench (Khandaq), a single trench created an impassable chokepoint that nullified a much larger army.
