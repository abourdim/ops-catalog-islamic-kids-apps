❓ Help
What is Lion of Allah?

An interactive canvas story about Hamza ibn Abdul Muttalib (may Allah be pleased with him), the uncle of Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him). Learn about his bravery, sacrifice, and the lessons from his life.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once loaded, everything runs in your browser with no internet needed.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Works on any device with a modern browser.

How do I change the theme?

Click any theme button in the controls bar.

How do I change the language?

Click EN / FR / ع. Arabic enables right-to-left layout automatically.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser. No data is sent anywhere.

Is there a quiz?

Yes! After the 6 story phases, you will answer 5 questions about Hamza's life to test your knowledge.

1

Click Start Mission on the splash screen

2

Read each phase of the story and enjoy the canvas illustrations

3

Click "Next" or press Enter/Space to advance through the story

4

After the story, answer 5 quiz questions (use keys 1-4 or click)

5

Learn the lessons of bravery, truth, forgiveness, and redemption

6

Click Restart to experience the story again

Purpose

Teach Muslim children the story of Hamza ibn Abdul Muttalib, Lion of Allah and Lion of His Messenger.

Themes

8 built-in themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Arctic, Sand.

Languages

Trilingual: English, Francais, Arabic. Arabic auto-enables RTL layout.

Sound

Web Audio API. Toggle with the speaker button.

Key Lessons

Bravery in standing for truth. Sacrifice for the sake of Allah. Even Wahshi was forgiven when he accepted Islam - showing the door of repentance is always open.

Privacy

Local-first. No tracking, no analytics, no external calls. All data stays in your browser.
