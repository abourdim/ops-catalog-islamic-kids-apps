Lesson: Tawheed Restored

Prophet Ibrahim (peace be upon him) built the Kaaba as a house of pure monotheism — worship of Allah alone. Over centuries, people filled it with 360 idols. When Prophet Muhammad ﷺ entered Makkah peacefully, he pointed at each idol, reciting the verse from Surah Al-Isra (17:81), and every idol fell. This restored Ibrahim's legacy of Tawheed — the Oneness of Allah — which is the foundation of Islam.

❓ Help
What is Purify the Kaaba?

A game about the historic event of Fath Makkah (Conquest of Makkah) when Prophet Muhammad ﷺ removed 360 idols from around the Kaaba, restoring it to the house of Tawheed that Prophet Ibrahim (peace be upon him) originally built.

How do I play?

Tap or click on the idol markers around the Kaaba to remove them. In Sweep mode, drag across multiple idols. Special idols like Hubal, al-Lat, al-Uzza, and Manat require extra taps or are larger.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once loaded, everything runs in your browser with no internet needed.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Works on any device with a modern browser — phone, tablet, laptop, desktop.

How do I change the theme?

Click any theme button (🖥 🌌 🔥 etc.) in the controls bar.

How do I change the language?

Click EN / FR / ع. Arabic enables right-to-left layout automatically.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser. No data is sent anywhere.

🕋 The Kaaba

The Kaaba was originally built by Prophet Ibrahim and his son Ismail (peace be upon them) as a house of worship for Allah alone. Over time, the people of Makkah filled and surrounded it with 360 idols.

⚔️ Fath Makkah

In 8 AH (630 CE), Prophet Muhammad ﷺ entered Makkah peacefully with 10,000 companions. He went to the Kaaba and personally pointed at each idol, which fell as he recited Quran 17:81.

🗿 Hubal

The chief idol of the Quraysh, placed atop the Kaaba. It was the largest and most prominent idol, made of red agate in human form. It was the last to be destroyed.

🗿 al-Lat, al-Uzza, Manat

Three prominent idols mentioned in the Quran (53:19-20). al-Lat was worshipped in Taif, al-Uzza near Makkah, and Manat between Makkah and Madinah. They were considered "daughters of God" by the polytheists.

📢 Bilal's Adhan

After the purification, Bilal ibn Rabah climbed atop the Kaaba and called the Adhan — the first ever called from that sacred place. A powerful moment symbolizing the triumph of Tawheed.
