❓ Help
What is Saladin's Spies?

A strategy intelligence game where you deploy spy agents across the Crusader states to gather intelligence for Saladin's campaign leading to the Battle of Hattin (1187 CE).

How do I place spies?

Click on a spy in the tray below the map, then click on a city on the map to deploy them there. Each spy gathers different types of intel from the city they are placed in.

What is detection risk?

Placing too many spies in one region increases the chance of detection. If detected, you lose intel from that area. Spread your spies wisely across all three Crusader states.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once loaded, everything runs in your browser with no internet needed.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Works on any device with a modern browser — phone, tablet, laptop, desktop.

How do I change the theme?

Click any theme button in the controls bar.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser. No data is sent anywhere.

🎯 Purpose

Learn about the importance of intelligence and preparation in Saladin's campaign. Knowledge is power — preparation wins wars.

🗺 The Crusader States

Kingdom of Jerusalem, County of Tripoli, and Principality of Antioch — the three main Crusader territories in the Levant during the 12th century.

⚔ Battle of Hattin

In 1187 CE, Saladin's well-prepared army decisively defeated the Crusader forces at the Horns of Hattin, leading to the liberation of Jerusalem.

🎨 Themes

8 built-in themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Arctic, Sand.

🌐 Languages

Trilingual: English, Francais, العربية. Arabic auto-enables RTL layout.

🔊 Sound

Web Audio API — no files needed. Toggle with the sound button.

🔒 Privacy

Local-first. No tracking, no analytics, no external calls. All data stays in your browser.
