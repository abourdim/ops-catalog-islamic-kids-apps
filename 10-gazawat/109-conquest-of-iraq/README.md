# Conquest of Iraq — Chain of Battles

Interactive strategy game about Khalid ibn al-Walid's lightning campaigns in Iraq (12-13 AH / 633-634 CE).

## Concept

Khalid ibn al-Walid fought a remarkable chain of battles in rapid succession along the Euphrates, defeating multiple Sassanid armies. The player manages battle tactics, river crossings, and troop movements through 8 consecutive engagements.

## The 8 Battles

| # | Battle | Key Lesson |
|---|--------|------------|
| 1 | Battle of Chains | Exploit enemy's rigid formations |
| 2 | Battle of Mazar | Speed and momentum are weapons |
| 3 | Battle of Walaja | Double envelopment tactics |
| 4 | Battle of Ullais | Dawn strikes with overwhelming speed |
| 5 | Siege of Hira | Negotiation shows wisdom, not weakness |
| 6 | Battle of Ain al-Tamr | Swift assault saves prisoners |
| 7 | Euphrates Crossing | Improvise with available resources |
| 8 | Founding of Basra | Strategic location for lasting legacy |

## Features

- Single HTML file, zero dependencies, fully offline
- 8 CSS themes with canvas map of Iraq showing Tigris/Euphrates
- Battle counter tracking the chain of engagements
- Trilingual with RTL support, Bismillah splash screen
- Web Audio API sounds, localStorage persistence

## Lesson

Speed, improvisation, and relentless momentum defeat larger armies. Wisdom equals courage — knowing when to fight and when to negotiate.
