What is this game?

A strategy game about Khalid ibn al-Walid's rapid campaigns in Iraq (12-13 AH / 633-634 CE), where he won a remarkable chain of battles against Sassanid forces.

How many battles did Khalid fight in Iraq?

Khalid fought and won multiple battles in rapid succession, earning his reputation as one of history's greatest military commanders.

Khalid in Iraq

Caliph Abu Bakr sent Khalid to Iraq in 12 AH. In a stunning campaign, he defeated multiple Sassanid armies in quick succession along the Euphrates.

Battle of Chains

The first battle was called "Battle of Chains" because the Persian commander Hormuz chained his soldiers together to prevent retreat — it backfired spectacularly.

River Strategy

Iraq's twin rivers (Tigris and Euphrates) made river crossings a critical strategic challenge throughout the campaign.
