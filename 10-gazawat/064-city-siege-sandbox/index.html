What is City Siege Sandbox?

A drag-and-drop siege planning game where you build and position siege equipment to breach fortified city walls, inspired by historical Islamic siege warfare.

How do siege tools differ?

Ladders scale walls directly, battering rams damage gates, catapults destroy walls from range, and shield walls protect other equipment from archer fire.

What are wall defenders?

Defenders fire arrows at your equipment as it approaches. Shield walls reduce damage to nearby units.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser.

🏰 Siege of Ta'if

After the Battle of Hunayn (8 AH), the Prophet (peace be upon him) besieged Ta'if using catapults (manjaniq). This was one of the earliest recorded uses of siege equipment in Islamic military history.

💥 The Manjaniq (Catapult)

Muslim armies adopted and improved siege technology. The manjaniq could hurl heavy stones over city walls, making it essential for siege warfare.

🛡 Testudo Formation

Shield walls (similar to the Roman testudo) were used to protect advancing troops from arrows while approaching city walls with ladders and rams.
