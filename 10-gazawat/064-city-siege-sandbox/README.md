# City Siege Sandbox — Siege Engineering

Interactive canvas siege planning game where players build and position siege equipment to breach fortified city walls.

## Concept

Plan and execute a siege against fortified city walls. Place ladders, battering rams, catapults, and shield formations strategically, then watch the assault unfold. Inspired by the Siege of Ta'if (8 AH) where Muslim forces used catapults (manjaniq).

## Gameplay

- Place ladders near walls to climb over (slow but steady damage)
- Position battering rams near the gate for heavy damage
- Set catapults far back for ranged wall bombardment
- Shield walls reduce arrow damage by 70% to nearby equipment
- Wall defenders fire arrows at approaching siege equipment
- Reduce wall HP to 0 to breach the city
- Progress through 5 increasingly fortified cities

## Features

- Single HTML file, zero dependencies, fully offline
- 8 CSS themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Arctic, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Sand
- Trilingual: English, French, Arabic (with RTL support)
- Splash screen with Bismillah
- Sidebar help panel with FAQ, How-To, and Wiki tabs
- Web Audio API sound effects (no audio files)
- High score persistence via localStorage
- Canvas-based siege with projectile system and wall damage visualization

## Lesson

Siege warfare required patience, engineering skill, and strategic thinking. Muslim armies developed sophisticated siege techniques while maintaining ethical conduct, often offering terms of peace before any assault.
