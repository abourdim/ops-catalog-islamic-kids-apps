📜 Diplomatic Immunity

The Prophet ﷺ established that envoys and messengers cannot be killed or harmed, regardless of their message. This predates the Vienna Convention by over 1300 years.

🏳 Musaylimah's Envoys

When Musaylimah's messengers brought insulting messages, the Prophet said: 'Were it not that envoys are not killed, I would have struck your necks.' This established the principle firmly.
