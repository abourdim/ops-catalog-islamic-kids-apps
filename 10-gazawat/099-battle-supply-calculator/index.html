What is this game?

A math-based logistics game where you calculate food, water, arrows, and camels needed for military campaigns of various sizes and distances.

How do I calculate?

Use the given rates (e.g., 2 kg of food per soldier per day) and multiply by the number of soldiers and travel days.

📦 Army Logistics

Supplying an army was critical. The Prophet carefully planned provisions for every campaign, ensuring soldiers had food, water, weapons, and transport.

🐪 Camels as Transport

Camels were the primary transport animal. One camel could carry about 150 kg of supplies and travel 40 km per day in desert conditions.
