What is Weapons Forge?

A crafting game where you forge historical Islamic weapons. Choose weapon type, material, heating method, and shaping technique to create weapons with unique stats.

Is this historically accurate?

Yes. Damascus steel (Wootz steel) was famous worldwide. Muslim blacksmiths produced some of the finest swords, including the legendary Dhul-Fiqar.

How are weapon stats calculated?

Each choice (material, heat, shape) contributes to attack, defense, durability, and weight. Better materials and techniques yield higher quality weapons.

Damascus Steel

Muslim smiths perfected Wootz/Damascus steel, creating blades with distinctive wavy patterns. These swords were legendary for being both sharp and flexible.

Dhul-Fiqar

The famous sword given to Ali ibn Abi Talib by the Prophet (peace be upon him). It became a symbol of Islamic valor and justice.

Islamic Armor

Muslim warriors used chainmail (dir'), lamellar armor, and helmets (bayda). The Quran mentions Allah teaching David to make chainmail (Surah Saba, 34:10-11).
