What is Riddah Wars?

A strategy game about Abu Bakr's campaign against the apostasy rebellions after the Prophet's passing. Command 11 armies to reunify Arabia under Islam.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once loaded, everything runs in your browser with no internet needed.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Works on any device with a modern browser. The map and panels adapt to your screen size.

How do I change the theme?

Click any theme button in the controls bar.

How do I change the language?

Click EN / FR / ع. Arabic enables right-to-left layout automatically.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser. No data is sent anywhere.

Context

After the Prophet Muhammad passed away in 11 AH (632 CE), many Arabian tribes rebelled against Islam. Some followed false prophets, others refused to pay Zakat. Abu Bakr, the first Caliph, famously declared he would fight anyone who distinguished between salah and zakat.

The 11 Fronts

Abu Bakr organized 11 armies sent simultaneously to different regions of Arabia. Each army had a specific commander and mission. The strategy was to crush all rebellions at once to prevent coordination.

Battle of Yamama

The fiercest battle was against Musaylimah the false prophet in Yamama. Khalid ibn al-Walid led the Muslim forces. Many huffaz (Quran memorizers) were martyred, which later prompted Abu Bakr to order the compilation of the Quran into a single book.

Themes

8 built-in themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Arctic, Sand.

Languages

Trilingual: English, Francais, al-Arabiyyah. Arabic auto-enables RTL layout.

Privacy

Local-first. No tracking, no analytics, no external calls. All data stays in your browser.
