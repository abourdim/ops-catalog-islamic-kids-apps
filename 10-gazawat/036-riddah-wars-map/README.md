# Riddah Wars — Abu Bakr defends the faith

Canvas-based multi-front war command game for Muslim kids.

## Overview

After the Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him) passed away in 11 AH (632 CE), many Arabian tribes rebelled against Islam. Some followed false prophets like Musaylimah and Tulayha, others refused to pay Zakat. Abu Bakr, the first Caliph, organized 11 armies to crush all rebellions simultaneously.

## Gameplay

1. **Phase 1 — Planning**: Assign 11 commanders to 11 rebellion fronts on the Arabian Peninsula map. Allocate limited troops across all fronts (must balance carefully).
2. **Phase 2 — Execution**: Watch battles resolve one by one on the map. Each front's outcome depends on commander match, troop strength vs. difficulty, and some luck.
3. **Phase 3 — Results**: Learn about Abu Bakr's firmness, the Battle of Yamama, the martyrdom of many Quran huffaz, and the decision to compile the Quran.

## Key Features

- Interactive canvas map of the Arabian Peninsula with 11 labeled regions
- 11 historical commanders with unique bonuses (Khalid ibn al-Walid, Ikrimah, Shurahbil, etc.)
- Difficulty ratings per front; Yamama (Musaylimah) is the fiercest
- 3 difficulty levels: Easy, Normal, Hard (affects troop pool and enemy strength)
- Battle-by-battle animated resolution on the map
- Historical lesson about Abu Bakr's famous declaration and the compilation of the Quran
