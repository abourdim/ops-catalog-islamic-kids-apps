# Al-Muraysi Campaign — Strategic Planning

Interactive strategic planning game about the Prophet's (peace be upon him) swift campaign against Banu Mustaliq at Al-Muraysi well (6 AH / 627 CE).

## Concept

When news reached Medina that Banu Mustaliq were gathering forces at Al-Muraysi well to attack, the Prophet acted with remarkable speed. The player makes 8 strategic decisions covering intelligence, march formation, scout deployment, water strategy, battle approach, captive policy, post-battle unity, and the overall lesson.

## The 8 Phases

| # | Phase | Description | Key Lesson |
|---|-------|-------------|------------|
| 1 | Intelligence Report | Respond to scout reports of enemy gathering | Swift action prevents greater harm |
| 2 | March Formation | Organize the army's approach | Tight formation maintains surprise |
| 3 | Scout Deployment | Deploy reconnaissance wisely | Balance caution with speed |
| 4 | Water Strategy | Handle the water source at Al-Muraysi | Secure resources before engagement |
| 5 | Battle Approach | How to begin the engagement | Always call to Islam first |
| 6 | Captive Policy | Decide fate of captives | Marriage alliance freed an entire tribe |
| 7 | Post-Battle Unity | Handle internal disputes | Wisdom over force preserves unity |
| 8 | Lesson of Campaign | Reflect on the strategic lesson | Swift mercy produces best outcomes |

## Features

- Single HTML file, zero dependencies, fully offline
- 8 CSS themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Arctic, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Sand
- Trilingual: English, French, Arabic (with RTL support)
- Splash screen with Bismillah
- Interactive canvas map showing army movement from Medina to Al-Muraysi
- Sidebar help panel with FAQ, How-To, and Wiki tabs
- Web Audio API sound effects (no audio files)
- Keyboard shortcuts (1-3 for choices, Enter to continue)
- High score persistence via localStorage
- Theme/language/sound preferences persisted

## Lesson

Swift, decisive action combined with mercy produces the best outcomes. The Prophet turned potential enemies into devoted allies — Juwayriyah's marriage led her entire tribe to embrace Islam willingly.
