What is this game?

A strategic planning game about the Prophet's ﷺ campaign against Banu Mustaliq at Al-Muraysi well (6 AH / 627 CE). Make tactical decisions about surprise, speed, and deployment.

Is it historically accurate?

Yes. All scenarios are based on authentic historical accounts of the Banu Mustaliq campaign.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once loaded, everything runs locally in your browser.

How do I change the theme?

Click any theme icon in the controls bar to switch between 8 visual themes.

How do I change the language?

Click EN, FR, or the Arabic button. Arabic auto-enables RTL layout.

The Campaign

In 6 AH (627 CE), the Prophet ﷺ learned that Banu Mustaliq were gathering forces at the Al-Muraysi well to attack Medina. He acted swiftly with a surprise march.

Swift Action

The Muslim army moved quickly and caught Banu Mustaliq unprepared. The battle was brief with minimal casualties, demonstrating the value of speed and surprise.

Mercy After Victory

After the victory, the Prophet ﷺ freed captives and married Juwayriyah bint al-Harith, leading her entire tribe to embrace Islam willingly.
