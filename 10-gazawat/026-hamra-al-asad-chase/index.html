What is Hamra al-Asad?

A strategy game about the day after the Battle of Uhud. The Prophet ﷺ led wounded Muslims on a bold march to Hamra al-Asad, lit campfires to appear as a massive army, and scared the Quraysh into retreating without a fight.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once loaded, everything runs in your browser with no internet needed.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Works on any device with a modern browser. Tap the canvas to interact!

How do I change the theme?

Click any theme button in the controls bar.

How do I change the language?

Click EN / FR / ع. Arabic enables right-to-left layout automatically.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser. No data is sent anywhere.

Purpose

Learn about the expedition to Hamra al-Asad — a brilliant example of psychological warfare and courage after defeat in early Islamic history.

Historical Context

After the Battle of Uhud (3 AH), the Muslims suffered losses. The very next day, the Prophet ﷺ ordered that only those who had fought at Uhud could march. Despite their wounds, they marched 13 km to Hamra al-Asad and lit large campfires, making Abu Sufyan believe a massive army awaited him. The Quraysh retreated without engaging.

Quran Reference

Surah Aal-Imran (3:172-174) — Those who responded to Allah and the Messenger after injury had struck them.

Lessons

Show of strength even when weak. Psychological warfare. Courage and obedience after defeat. Unity of the believers.
