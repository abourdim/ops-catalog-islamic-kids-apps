What is Spoils Division?

An interactive calculator to learn how war spoils (ghanaim) are divided in Islam. 1/5 goes to Bayt al-Mal (state treasury for orphans, the poor, travelers) and 4/5 goes to the fighters.

What is Khums?

Khums means "one-fifth." It is the share of spoils designated for Allah, His Messenger, near relatives, orphans, the needy, and travelers, as stated in Quran 8:41.

Why do cavalry get double?

A cavalryman (horse rider) receives 3 shares: 2 for the horse and 1 for the rider. Infantry soldiers get 1 share each. This is because maintaining a horse for battle was a significant contribution.

Purpose

Learn the Islamic rules of spoils (ghanaim) distribution through interactive math exercises. Based on Quran 8:41.

The Rule

1/5 (Khums) goes to Bayt al-Mal: for Allah's cause, the Messenger, relatives, orphans, the needy, and travelers. 4/5 goes to the fighters who participated in battle.

Cavalry vs Infantry

A cavalryman gets 3 shares (2 for horse + 1 for rider). An infantryman gets 1 share. Total shares = (cavalry x 3) + infantry.
