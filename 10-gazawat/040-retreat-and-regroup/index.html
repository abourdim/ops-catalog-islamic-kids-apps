❓ Help
What is Tactical Retreat?

A canvas-based tactical game where you command an orderly retreat. Place rally flags, guide soldiers to safety, and protect the rear guard to regroup and counter-attack.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once loaded, everything runs in your browser with no internet needed.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Works on any device with a modern browser — phone, tablet, laptop, desktop. Tap to place flags.

How do I change the theme?

Click any theme button in the controls bar.

How do I change the language?

Click EN / FR / ع. Arabic enables right-to-left layout automatically.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser. No data is sent anywhere.

What are the 3 scenarios?

Fighting Retreat (Mu'tah style) — steady pullback under pressure. Feigned Retreat (Ain Jalut) — lure enemy then counter. Strategic Withdrawal — fall back to high ground for advantage.

🎯 Purpose

Learn tactical retreat concepts from Islamic history. Not every retreat is a defeat — a well-ordered withdrawal saves the army to fight another day.

⚔️ Mu'tah

The Battle of Mu'tah (629 CE): Khalid ibn al-Walid took command and executed a brilliant fighting retreat, saving the Muslim army from a vastly larger force.

🏹 Ain Jalut

The Battle of Ain Jalut (1260 CE): The Mamluks used a feigned retreat to lure the Mongol army into an ambush, achieving a historic victory.

🎨 Themes

8 built-in themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Arctic, Sand.

🌐 Languages

Trilingual: English, Francais, Arabic. Arabic auto-enables RTL layout.

🔊 Sound

Web Audio API — no files needed. Toggle with the sound button.

🔒 Privacy

Local-first. No tracking, no analytics, no external calls. All data stays in your browser.
