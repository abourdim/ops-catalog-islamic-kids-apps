Help
What is this game?

An interactive simulation of the Treaty of Hudaybiyyah negotiation. You play as a companion of the Prophet (peace be upon him) and learn why accepting seemingly unfair terms was actually a great victory.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once loaded, everything runs in your browser with no internet needed.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Works on any device with a modern browser.

How do I change the theme?

Click any theme button (T M E P S R A D) in the controls bar.

How do I change the language?

Click EN / FR / (ayn). Arabic enables right-to-left layout automatically.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser. No data is sent anywhere.

Purpose

Learn about the Treaty of Hudaybiyyah — one of the most important events in Islamic history. Understand how short-term sacrifice leads to long-term victory.

Historical Context

In 6 AH (628 CE), the Prophet (peace be upon him) set out with 1,400 Muslims for Umrah. The Quraysh blocked them at Hudaybiyyah. What followed was a negotiation that changed the course of history.
