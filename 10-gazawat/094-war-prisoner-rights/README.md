# War Prisoner Rights

Interactive scenario-based game teaching Islamic principles of treating prisoners of war with dignity, kindness, and justice — centuries before the Geneva Conventions.

## Concept

Islam established comprehensive prisoner-of-war rights 1,400 years ago. Players face 10 scenarios about feeding, clothing, sheltering, and respecting captives, learning Quranic verses and Prophetic teachings that mandate humane treatment.

## The 10 Scenarios

| # | Topic | Key Principle |
|---|-------|---------------|
| 1 | Feeding | Feed prisoners before yourself (76:8) |
| 2 | Clothing | Provide clean clothing |
| 3 | Torture | Absolutely forbidden |
| 4 | Family separation | Prohibited — keep families together |
| 5 | Medical care | Treat wounded enemies equally |
| 6 | Freedom of worship | No compulsion in religion (2:256) |
| 7 | Paths to freedom | Ransom, exchange, release, or teaching |
| 8 | Humiliation | Strictly forbidden |
| 9 | Shelter | Share tents and adequate housing |
| 10 | Education as ransom | Teaching children to read for freedom |

## Features

- Single HTML file, zero dependencies, fully offline
- 8 CSS themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Arctic, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Sand
- Trilingual: English, French, Arabic (with RTL support)
- Splash screen with Bismillah
- Sidebar help panel with FAQ, How-To, and Wiki tabs
- Web Audio API sound effects (no audio files)
- High score persistence via localStorage
- Scenarios shuffled each playthrough

## Lesson

Human dignity is sacred in Islam regardless of whether someone is friend or foe. The Prophet established prisoner rights that prioritize feeding, clothing, sheltering, and respecting captives — principles that modern international law took centuries to codify.
