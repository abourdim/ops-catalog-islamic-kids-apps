What is this game about?

Learn Islamic principles for treating prisoners of war with dignity. Each scenario tests your knowledge of these rights through historical examples.

Why is this important?

Islam established prisoner-of-war rights 1,400 years ago — long before modern conventions. These principles show Islam's emphasis on human dignity even in warfare.

📖 Quran on Prisoners

"And they feed, for the love of Allah, the poor, the orphan, and the captive" (76:8). Feeding prisoners is an act of worship in Islam.

⚖ Rights of POWs in Islam

Prisoners must be fed, clothed, sheltered, not tortured, not humiliated, families not separated, and given medical care.
