What is River Defense?

A tower-defense style game where you defend a river crossing by placing archers, spearmen, barriers, and fire pits to prevent enemies from crossing to your side.

How do barriers work?

Barriers block bridge/ford crossing points. Enemies must destroy them before passing, giving your archers more time to attack.

What do fire pits do?

Fire pits deal continuous damage to enemies trying to cross at ford (shallow) points. Very effective against swimmers.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Fully self-contained in your browser.

🌊 Battle of Yarmouk

At Yarmouk (15 AH / 636 CE), Khalid ibn al-Walid used the Yarmouk river gorge as a natural barrier. The Muslim army trapped the Byzantine forces against the river, turning geography into a decisive advantage.

🛡 River Tactics

Controlling river crossings was vital in medieval warfare. Defenders at a ford or bridge could hold off much larger forces by limiting how many enemies could cross at once.

🔥 Defensive Engineering

Muslim armies were skilled at field fortifications. From the Trench of Medina to river barriers, they used engineering to multiply their defensive advantage.
