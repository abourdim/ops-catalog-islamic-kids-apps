# River Defense Sandbox — Control the Crossing

Interactive canvas strategy game where players defend a river crossing with archers, spearmen, barriers, and fire pits.

## Concept

Defend a strategic river crossing against enemy forces. Position archers along the bank, build barriers on bridges, place fire pits at fords, and station spearmen to intercept any who make it across. Inspired by the Battle of Yarmouk river tactics.

## Gameplay

- River with 2 bridges and 1 ford (shallow crossing point)
- Place archers on your bank for ranged attacks across the river
- Build barriers on bridges to block and slow enemies
- Fire pits deal continuous damage at ford crossing points
- Spearmen guard your side for melee combat
- Enemies attempt to cross at bridge and ford points
- If 5+ enemies cross to your side, the defense fails
- 5 waves of increasing difficulty

## Features

- Single HTML file, zero dependencies, fully offline
- 8 CSS themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Arctic, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Sand
- Trilingual: English, French, Arabic (with RTL support)
- Splash screen with Bismillah
- Sidebar help panel with FAQ, How-To, and Wiki tabs
- Web Audio API sound effects (no audio files)
- High score persistence via localStorage
- Animated water with flowing wave effects

## Lesson

Controlling river crossings was a decisive factor in many Islamic battles. At Yarmouk, Khalid ibn al-Walid used the river gorge as a natural barrier, demonstrating how terrain awareness could overcome numerical disadvantage.
