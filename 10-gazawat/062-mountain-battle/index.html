❓ Help
What is Mountain Battle?

A strategy game where you use mountain terrain advantages to defend against waves of enemies. Place archers on high ground for bonus damage and block passes with infantry.

What is the elevation bonus?

Units placed on higher terrain (ridges/peaks shown in lighter color) deal 50% more damage due to the high ground advantage.

How do boulder traps work?

Boulders roll downhill when enemies pass, dealing area damage. Place them on slopes for maximum effect.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once loaded, everything runs locally in your browser.

⛰ Battle of Uhud

At Uhud (3 AH), the Prophet (peace be upon him) positioned archers on Mount Ainain to protect the flank. When they left their position, the tide of battle turned, teaching the critical lesson of holding high ground.

🏹 Archers of Uhud

Fifty archers were placed on a strategic hill with strict orders not to leave. Their elevation gave them a decisive advantage over enemy cavalry.

🪨 Terrain Advantage

Mountain warfare gives defenders natural advantages: higher ground for archers, narrow passes as chokepoints, and gravity for rolling boulders.
