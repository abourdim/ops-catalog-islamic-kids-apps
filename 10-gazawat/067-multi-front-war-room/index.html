❓ Help
What is Multi-Front War Room?

A turn-based strategy game where you command armies across 4 fronts simultaneously. Allocate limited troops from your reserve to hold all fronts for 10 turns.

How does combat work?

Each turn, enemy threat on each front increases randomly. If your troops on a front are less than enemy strength, you lose that front. Lose any front and the campaign fails.

How do I get reinforcements?

Each turn you receive 5-10 reinforcements added to your reserve. You also recoup 2 troops per front that is holding strong.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Fully self-contained in your browser.

🗺 Multi-Front Campaigns

During the Ridda wars and the early conquests, Abu Bakr al-Siddiq managed multiple military campaigns simultaneously, sending armies under different commanders to different regions.

⚔ Khalid's March

Khalid ibn al-Walid's famous march from Iraq to Syria demonstrated the ability to redeploy forces between fronts — a critical skill in multi-front warfare.

📊 Resource Management

Managing limited resources across multiple theaters of operation was a hallmark of early Islamic military leadership. Commanders had to balance reinforcements carefully.
