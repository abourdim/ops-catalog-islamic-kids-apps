What is Prophet's Letters?

An interactive tool to learn about the diplomatic letters the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ sent to world leaders inviting them to Islam. Fill in blanks or arrange letter parts in the correct order.

How many letters are included?

6 historical letters: to Heraclius (Rome), Khosrow II (Persia), Najashi (Abyssinia), Muqawqis (Egypt), Mundhir ibn Sawa (Bahrain), and Jaifer & Abd (Oman).

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once loaded, everything runs in your browser with no internet needed.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Works on any device with a modern browser.

How do I change the theme?

Click any theme button in the controls bar.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally. No data is sent anywhere.

Purpose

Learn the art of prophetic diplomacy. The Prophet ﷺ sent letters to rulers in the 6th/7th year after Hijrah, each following a respectful structure: Bismillah, greeting, invitation to Islam, and a reminder of consequence.

Historical Context

After the Treaty of Hudaybiyyah, the Prophet ﷺ sent ambassadors with sealed letters to major world leaders. Each letter was stamped with his seal reading "Muhammad Rasul Allah".

Themes

8 built-in themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Arctic, Sand.

Languages

Trilingual: English, Francais, al-Arabiyyah. Arabic auto-enables RTL layout.

Sound

Privacy
