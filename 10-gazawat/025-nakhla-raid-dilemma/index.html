❓ Help
What is the Nakhla Raid Dilemma?

An interactive story about the first armed encounter in early Islamic history. You face the same moral dilemma the companions faced and learn about the ethical complexity involved.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once loaded, everything runs in your browser with no internet needed.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Works on any device with a modern browser — phone, tablet, laptop, desktop.

How do I change the theme?

How do I change the language?

Click EN / FR / ع. Arabic enables right-to-left layout automatically.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser. No data is sent anywhere.

Is this historically accurate?

Yes. The story is based on authentic narrations from the Seerah (prophetic biography). The Quran verse cited is Surah Al-Baqarah 2:217.

🎯 Purpose

Teach Muslim kids about the ethical complexity of the Nakhla Raid — the first armed encounter in Islam — through interactive decision-making.

📖 Historical Context

In Rajab 2 AH, the Prophet ﷺ sent Abdullah ibn Jahsh with 12 men on a reconnaissance mission toward Mecca. They encountered a Quraysh caravan at Nakhla on the last day of Rajab (a sacred month). The companions debated whether to attack.

📜 Quran Reference

Surah Al-Baqarah 2:217 — revealed to address the controversy about fighting in the sacred month.
