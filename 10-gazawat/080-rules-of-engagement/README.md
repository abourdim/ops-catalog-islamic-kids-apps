# Rules of Engagement

Interactive scenario-based ethics game about Islamic rules of war. Players face moral dilemmas and must choose the response that aligns with Islamic principles of mercy, justice, and protection of civilians.

## Concept

Islam established comprehensive rules of warfare 1400 years before modern Geneva Conventions. Abu Bakr al-Siddiq's famous Ten Commandments to Muslim armies, and the Prophet's (peace be upon him) own conduct in battle, form the foundation of Islamic military ethics. Players learn these principles through engaging moral scenarios.

## Gameplay

1. Read each scenario describing a wartime dilemma
2. Choose from 3 possible responses
3. See the correct Islamic ruling with its source
4. Learn the principle and historical context
5. Complete all 10 scenarios
6. Score based on percentage of correct answers

## The 10 Scenarios

| # | Dilemma | Islamic Ruling |
|---|---------|----------------|
| 1 | Treatment of prisoners | Feed and treat them well (Quran 76:8) |
| 2 | Enemy orchards and farms | Do not destroy crops or trees |
| 3 | Monastery with monks | Leave them in peace |
| 4 | Women and children found | Ensure their safety and provide food |
| 5 | Enemy peace envoy | Listen and consider peace (Quran 8:61) |
| 6 | Mutilating fallen enemies | Absolutely forbidden |
| 7 | Wounded enemy soldier | Provide medical aid |
| 8 | Poisoning water wells | Forbidden — water is sacred |
| 9 | Enemy who betrayed treaty surrenders | Accept surrender, grant safety |
| 10 | Library and cultural treasures | Preserve and protect them |

## Features

- Single HTML file, zero dependencies, fully offline
- 8 CSS themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Arctic, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Sand
- Trilingual: English, French, Arabic (with RTL support)
- Splash screen with Bismillah
- Sidebar help panel with FAQ, How-To, and Wiki tabs
- Web Audio API sound effects (no audio files)
- Keyboard shortcuts (1-3 for choices, Enter to continue)
- Scenarios shuffled each playthrough
- Detailed explanations with Quranic and historical sources
- High score persistence via localStorage
- Progress bar tracking completion

## Lesson

Ethics in warfare: Islam teaches that even in the heat of battle, moral principles must never be abandoned. Protection of civilians, treatment of prisoners with dignity, preservation of nature and culture, and honoring treaties are non-negotiable Islamic values. True strength is shown not in destruction, but in restraint and mercy.
