What is this game about?

An interactive story about the night before the Battle of Badr (17 Ramadan, 2 AH). You make the same decisions the Prophet made about consultation, strategy, and prayer.

Why is Badr important?

Badr was the first major battle in Islam. 313 Muslims faced over 1000 Quraysh. It was called 'Yawm al-Furqan' — the Day of Distinction between truth and falsehood.

1

Follow the timeline of events the night before Badr

3

Learn the wisdom behind each decision

⚔ Battle of Badr

17 Ramadan, 2 AH (624 CE). 313 Muslims vs 1000+ Quraysh. The first major battle of Islam and a decisive victory by Allah's permission.

🙏 Shura (Consultation)

The Prophet consulted his companions before Badr, establishing the principle that leaders must consult their people in important decisions.
