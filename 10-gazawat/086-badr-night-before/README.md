# Badr: The Night Before — 17 Ramadan, 2 AH

Interactive chronological story about the night before the Battle of Badr. Players make the same decisions the Prophet (peace be upon him) made — consultation (shura), strategic planning, prayer, and leadership.

## Concept

The Battle of Badr was the first major battle in Islam — 313 Muslims vs over 1000 Quraysh. Players follow the timeline from afternoon through dawn, making decisions at each key moment.

## The 10 Chapters

| # | Time | Decision |
|---|------|----------|
| 1 | Afternoon | Consult companions (Shura) |
| 2 | Late Afternoon | Accept al-Hubab's better position |
| 3 | Evening | Build the command shelter (arish) |
| 4 | Night | Recognize divine rain as blessing |
| 5 | Late Night | Intense prayer and dua |
| 6 | Pre-Dawn | Justice — Sawad's retaliation |
| 7 | Dawn | Inspire with Paradise, not worldly gain |
| 8 | Battle Begins | Send family first — lead by example |
| 9 | During Battle | The handful of dust miracle |
| 10 | After Victory | Treat prisoners with mercy |

## Features

- Single HTML file, zero dependencies, fully offline
- 8 CSS themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Arctic, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Sand
- Trilingual: English, French, Arabic (with RTL support)
- Splash screen with Bismillah
- Sequential story mode (chronological, not shuffled)
- Sidebar help panel with FAQ, How-To, and Wiki tabs
- Web Audio API sound effects (no audio files)
- High score persistence via localStorage

## Lesson

Badr teaches consultation, accepting good advice, combining preparation with prayer, total reliance on Allah, mercy to the defeated, and leadership by example.
