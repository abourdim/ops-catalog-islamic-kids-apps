❓ Help
What is this game?

An interactive story about the Banu Qaynuqa incident in early Islamic history. You make diplomatic choices at each phase and learn about treaties, justice, and mercy.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once loaded, everything runs in your browser with no internet needed.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Works on any device with a modern browser — phone, tablet, laptop, desktop.

How do I change the theme?

Click any theme button (🖥 🌌 🔥 etc.) in the controls bar.

How do I change the language?

Click EN / FR / ع. Arabic enables right-to-left layout automatically.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser. No data is sent anywhere.

How is wisdom scored?

Each phase has choices. Wise, historically accurate choices earn more wisdom points. Making all the best choices earns a perfect score.

🎯 Purpose

Teach children about the Banu Qaynuqa incident: the Constitution of Medina, treaty obligations, consequences of betrayal, the siege, and the merciful resolution of exile.

📜 Historical Context

After the Prophet (peace be upon him) migrated to Medina, he established the Constitution of Medina — a treaty binding all tribes (Muslim, Jewish, and others) to mutual defense and peaceful coexistence. Banu Qaynuqa were one of three major Jewish tribes in Medina who signed this treaty.
