What is Scout Mission?

A decision-based stealth game where you lead scouting parties on intelligence-gathering missions before major Islamic battles.

How does stealth work?

Each decision affects your stealth level. Good choices maintain stealth and gather intel. Bad choices reduce stealth and risk detection.

🕵 Scouting in Islam

The Prophet regularly sent scouts before battles. Before Badr, he sent Ali and Zubayr to gather intelligence about the Quraysh caravan and army.

👁 Hudhayfah at Khandaq

During the Battle of the Trench, the Prophet sent Hudhayfah ibn al-Yaman on a dangerous solo mission into the enemy camp to assess their morale.
