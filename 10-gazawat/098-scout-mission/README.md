# Scout Mission

Decision-based stealth game where players lead scouting parties on intelligence-gathering missions before major Islamic battles. Features a stealth meter and intel score system.

## Concept

The Prophet regularly sent scouts before battles. Players experience 3 historical scouting missions with 2 decision points each, managing stealth while gathering maximum intelligence. Based on real missions including Hudhayfah's legendary infiltration of the enemy camp at the Trench.

## The 3 Missions (6 Decisions)

| Mission | Historical Basis | Key Decisions |
|---------|-----------------|---------------|
| Pre-Badr Intelligence | Scouts sent to wells of Badr | Night approach, quiet observation |
| Hudhayfah at the Trench | Solo infiltration of enemy camp | Blending in, counter-interrogation |
| Mecca Reconnaissance | Pre-conquest intelligence | Merchant disguise, indirect return route |

## Features

- Single HTML file, zero dependencies, fully offline
- Stealth meter that decreases with bad decisions
- Intel score tracking across missions
- 8 CSS themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Arctic, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Sand
- Trilingual: English, French, Arabic (with RTL support)
- Splash screen with Bismillah
- Sidebar help panel with FAQ, How-To, and Wiki tabs
- Web Audio API sound effects (no audio files)
- High score persistence via localStorage

## Lesson

Intelligence gathering requires patience, wisdom, and courage. The Prophet valued information and careful planning. Hudhayfah's mission at the Trench shows that a single brave scout with good judgment can be worth more than an entire army.
