# Post-Battle Mercy

Interactive decision game showing how the Prophet (peace be upon him) demonstrated extraordinary mercy after every battle — from Badr to Uhud to the Conquest of Mecca.

## Concept

After each battle, the Prophet faced critical decisions about captives, enemies, and defeated peoples. In every case, he chose mercy over revenge, transforming enemies into allies. Players face 10 real historical scenarios and must choose the merciful response.

## The 10 Scenarios

| # | Battle | Scenario | Prophet's Response |
|---|--------|----------|-------------------|
| 1 | Badr | 70 captives taken | Treated kindly, ransom/teaching |
| 2 | Uhud | Prophet wounded, urged to curse | Prayed for enemy guidance |
| 3 | Mecca Conquest | City at his mercy | General amnesty |
| 4 | Badr | Suhayl captured, tongue threat | Forbade mutilation |
| 5 | Hunayn | Distributing war spoils | Generous to new Muslims |
| 6 | Ta'if | People refuse Islam | Prayed for their children |
| 7 | Badr | Literate captives | Teaching children for freedom |
| 8 | Uhud | Hamza's body mutilated | Forbade revenge mutilation |
| 9 | Hudaybiyyah | Harsh treaty terms | Accepted for peace |
| 10 | Hunayn | 6,000 captive women/children | Released them all |

## Features

- Single HTML file, zero dependencies, fully offline
- 8 CSS themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Arctic, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Sand
- Trilingual: English, French, Arabic (with RTL support)
- Splash screen with Bismillah
- Sidebar help panel with FAQ, How-To, and Wiki tabs
- Web Audio API sound effects (no audio files)
- High score persistence via localStorage
- Scenarios shuffled each playthrough
- Keyboard shortcuts (1-3 for choices, Enter to continue)

## Lesson

True strength lies in mercy after victory. The Prophet consistently chose compassion over revenge, forgiveness over punishment, and peace over conflict — winning hearts and transforming enemies into devoted allies.
