What is Post-Battle Mercy?

A decision game where you learn how the Prophet showed mercy after battles. Each scenario presents a real historical situation and you choose the merciful response.

Which battles are covered?

Scenarios from Badr, Uhud, Banu Qaynuqa, Hudaybiyyah, the Conquest of Mecca, Hunayn, and Ta'if.

Is this historically accurate?

Yes. All scenarios are based on authentic historical sources about the Prophet's conduct after battles.

🕊 Mercy in Islam

The Prophet said: "The merciful are shown mercy by the Most Merciful. Be merciful to those on earth, and He who is in heaven will be merciful to you."

⚖ Battle of Badr

2 AH (624 CE). First major battle. Despite being outnumbered, the Muslims were victorious. The Prophet treated captives with unprecedented kindness.

🕊 Conquest of Mecca

8 AH (630 CE). The Prophet entered Mecca peacefully and declared a general amnesty, forgiving even his worst enemies.
