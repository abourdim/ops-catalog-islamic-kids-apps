What is this?

An interactive encyclopedia of major Islamic battles from the Prophetic era through the Rashidun Caliphate. Browse, search, filter, and test your knowledge.

How many battles are included?

16 major battles spanning from Badr (2 AH) to the conquest of Egypt (21 AH).

Prophetic Era

Battles during the lifetime of the Prophet ﷺ (2-11 AH / 624-632 CE), including Badr, Uhud, the Trench, Khaybar, and the Conquest of Mecca.

Rashidun Era

Campaigns under the four Rightly Guided Caliphs (11-40 AH / 632-661 CE), expanding Islam from Arabia to Egypt, Syria, Iraq, and Persia.

Key Commanders

The Prophet ﷺ, Khalid ibn al-Walid, Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas, Amr ibn al-As, Abu Ubayda ibn al-Jarrah, and many more heroes of Islam.
