❓ Help
What is March to Tabuk?

A resource management game where you lead the Muslim army on the longest expedition — 700 km from Medina to Tabuk through scorching desert.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once loaded, everything runs in your browser with no internet needed.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Works on any device with a modern browser — phone, tablet, laptop, desktop.

How do I change the theme?

Click any theme button in the controls bar.

How do I change the language?

Click EN / FR / ع. Arabic enables right-to-left layout automatically.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser. No data is sent anywhere.

What are the difficulty levels?

Easy: more starting resources, slower consumption. Normal: balanced. Hard: fewer resources, faster consumption, harsher events.

🎯 Purpose

Learn about Ghazwat Tabuk — the longest military expedition led by the Prophet (peace be upon him) in 9 AH. Manage resources across 700 km of desert.

📜 Historical Context

In the extreme heat of summer, 30,000 soldiers marched from Medina to Tabuk to confront a rumored Roman invasion. The Romans never appeared — a victory through deterrence.

🐪 Uthman's Sacrifice

Uthman ibn Affan (may Allah be pleased with him) donated 1,000 camels, 70 horses, and 1,000 dinars to equip the army — one of the greatest acts of generosity in Islamic history.

🎨 Themes

8 built-in themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Arctic, Sand.

🌐 Languages

Trilingual: English, Francais, العربية. Arabic auto-enables RTL layout.

🔊 Sound

Web Audio API — no files needed. Toggle with the speaker button.

🔒 Privacy

Local-first. No tracking, no analytics, no external calls. All data stays in your browser.
