# March to Tabuk

**Lead the longest expedition** — a canvas-based resource management game for Muslim kids.

## Overview

Players lead 30,000 soldiers on the 700 km march from Medina to Tabuk through scorching desert, managing water, food, camels, morale, and soldiers. Based on the historical Ghazwat Tabuk (9 AH).

## Gameplay

- **March**: Advance toward Tabuk (consumes resources, costs morale)
- **Rest**: Recover morale and soldiers (still consumes daily rations)
- **Ration**: Stretch supplies (costs morale)

### Random Events
- Sandstorms, extreme heat, oasis discoveries
- Uthman ibn Affan's historic donation of 1,000 camels + gold
- Hypocrites trying to discourage the army (dialogue choices)
- Friendly tribes offering supplies or alliances
- Scout reports with route decisions
- Prayer and du'a moments

### Two-Phase Journey
1. **Medina to Tabuk** (700 km) — Roman army withdraws without fighting
2. **Return to Medina** (700 km) — manage remaining resources for the trip home

### Victory Conditions
- Reach Tabuk with surviving army
- Return to Medina with resources intact
- Score based on distance, morale, soldiers, and resources preserved

## Lessons
- **Sacrifice**: Uthman's generosity for the Ummah
- **Exposing Hypocrites**: The march revealed true believers
- **Deterrence**: Strength prevented war without fighting
- **Longest Expedition**: 700 km demonstrated Muslim determination
- **Diplomacy**: Treaties with neighboring tribes

## Features

- Single HTML file, zero dependencies, fully offline
- Canvas-based desert map with animated route and waypoints
- 8 CSS themes: Terminal, Midnight, Ember, Phosphor, Signal, Redshift, Arctic, Sand
- 3 difficulty levels (Easy, Normal, Hard)
- Trilingual UI (EN / FR / AR with RTL support)
- Web Audio sound effects (no audio files)
- Keyboard shortcuts (1-3 for actions, Enter to start, Esc to close help)
- Persistent preferences (theme, language, sound, high score)
- Sidebar help panel with FAQ, How-To, and Wiki tabs
- Activity log tracking all events
