What is this app?

Solve algebra equations the way Al-Khwarizmi taught in the 9th century. You balance equations visually by performing al-jabr (completion) and al-muqabala (balancing) operations.

Who was Al-Khwarizmi?

Muhammad ibn Musa al-Khwarizmi (780-850 CE) was a Muslim mathematician whose book on algebra gave the subject its name. The word "algorithm" also comes from his name.

What is al-jabr?

Al-jabr means "completion" — moving a negative term to the other side of the equation, making it positive. This is the origin of the word "algebra".

What is al-muqabala?

Al-muqabala means "balancing" — subtracting equal terms from both sides to simplify the equation.

📕 Al-Kitab al-Mukhtasar

Al-Khwarizmi's foundational book on algebra, written around 820 CE in Baghdad. It systematically solved linear and quadratic equations.

🎓 Six Standard Forms

Al-Khwarizmi classified all equations into six standard forms and provided geometric proofs for each solution method.

🌎 Legacy

His work was translated into Latin and became the main mathematics textbook in Europe for centuries. The word "algebra" comes from "al-jabr" in his book title.
