Who was Ibn al-Haytham?

Abu Ali ibn al-Haytham (965-1040 CE) wrote the Book of Optics, revolutionizing our understanding of light and vision. He proved light travels in straight lines.

What is refraction?

The bending of light when it passes between mediums of different density, like air to glass. Ibn al-Haytham studied this extensively.

What is a camera obscura?

A dark chamber with a pinhole that projects an inverted image. Ibn al-Haytham used it to prove light travels in straight lines.

Kitab al-Manazir

Written 1011-1021 CE, this 7-volume masterwork laid the foundation for modern optics and the scientific method.

Straight-Line Light

Ibn al-Haytham proved that light travels in straight lines — overturning the Greek emission theory that eyes shoot out light.

Legacy

His work was translated to Latin and used by Roger Bacon, Kepler, and others who built modern optics on his foundation.
