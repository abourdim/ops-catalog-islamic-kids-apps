Who was Al-Zahrawi?

Abu al-Qasim al-Zahrawi (936-1013 CE), Albucasis in the West, is the father of modern surgery from Cordoba, Spain.

How many instruments?

Over 200 surgical instruments, many still used in modified form today.

What is Al-Tasrif?

His 30-volume medical encyclopedia with the first illustrated surgical textbook in history.

Al-Tasrif

Written ~1000 CE, 30 volumes covering all medical practice. Volume 30 on surgery was the most influential.

Catgut Sutures

Al-Zahrawi invented absorbable sutures from animal intestines — still the basis of modern sutures.

Surgical Illustrations

First doctor to include detailed instrument drawings in a medical text.
