What is the Canon of Medicine?

Al-Qanun fi al-Tibb by Ibn Sina (~1025 CE) — a 5-book medical encyclopedia used for over 600 years.

What are the five books?

Book I: General Principles, Book II: Simple Drugs, Book III: Diseases by Organ, Book IV: General Diseases, Book V: Compound Medicines.

Why was it important?

It systematized all medical knowledge with clinical observations and a logical framework for diagnosis.
